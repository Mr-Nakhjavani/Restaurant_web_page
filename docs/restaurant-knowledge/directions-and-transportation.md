## By car

- From Sheikh Zayed Road, take Exit 50 towards Al Mankhool / Bur Dubai, follow Khalid Bin Al Waleed Road (Bank Street) towards Al Ghubaiba, then turn right onto Al Seef Street.
- From Deira, cross Al Maktoum Bridge, keep right towards Bur Dubai and follow signs for Al Seef.
- Our entrance faces Al Seef Street at Heritage Quarter Gate 3; a drop-off bay is directly outside the takeaway counter.

## Parking

- Paid public parking is available along Al Seef Street and in the Al Seef multi-storey car park (about 3 minutes' walk). Standard Dubai public-parking tariffs apply and are paid through the official parking app, SMS or ticket machines.
- Valet parking is offered by the Heritage Quarter at Gate 3 from 18:00 to 24:00 on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays (AED 40, paid to the valet operator).
- Two accessible parking bays are located beside Gate 3.
- Takeaway customers may use the 10-minute drop-off bay outside the collection counter.

Parking is not owned or validated by the restaurant; we cannot reserve spaces. Tariffs and hours are set by the parking operator.

## By taxi or ride-hailing

- Ask for “Al Seef, Heritage Quarter, Gate 3, near Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood”.
- A taxi from Dubai International Airport (DXB) usually takes 15–25 minutes depending on traffic; from Downtown Dubai about 15–20 minutes.
- Ride-hailing pick-up and drop-off is at the Gate 3 lay-by on Al Seef Street.

## By metro

- Take the Green Line to Al Fahidi Metro Station. Use the exit towards Al Fahidi Street / Al Seef and walk north-east towards the creek for about 10 minutes, passing the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood on your left.
- From the Red Line, change to the Green Line at BurJuman, or walk 20–25 minutes from BurJuman station.

## By bus

- Several RTA bus routes stop at the Al Ghubaiba Bus Station and on Al Fahidi Street; from either stop the restaurant is a 10–12 minute walk along the creek.

## By abra (water taxi)

- From Deira, take a traditional abra from Deira Old Souq Abra Station across the creek to Bur Dubai Abra Station (a few minutes, paid in cash or with a Nol card), then walk about 12 minutes south-east along the creek promenade.
- Al Seef also has a water-taxi and ferry stop; schedules are operated by the RTA and change seasonally.

## On foot

- From the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood, walk towards the creek and turn left along the Al Seef promenade; we are the coral-stone building with the brass ghaf-tree sign, about 5 minutes away.
- The promenade is step-free; some heritage alleys in Al Fahidi have uneven paving.

## Accessibility

- Step-free route from Gate 3 and from the promenade, accessible restroom on the ground floor.
- Wheelchair-accessible tables are available in the main hall, at the window and on the terrace (via a ramp). The majlis rooms use floor seating and are not suitable for wheelchairs; Majlis Al Sidr can be set with low chairs on request.
