# Bayt Al Ghaf catering menu

Every Bayt Al Ghaf dish available for catering with unit price, servings, daily maximum, extra notice and minimum quantity.

## Shorbat Adas for catering

Shorbat Adas is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 26 each (serves 1). Up to 80 per day. Contains gluten, celery.

## Sambousa Trio for catering

Sambousa Trio is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 34 each (serves 2). Up to 150 per day. Sold by the tray of 6 for catering. Contains gluten, milk.

## Nakhi for catering

Nakhi is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 22 each (serves 1). Up to 80 per day. Contains none of the major allergens.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts for catering

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 38 each (serves 2). Up to 60 per day. Contains gluten, tree nuts, sesame.

## Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) for catering

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 30 each (serves 1). Up to 100 per day. Contains none of the major allergens.

## Harees for catering

Harees is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 48 each (serves 1). Up to 40 per day. Needs at least 48 hours' notice. Bulk harees is cooked in a dedicated overnight batch and needs at least 48 hours' notice. Contains gluten, milk.

## Lamb Thareed for catering

Lamb Thareed is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 62 each (serves 1). Up to 60 per day. Contains gluten, milk.

## Saloonat Dajaj for catering

Saloonat Dajaj is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 52 each (serves 1). Up to 120 per day. Contains none of the major allergens.

## Lamb Machboos for catering

Lamb Machboos is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 72 each (serves 1). Up to 150 per day. Contains milk, sulphites.

## Chicken Machboos for catering

Chicken Machboos is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 58 each (serves 1). Up to 200 per day. Contains milk, sulphites.

## Camel Machboos (Thursday) for catering

Camel Machboos (Thursday) is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 92 each (serves 1). Up to 40 per day. Needs at least 72 hours' notice. Camel meat is ordered specially for catering and needs 72 hours' notice. Contains milk, sulphites.

## Saffron Shish Tawook for catering

Saffron Shish Tawook is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 68 each (serves 1). Up to 80 per day. Contains milk.

## Camel Kofta for catering

Camel Kofta is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 76 each (serves 1). Up to 60 per day. Needs at least 48 hours' notice. Camel mince for catering needs 48 hours' notice. Contains gluten, tree nuts.

## Vegetable Machboos for catering

Vegetable Machboos is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 48 each (serves 1). Up to 100 per day. Contains celery.

## Bamia Saloona for catering

Bamia Saloona is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 44 each (serves 1). Up to 60 per day. Contains none of the major allergens.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona for catering

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 44 each (serves 1). Up to 60 per day. Contains celery.

## Luqaimat for catering

Luqaimat is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 32 each (serves 2). Up to 120 per day. Catering luqaimat are packed with dibs on the side; quantity is counted in portions of 10 pieces. Contains gluten, milk, sesame.

## Khanfaroosh for catering

Khanfaroosh is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 30 each (serves 2). Up to 60 per day. Contains gluten, eggs.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) for catering

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 30 each (serves 3). Up to 30 per day. Catering gahwa is supplied in insulated flasks (1 flask = 1 dallah). Contains none of the major allergens.

## Karak Chai for catering

Karak Chai is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 12 each (serves 1). Up to 100 per day. Catering karak is supplied in flasks of 8 cups; each unit counts as one cup. Contains milk.

## Chilled Laban with Mint for catering

Chilled Laban with Mint is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 14 each (serves 1). Up to 150 per day. Contains milk.

## Limon Na'na for catering

Limon Na'na is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 22 each (serves 1). Up to 100 per day. Contains none of the major allergens.

## Majlis Sharing Platter for catering

Majlis Sharing Platter is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 329 each (serves 4). Up to 15 per day. Each platter counts as 4 portions towards catering capacity. Contains gluten, milk, sesame, sulphites.

## Whole Lamb Ouzi for catering

Whole Lamb Ouzi is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 1,450 each (serves 10). Catering orders only. Minimum 1. Up to 3 per day. Needs at least 48 hours' notice. Each ouzi counts as 12 portions. Maximum 3 per day. Contains milk, tree nuts, sulphites.

## Office Machboos Lunch Box for catering

Office Machboos Lunch Box is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 45 each (serves 1). Catering orders only. Minimum 10. Up to 150 per day. Minimum 10 boxes per order; mix of mains allowed by ordering separate lines. Contains gluten, milk, sesame, sulphites.

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) for catering

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is available for Bayt Al Ghaf catering at AED 125 each (serves 1). Up to 120 per day. Needs at least 24 hours' notice. Iftar catering for groups needs 24 hours' notice. Contains gluten, milk, celery, sesame, sulphites.
