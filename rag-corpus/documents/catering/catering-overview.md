# Bayt Al Ghaf catering and large orders

How Bayt Al Ghaf catering works: when an order becomes a catering order, the quote and confirmation steps, notice periods, capacity, discounts, deposits, delivery, cancellation and when to contact the events team.

## When a Bayt Al Ghaf order is a catering order

Regular orders are limited to 20 kitchen portions. Anything larger, and any catering-only dish (Whole Lamb Ouzi and Office Machboos Lunch Box), is a catering order.

## How a Bayt Al Ghaf catering order works

- 1. The customer asks for a catering quote with the dishes, quantities, date and time, and pickup or delivery area. The quote checks notice, capacity, delivery and price.
- 2. A quote is valid for 48 hours and locks the price.
- 3. The customer places the catering order from the quote. It is received as “awaiting confirmation” with a number starting CAT-.
- 4. The catering team calls to confirm within 4 hours and arranges any deposit. The order is only confirmed after that call.

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering notice periods

- 21 to 60 portions: at least 24 hours' notice
- 61 to 150 portions: at least 48 hours' notice
- 151 to 250 portions: at least 72 hours' notice

Some dishes need more notice than the tier: Harees (48 hours), Camel Machboos (Thursday) (72 hours), Camel Kofta (48 hours), Whole Lamb Ouzi (48 hours) and Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (24 hours). The longest requirement applies.

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering capacity

- Maximum per catering order: 250 portions.
- Maximum across all catering orders on one day: 300 portions.
- Collection or delivery between 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm), up to 90 days ahead.
- Some dishes have their own daily maximum (listed on the catering menu).

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering discounts

- Up to 50 portions: no discount.
- 51 portions or more: 5% off the food.
- 101 portions or more: 8% off the food.
- The discount applies to the food, not to the delivery fee.

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering deposits

Catering orders of AED 2,500 or more (including delivery) need a 50% deposit. The catering team arranges it by bank transfer after confirming the order. No payment is taken online.

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering delivery

Catering can be collected at Al Seef or delivered to zones A to D: Zone A — Old Dubai (AED 7 fee, minimum AED 50), Zone B — Creek & Deira (AED 12 fee, minimum AED 80), Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud (AED 18 fee, minimum AED 120) and Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only) (AED 75 fee, minimum AED 1,000). The delivery fee always applies to catering. Zone D is for catering orders only.

## Cancelling a Bayt Al Ghaf catering order

Catering orders can be cancelled free of charge until 24 hours before collection or delivery. Later cancellations and changes are handled by the catering team.

## When to contact the Bayt Al Ghaf events team

Contact the events team at events@baytalghaf.example for orders above 250 portions, events beyond the daily capacity, dates more than 90 days away, or services such as staff, equipment, live cooking or an on-site buffet. Bayt Al Ghaf catering is food prepared in the restaurant kitchen for collection or delivery.

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering: worked examples

- 40 × Office Machboos Lunch Box at AED 45 = AED 1,800 (no discount) for pickup: total AED 1,800. Notice: at least 24 hours. Deposit: none.
- 60 × Lamb Machboos at AED 72 = AED 4,320, minus 5% (AED 216) = AED 4,104 for pickup: total AED 4,104. Notice: at least 24 hours. Deposit: AED 2,052 (50%).
- 120 × Chicken Machboos at AED 58 = AED 6,960, minus 8% (AED 556.80) = AED 6,403.20, plus AED 75 delivery to Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only): total AED 6,478.20. Notice: at least 48 hours. Deposit: AED 3,239.10 (50%).
- 100 × Lamb Machboos at AED 72 = AED 7,200, minus 5% (AED 360) = AED 6,840 for pickup: total AED 6,840. Notice: at least 48 hours. Deposit: AED 3,420 (50%).
- 30 × Luqaimat at AED 32 = AED 960 (no discount) for pickup: total AED 960. Notice: at least 24 hours. Deposit: none.

## Impossible catering requests at Bayt Al Ghaf

- 200 machboos in 20 minutes is impossible: 200 portions need 72 hours' notice, and Lamb Machboos is limited per day. The earliest feasible date can be offered instead.
- More than 250 portions in one order is above the maximum; the events team can advise.
- Dishes that are cooked to order cannot be ordered in catering quantities: Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Muhammar with Fried Safi, Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Friday Family Machboos Platter and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).
