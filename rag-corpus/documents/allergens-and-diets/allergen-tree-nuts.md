# Tree nuts at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain tree nuts, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding tree nuts.

## Tree nuts at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Tree nuts are used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 4 dishes contain tree nuts in the standard recipe, 2 dishes may contain traces, and 42 dishes have no tree nuts in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain tree nuts

- Walnuts contains tree nuts. Walnut halves, toasted.
- Pistachios contains tree nuts. Shelled pistachios.
- Pine nuts contains tree nuts. Toasted pine nuts. Botanically seeds, but we label them as tree nuts.
- Almonds contains tree nuts. Blanched almonds, fried until golden.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain tree nuts

- Al Ghaf House Red (sauce) contains tree nuts from walnuts.
- Pistachio Cardamom Crumb (topping) contains tree nuts from pistachios.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain tree nuts

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38): contains tree nuts from pine nuts
- Camel Kofta (AED 76): contains tree nuts from the Al Ghaf House Red
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36): contains tree nuts from the Pistachio Cardamom Crumb
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450): contains tree nuts from almonds and pine nuts

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of tree nuts

- Rubyan Mashwi: Made in the same blender and on the same station as Al Ghaf House Red, which contains walnuts and wheat; we cannot rule out traces of tree nuts or gluten.
- Date & Cardamom Shake: Blended in the drinks blender, which is also used for drinks with pistachio crumb.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without tree nuts

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: choose “No pine nuts”
- Camel Kofta: choose “Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)”
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: choose “No pistachio crumb”
- Whole Lamb Ouzi: choose “No nut garnish”

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes with no tree nuts in the recipe

Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Shorbat Adas, Sambousa Trio, Nakhi, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Mehyawa Regag Bites, Harees, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Saffron Shish Tawook, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Luqaimat, Khanfaroosh, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Karak Chai, Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na, Date & Cardamom Shake, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). These dishes have no tree nuts in their recipe; traces from the shared kitchen are still possible.

## Advice for guests avoiding tree nuts at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Al Ghaf House Red contains walnuts. Al Ghaf House Green has no nut ingredients but is made on the same blender as House Red, so it may contain traces of tree nuts.
- The hummus is topped with pine nuts, which are counted as tree nuts. The pistachio crumb contains pistachios.
- Nutmeg is a seed spice, not a tree nut. Bayt Al Ghaf does not use coconut or peanuts in any recipe.
- The kitchen is not nut-free.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from tree nuts or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe tree nuts allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
