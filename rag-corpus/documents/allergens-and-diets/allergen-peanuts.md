# Peanuts at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain peanuts, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding peanuts.

## Peanuts at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Peanuts are not used as an ingredient anywhere in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. No dish contains peanuts as an ingredient, but supplier products may carry traces.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain peanuts

No Bayt Al Ghaf ingredient contains peanuts.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain peanuts

No Bayt Al Ghaf sauce, marinade or spice blend contains peanuts.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain peanuts

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish contains peanuts as an ingredient.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of peanuts

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish carries a specific trace warning for peanuts, although the general kitchen statement still applies.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without peanuts

None of the Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain peanuts can be made without it through a menu option.

## Advice for guests avoiding peanuts at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Bayt Al Ghaf does not use peanuts in any recipe, but supplier products may carry traces.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from peanuts or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe peanuts allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
