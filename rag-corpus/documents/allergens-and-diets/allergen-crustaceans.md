# Crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding crustaceans (prawns and shrimp).

## Crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) are used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 1 dish contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) in the standard recipe, 5 dishes may contain traces, and 45 dishes have no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp)

- Gulf prawns contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp). Jumbo Gulf prawns, deveined.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp)

No Bayt Al Ghaf sauce, marinade or spice blend contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp).

## Advice for guests avoiding crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Prawns (in Rubyan Mashwi) are cooked on the shared charcoal grill, so other grilled dishes may carry traces of crustaceans.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
