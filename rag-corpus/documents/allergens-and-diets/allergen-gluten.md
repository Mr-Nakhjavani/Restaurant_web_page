# Gluten (wheat) at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain gluten (wheat), which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding gluten (wheat).

## Gluten (wheat) at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Gluten (wheat) is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 23 dishes contain gluten (wheat) in the standard recipe, 3 dishes may contain traces, and 23 dishes have no gluten (wheat) in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain gluten (wheat)

- Wheat flour contains gluten (wheat). White wheat flour used for regag, chebab, luqaimat, sambousa pastry and dough sheets.
- Wholewheat flour contains gluten (wheat). Stone-ground wholewheat flour.
- Hulled wheat (harees wheat) contains gluten (wheat). Whole hulled wheat grains soaked overnight and slow-cooked for harees.
- Wheat vermicelli contains gluten (wheat). Fine wheat vermicelli, toasted in ghee for balaleet.
- Regag bread (house-made) contains gluten (wheat). Paper-thin crisp Emirati bread baked to order on a hot iron plate from wheat flour, water and salt.
- Sambousa pastry sheets contains gluten (wheat). Thin wheat-flour pastry sheets (wheat flour, water, sunflower oil, salt).
- Breadcrumbs contains gluten (wheat). Dried white wheat breadcrumbs (wheat flour, yeast, salt).
- Sourdough croutons contains gluten (wheat). Cubes of wheat sourdough toasted with olive oil and salt.
- Brioche bun contains gluten (wheat). Bakery brioche bun made with wheat flour, eggs, butter and milk, topped with sesame seeds.
- Margooga dough sheets contains gluten (wheat). Hand-stretched sheets of wheat-flour dough (wheat flour, water, salt) cooked in the stew.
- Soy sauce contains gluten (wheat). Naturally brewed soy sauce (soybeans, wheat, salt, water).

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain gluten (wheat)

- Al Ghaf House Red (sauce) contains gluten (wheat) from breadcrumbs.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (glaze) contains gluten (wheat) from soy sauce.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of gluten (wheat)

- Hammour Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame). Fish may contain small bones.
- Rubyan Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame). Made in the same blender and on the same station as Al Ghaf House Red, which contains walnuts and wheat; we cannot rule out traces of tree nuts or gluten.
- Saffron Shish Tawook: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without gluten (wheat)

- Bayd w Tamat: choose “No bread”
- Shorbat Adas: choose “No croutons”
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: choose “No regag”
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): choose “No glaze”
- Camel Kofta: choose “Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)” and “No bread”

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes with no gluten (wheat) in the recipe

Nakhi, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Saffron Shish Tawook, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Karak Chai, Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na, Date & Cardamom Shake and Whole Lamb Ouzi. These dishes have no gluten (wheat) in their recipe; traces from the shared kitchen are still possible.

## Advice for guests avoiding gluten (wheat) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Regag, chebab, sambousa pastry, harees wheat, vermicelli (balaleet), margooga dough, croutons, breadcrumbs and the brioche bun all contain wheat.
- Sauce No. 7 contains soy sauce, which is brewed with wheat, so dishes glazed with Sauce No. 7 contain gluten.
- No dish is certified gluten-free. Wheat flour is handled throughout the kitchen and the general fryer is shared.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from gluten (wheat) or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe gluten (wheat) allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
