# Bayt Al Ghaf allergen policy

How Bayt Al Ghaf handles allergies: the kitchen allergen statement, what staff will and will not say, severe allergies, shared equipment and a dish-by-dish allergen list.

## Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen allergen statement

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## How Bayt Al Ghaf answers allergy questions

- Staff say what a dish contains as ingredients and what it may contain as traces, using the recipe of that exact dish and any options chosen.
- Bayt Al Ghaf never says a dish is “safe”, “allergen-free” or “100% free” of an allergen, and never guarantees there is no cross-contamination, because there is one shared kitchen.
- Guests with a severe allergy (anaphylaxis, an EpiPen, coeliac disease) are asked to speak to the duty manager before ordering. Allergies written in order notes are flagged and the duty manager calls the guest back.
- Leaving an ingredient out (for example “no sesame seeds”) removes it from the recipe, not from the kitchen, so a trace warning still applies.
- Bayt Al Ghaf cannot cook any dish in a separate allergen-free kitchen, and cannot make the whole menu free from any allergen.
- All meat and poultry are halal; no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

## Shared equipment in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen

- General fryer: sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Seafood fryer: used only for safi (rabbitfish).
- Charcoal grill: lamb, camel, chicken, hammour and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Breakfast plancha and bread station: regag, eggs, cheese and chebab; the mehyawa regag is also made here.
- Sauce station blender: shared by Al Ghaf House Red (walnuts) and Al Ghaf House Green.
