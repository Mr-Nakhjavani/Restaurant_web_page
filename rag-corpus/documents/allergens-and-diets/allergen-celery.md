# Celery at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain celery, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding celery.

## Celery at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Celery is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 4 dishes contain celery in the standard recipe, 26 dishes may contain traces, and 42 dishes have no celery in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain celery

- Celery contains celery. Fresh celery stalks.
- Vegetable stock (house-made) contains celery. Stock simmered from onion, carrot, celery, tomato, loomi and bay leaf. Contains celery.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain celery

No Bayt Al Ghaf sauce, marinade or spice blend contains celery.

## Advice for guests avoiding celery at Bayt Al Ghaf

- The house vegetable stock contains celery; the dishes made with it are listed above.
- Bezar spice blend may contain traces of celery and mustard from the spice supplier, so most dishes seasoned with bezar carry a celery trace warning.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from celery or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe celery allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
