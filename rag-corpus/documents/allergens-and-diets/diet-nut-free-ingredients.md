# Dishes with no nut ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf

Dishes with no nut ingredients on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: which dishes qualify as standard, which qualify with a change, and what to know.

## Dishes with no nut ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this means

At Bayt Al Ghaf, dishes listed here have no tree nuts or peanuts in their recipe. Bayt Al Ghaf is not a nut-free kitchen: walnuts, pine nuts, pistachios and almonds are used.
