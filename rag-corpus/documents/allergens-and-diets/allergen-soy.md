# Soy at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain soy, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding soy.

## Soy at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Soy is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 2 dishes contain soy in the standard recipe, 4 dishes may contain traces, and 44 dishes have no soy in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain soy

- Soy sauce contains soy. Naturally brewed soy sauce (soybeans, wheat, salt, water).

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain soy

- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (glaze) contains soy from soy sauce.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain soy

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118): contains soy from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72): contains soy from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of soy

- Hammour Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame). Fish may contain small bones.
- Rubyan Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Saffron Shish Tawook: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Camel Kofta: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without soy

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): choose “No glaze”
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli: choose “No Sauce No. 7”

These dishes have no soy in their recipe; traces from the shared kitchen are still possible.

## Advice for guests avoiding soy at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Sauce No. 7 contains soy sauce. The brioche bun supplier states the bun may contain soy.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from soy or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe soy allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
