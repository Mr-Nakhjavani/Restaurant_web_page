# Eggs at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain eggs, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding eggs.

## Eggs at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Eggs are used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 10 dishes contain eggs in the standard recipe, 6 dishes may contain traces, and 36 dishes have no eggs in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain eggs

- Brioche bun contains eggs. Bakery brioche bun made with wheat flour, eggs, butter and milk, topped with sesame seeds.
- Eggs contains eggs. Free-range hen's eggs.
- Egg yolk contains eggs. Free-range egg yolks (pasteurised for aioli and ice cream).

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain eggs

- Loomi Aioli (sauce) contains eggs from egg yolk.

## Advice for guests avoiding eggs at Bayt Al Ghaf

- The loomi aioli contains egg. Toum (garlic sauce) is egg-free.
- Most Emirati breakfast dishes contain egg; the exact dishes are listed above.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from eggs or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe eggs allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
