# Fish at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain fish, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding fish.

## Fish at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Fish is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 4 dishes contain fish in the standard recipe, 5 dishes may contain traces, and 42 dishes have no fish in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain fish

- Hammour (orange-spotted grouper) fillet contains fish. Skin-on hammour fillet from licensed suppliers; sourced only outside the UAE seasonal fishing ban and replaced by sha'ari (emperor fish) when unavailable.
- Kingfish (kanaad) contains fish. Kingfish (narrow-barred Spanish mackerel) steaks, bone-in.
- Safi (rabbitfish) contains fish. Whole Gulf rabbitfish, cleaned and scored, about 300 g each.
- Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) contains fish. Small Gulf sardines and anchovies salted and fermented for mehyawa.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain fish

- Mehyawa (sauce) contains fish from small salted fish (sardine/anchovy).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain fish

- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26): contains fish from the Mehyawa
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64): contains fish from kingfish (kanaad)
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69): contains fish from safi (rabbitfish)
- Hammour Mashwi (AED 98): contains fish from hammour (orange-spotted grouper) fillet

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Saffron Shish Tawook: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Camel Kofta: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame). Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without fish

None of the Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain fish can be made without it through a menu option.

## Advice for guests avoiding fish at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Mehyawa is a fermented fish sauce. The Mehyawa Regag contains fish even though it is a bread dish.
- Hammour and safi (rabbitfish) are fish. Safi is fried in a separate seafood fryer.
- Fish is cooked on the shared charcoal grill, so grilled meat dishes may carry traces of fish.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from fish or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe fish allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
