# Milk and dairy at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain milk (dairy), which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding milk (dairy).

## Milk and dairy at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Milk (dairy) is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 29 dishes contain milk (dairy) in the standard recipe, 1 dish may contain traces, and 17 dishes have no milk (dairy) in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain milk (dairy)

- Brioche bun contains milk (dairy). Bakery brioche bun made with wheat flour, eggs, butter and milk, topped with sesame seeds.
- Ghee (clarified butter) contains milk (dairy). Cow's-milk ghee; a dairy product.
- Butter contains milk (dairy). Unsalted cow's-milk butter.
- Full-cream milk contains milk (dairy). Fresh full-cream cow's milk.
- Evaporated milk contains milk (dairy). Unsweetened evaporated cow's milk, the traditional base for karak.
- Camel milk contains milk (dairy). Pasteurised UAE camel milk. Camel milk is a milk (dairy) allergen.
- Milk powder contains milk (dairy). Full-cream cow's milk powder.
- Natural yogurt contains milk (dairy). Plain full-fat cow's-milk yogurt.
- Laban (cultured buttermilk) contains milk (dairy). Cultured cow's-milk buttermilk.
- Fresh cream contains milk (dairy). Cow's-milk cream (35% fat).
- Cream cheese contains milk (dairy). Soft cow's-milk cream cheese.
- Processed cheese contains milk (dairy). Processed cow's-milk cheese slices, the classic regag filling.
- White brined cheese (akkawi) contains milk (dairy). Mild white brined cow's-milk cheese, soaked to reduce salt.

These dishes have no milk (dairy) in their recipe; traces from the shared kitchen are still possible.

## Advice for guests avoiding milk (dairy) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Ghee (clarified butter) is a dairy product. It is used in machboos, harees, balaleet, the hashu topping and many sweets.
- Camel milk (in the camel milk ice cream and as an option in karak and the date shake) is still milk and contains lactose.
- Laban and the saffron yoghurt sauce are dairy.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from milk (dairy) or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe milk (dairy) allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
