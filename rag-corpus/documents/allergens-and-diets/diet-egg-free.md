# Egg-free dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf

Egg-free dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: which dishes qualify as standard, which qualify with a change, and what to know.

## Egg-free dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this means

At Bayt Al Ghaf, dishes listed here have no egg in their recipe. Eggs are cooked on the breakfast plancha, and khanfaroosh (which contains egg) is fried in the general fryer.

## Egg-free dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: the full list

36 dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu qualify as standard:

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) — Velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lime and sourdough croutons.
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) — Six crisp sambousa: spiced lamb, akkawi cheese and potato-vegetable, with dakkus.
- Nakhi (AED 22) — Warm boiled chickpeas with cumin, chilli and lime — an old Emirati snack.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) — Silky tahini hummus topped with bezar-spiced lamb and toasted pine nuts.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) — Tomato, cucumber, jarjeer, radish and pomegranate with loomi-sumac dressing.
- Harees (AED 48) — Wheat and lamb slow-cooked overnight and beaten smooth, finished with ghee.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) — Lamb and vegetable saloona ladled over layers of regag bread.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) — Chicken and vegetable stew with loomi and bezar, served with white rice.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) — Monday special: kingfish steaks in a tamarind-tomato saloona with rice.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) — Tuesday special: lamb and vegetable stew cooked with hand-stretched dough sheets.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (AED 54) — Wednesday special: chicken and rice beaten to a creamy, spiced porridge.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) — Our signature: bone-in lamb shoulder and spiced basmati with loomi and hashu.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) — Half a chicken and spiced basmati cooked in loomi stock, topped with hashu.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) — Sweet saffron and date-syrup rice with a whole fried rabbitfish.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) — Thursday special: young camel slow-braised for three hours, with spiced rice and hashu.
- Hammour Mashwi (AED 98) — Charcoal-grilled hammour fillet in bezar and lime, with rice and dakkus.
- Rubyan Mashwi (AED 105) — Six jumbo Gulf prawns grilled in garlic-lime butter, with rice and House Green.
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118) — Five laban-marinated lamb chops glazed with Sauce No. 7, with grilled tomato and toum.
- Saffron Shish Tawook (AED 68) — Chicken skewers in saffron-yogurt marinade with grilled peppers and toum.
- Camel Kofta (AED 76) — Charcoal-grilled camel kofta with Al Ghaf House Red and regag.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) — Vegan machboos with chickpeas, eggplant, zucchini, carrot and potato.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) — Tender okra in a garlicky tomato saloona with coriander and loomi; with rice.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) — Sweet pumpkin and chickpeas in a turmeric-ginger saloona; with rice.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) — Crisp saffron-cardamom dumplings, warm dibs and toasted sesame.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) — Winter only: warm pumpkin asida with ghee, saffron and cardamom.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) — A dallah of lightly roasted Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron, served with dates.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) — Strong black tea simmered with evaporated milk, cardamom and saffron.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) — Clear black tea with lime and mint.
- Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14) — Cold, lightly salted cultured buttermilk with fresh and dried mint.
- Limon Na'na (AED 22) — Fresh lemon juice blended with mint and ice.
- Date & Cardamom Shake (AED 28) — Khalas dates blended with cold milk, date syrup and cardamom.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) — Serves 4: lamb machboos, chicken saloona, sambousa, house salad and luqaimat.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) — Fridays only. Serves 5: lamb shoulder and whole chicken machboos on one great tray.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) — Catering only. Serves 10–12: whole slow-roasted lamb on spiced rice with nuts.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) — Catering only, minimum 10: machboos, house salad, three luqaimat and water.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) — Ramadan only: dates & laban, lentil shorba, sambousa, a main of your choice, luqaimat and tea.

## Egg-free dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf with a menu option

These dishes qualify when ordered with the option shown: Balaleet with “No omelette” and Regag with Egg & Cheese with “No egg”.
