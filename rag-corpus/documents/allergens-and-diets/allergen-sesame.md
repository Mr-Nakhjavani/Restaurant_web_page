# Sesame at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain sesame, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding sesame.

## Sesame at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Sesame is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 9 dishes contain sesame in the standard recipe, 6 dishes may contain traces, and 37 dishes have no sesame in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain sesame

- Brioche bun contains sesame. Bakery brioche bun made with wheat flour, eggs, butter and milk, topped with sesame seeds.
- Toasted sesame oil contains sesame. Toasted sesame oil.
- Sesame seeds contains sesame. Toasted white sesame seeds.
- Tahini contains sesame. Ground sesame paste.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain sesame

- Tahini Lemon Sauce (sauce) contains sesame from tahini.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (glaze) contains sesame from toasted sesame oil.

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118): contains sesame from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72): contains sesame from brioche bun and the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Luqaimat (AED 32): contains sesame from sesame seeds
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329): contains sesame from sesame seeds
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349): contains sesame from sesame seeds

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125): contains sesame from sesame seeds
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355): contains sesame from sesame seeds

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of sesame

- Sambousa Trio: Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Hammour Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame). Fish may contain small bones.
- Rubyan Mashwi: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Saffron Shish Tawook: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Camel Kofta: Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Khanfaroosh: Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without sesame

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): choose “No glaze”
- Luqaimat: choose “No sesame seeds”

## Advice for guests avoiding sesame at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Tahini (in hummus and the tahini-lemon sauce) is ground sesame. Sauce No. 7 contains sesame oil. Luqaimat are topped with sesame seeds unless ordered without. The brioche bun is topped with sesame seeds.
- Sesame is fried in the general fryer (luqaimat), so fried dishes may carry traces of sesame.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from sesame or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe sesame allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
