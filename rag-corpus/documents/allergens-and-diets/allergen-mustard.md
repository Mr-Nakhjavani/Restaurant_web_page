# Mustard at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain mustard, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding mustard.

## Mustard at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Mustard is used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 2 dishes contain mustard in the standard recipe, 28 dishes may contain traces, and 44 dishes have no mustard in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

- Dijon mustard contains mustard. Prepared mustard (mustard seed, vinegar, salt).

## Advice for guests avoiding mustard at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Mehyawa contains mustard seed. The loomi aioli contains mustard.
- Bezar may contain traces of mustard from the spice supplier.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from mustard or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe mustard allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
