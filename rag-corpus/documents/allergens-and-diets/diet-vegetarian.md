# Vegetarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf

Vegetarian dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: which dishes qualify as standard, which qualify with a change, and what to know.

## Vegetarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this means

At Bayt Al Ghaf, dishes listed here contain no meat, poultry, fish or seafood (they may contain dairy, eggs or honey). Vegetarian dishes share the kitchen with meat and fish. Fried vegetarian dishes share the general fryer with the sambousa, which includes a lamb filling.

## Vegetarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: the full list

21 dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu qualify as standard:

- Balaleet (AED 34) — Sweet saffron and cardamom vermicelli crowned with a thin omelette.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) — Three saffron-cardamom Emirati pancakes with warm dibs and cream cheese.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) — Paper-thin crisp regag folded over egg and melted cheese.
- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) — Soft eggs scrambled with tomato, onion, green chilli and bezar, with regag.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) — Saturdays & Sundays only: a generous traditional breakfast spread for two.
- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) — Velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lime and sourdough croutons.
- Nakhi (AED 22) — Warm boiled chickpeas with cumin, chilli and lime — an old Emirati snack.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) — Tomato, cucumber, jarjeer, radish and pomegranate with loomi-sumac dressing.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) — Vegan machboos with chickpeas, eggplant, zucchini, carrot and potato.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) — Tender okra in a garlicky tomato saloona with coriander and loomi; with rice.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) — Sweet pumpkin and chickpeas in a turmeric-ginger saloona; with rice.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) — Crisp saffron-cardamom dumplings, warm dibs and toasted sesame.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) — Little saffron and rosewater cakes, crisp-edged and fragrant.
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36) — Three scoops of house-churned camel-milk and date ice cream with pistachio crumb.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) — Winter only: warm pumpkin asida with ghee, saffron and cardamom.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) — A dallah of lightly roasted Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron, served with dates.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) — Strong black tea simmered with evaporated milk, cardamom and saffron.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) — Clear black tea with lime and mint.
- Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14) — Cold, lightly salted cultured buttermilk with fresh and dried mint.
- Limon Na'na (AED 22) — Fresh lemon juice blended with mint and ice.
- Date & Cardamom Shake (AED 28) — Khalas dates blended with cold milk, date syrup and cardamom.

## Vegetarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf with a menu option

These dishes qualify when ordered with the option shown: Sambousa Trio with “Vegetarian (3 cheese, 3 vegetable)”, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts with “No lamb (vegetarian hummus)” and Office Machboos Lunch Box with “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)”.
