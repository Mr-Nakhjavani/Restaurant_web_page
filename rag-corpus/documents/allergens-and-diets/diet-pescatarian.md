# Pescatarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf

Pescatarian dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: which dishes qualify as standard, which qualify with a change, and what to know.

## Pescatarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this means

At Bayt Al Ghaf, dishes listed here contain fish or seafood but no meat or poultry. Fish and prawns are grilled on the shared charcoal grill.

## Pescatarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf: the full list

5 dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu qualify as standard:

- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26) — Crisp regag spread with mehyawa, egg and cheese, cut into bites.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) — Monday special: kingfish steaks in a tamarind-tomato saloona with rice.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) — Sweet saffron and date-syrup rice with a whole fried rabbitfish.
- Hammour Mashwi (AED 98) — Charcoal-grilled hammour fillet in bezar and lime, with rice and dakkus.
- Rubyan Mashwi (AED 105) — Six jumbo Gulf prawns grilled in garlic-lime butter, with rice and House Green.

## Pescatarian dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf with a menu option

These dishes qualify when ordered with the option shown: Regag with Egg & Cheese with “Add mehyawa (fermented fish sauce)”.
