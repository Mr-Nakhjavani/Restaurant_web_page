# Sulphites at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain sulphites, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding sulphites.

## Sulphites at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Sulphites are used as an ingredient in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. 9 dishes contain sulphites in the standard recipe, 0 dishes may contain traces, and 37 dishes have no sulphites in their ingredients.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain sulphites

- Golden raisins contains sulphites. Golden raisins preserved with sulphur dioxide (contain sulphites).

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain sulphites

- Hashu Topping (topping) contains sulphites from golden raisins.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of sulphites

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish carries a specific trace warning for sulphites, although the general kitchen statement still applies.

## Advice for guests avoiding sulphites at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Golden raisins contain sulphites. They are in the hashu topping on machboos, which can be ordered without raisins.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from sulphites or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe sulphites allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
