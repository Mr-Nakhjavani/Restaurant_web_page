# Lupin at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain lupin, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding lupin.

## Lupin at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Lupin is not used as an ingredient anywhere in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. No dish contains lupin as an ingredient, but supplier products may carry traces.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain lupin

No Bayt Al Ghaf ingredient contains lupin.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain lupin

No Bayt Al Ghaf sauce, marinade or spice blend contains lupin.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain lupin

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of lupin

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish carries a specific trace warning for lupin, although the general kitchen statement still applies.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without lupin

None of the Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain lupin can be made without it through a menu option.

## Advice for guests avoiding lupin at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Bayt Al Ghaf does not use lupin in any recipe, but supplier products may carry traces.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from lupin or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe lupin allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
