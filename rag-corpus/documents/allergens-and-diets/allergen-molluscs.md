# Molluscs at Bayt Al Ghaf

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes, sauces and ingredients contain molluscs, which may contain traces, which can be made without it, and advice for guests avoiding molluscs.

## Molluscs at Bayt Al Ghaf: summary

Molluscs are not used as an ingredient anywhere in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. No dish contains molluscs as an ingredient, but supplier products may carry traces.

Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Ingredients at Bayt Al Ghaf that contain molluscs

No Bayt Al Ghaf ingredient contains molluscs.

## Bayt Al Ghaf sauces and preparations that contain molluscs

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that may contain traces of molluscs

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish carries a specific trace warning for molluscs, although the general kitchen statement still applies.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made without molluscs

None of the Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that contain molluscs can be made without it through a menu option.

## Advice for guests avoiding molluscs at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Bayt Al Ghaf does not use molluscs (such as squid, mussels or oysters) in any recipe.

Bayt Al Ghaf never describes a dish as completely free from molluscs or any other allergen, because all food is prepared in one shared kitchen. Guests with a severe molluscs allergy should speak to the duty manager before ordering, and mention the allergy in the order notes so the team can call back.
