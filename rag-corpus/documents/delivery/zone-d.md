# Bayt Al Ghaf delivery: Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only)

Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only): areas covered, delivery fee, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, drive time and what can be delivered.

## Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only) in brief

Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only) is a Bayt Al Ghaf delivery zone. Catering and bulk orders only; regular takeaway delivery is not available in this zone.

- Areas: Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Lakes Towers, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Al Barsha, Al Quoz, Umm Suqeim, Jumeirah 3, Dubai Hills, Mirdif, Al Qusais, Al Nahda, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai Internet City and Dubai Media City
- Delivery fee: AED 75 (75 dirhams)
- Minimum order: AED 1,000 (1000 dirhams)
- Free delivery: not offered in this zone
- Typical drive time: 50 minutes
- Regular takeaway delivery: no — catering orders only
- Delivery hours: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) (catering 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm))

## Delivery examples for Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only)

Regular takeaway orders are not delivered to Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only). Catering orders are delivered there for AED 75, with a minimum catering order of AED 1,000.

- “jlt” means Jumeirah Lakes Towers
- “the palm” means Palm Jumeirah
- “barsha” means Al Barsha
