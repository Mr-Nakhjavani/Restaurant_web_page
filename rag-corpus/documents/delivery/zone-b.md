# Bayt Al Ghaf delivery: Zone B — Creek & Deira

Zone B — Creek & Deira: areas covered, delivery fee, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, drive time and what can be delivered.

## Zone B — Creek & Deira in brief

Zone B — Creek & Deira is a Bayt Al Ghaf delivery zone. Neighbourhoods roughly 3–7 km away, including Deira across the creek.

- Areas: Al Karama, Oud Metha, Umm Hurair 2, Al Jaddaf, Za'abeel, Al Satwa, Al Bada'a, Deira, Al Ras, Al Rigga, Port Saeed, Al Muraqqabat and Al Sabkha
- Delivery fee: AED 12 (12 dirhams)
- Minimum order: AED 80 (80 dirhams)
- Free delivery: on orders of AED 350 or more
- Typical drive time: 25 minutes
- Regular takeaway delivery: yes
- Delivery hours: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) (catering 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm))

## Delivery examples for Zone B — Creek & Deira

- An order of AED 70 to Al Karama is below the AED 80 minimum and cannot be delivered until more is added.
- An order of AED 80 to Al Karama pays the AED 12 delivery fee, for a total of AED 92.
- An order of AED 350 or more to Al Karama is delivered free.
- A dish that needs 25 minutes to prepare can arrive in Al Karama at the earliest 50 minutes after ordering.
