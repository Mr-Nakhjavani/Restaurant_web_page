# Bayt Al Ghaf delivery: Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud

Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud: areas covered, delivery fee, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, drive time and what can be delivered.

## Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud in brief

Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud is a Bayt Al Ghaf delivery zone. Neighbourhoods roughly 7–12 km away.

- Areas: Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, DIFC, Trade Centre, Jumeirah 1, Al Wasl, Al Garhoud, Dubai Festival City, Ras Al Khor and Dubai Healthcare City
- Delivery fee: AED 18 (18 dirhams)
- Minimum order: AED 120 (120 dirhams)
- Free delivery: not offered in this zone
- Typical drive time: 35 minutes
- Regular takeaway delivery: yes
- Delivery hours: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) (catering 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm))

## Delivery examples for Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud

- An order of AED 110 to Downtown Dubai is below the AED 120 minimum and cannot be delivered until more is added.
- An order of AED 120 to Downtown Dubai pays the AED 18 delivery fee, for a total of AED 138.
- There is no free delivery to Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud; the AED 18 fee always applies.
- A dish that needs 25 minutes to prepare can arrive in Downtown Dubai at the earliest 60 minutes after ordering.
