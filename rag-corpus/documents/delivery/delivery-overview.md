# Bayt Al Ghaf delivery

Bayt Al Ghaf delivery hours, zones, fees and minimums, rules for addresses and phone numbers, payment, failed deliveries and dishes that are not delivered.

## Bayt Al Ghaf delivery in brief

Bayt Al Ghaf delivers with its own drivers in Dubai, with arrival between 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) every day. Regular orders are delivered to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud; Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only) is for catering orders only. Bayt Al Ghaf does not deliver outside Dubai — not to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman or the other emirates — and is not on third-party delivery apps.

## Bayt Al Ghaf delivery fees and minimum orders

- Zone A — Old Dubai: fee AED 7, minimum order AED 50, free delivery above AED 250, typical drive 15 minutes
- Zone B — Creek & Deira: fee AED 12, minimum order AED 80, free delivery above AED 350, typical drive 25 minutes
- Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud: fee AED 18, minimum order AED 120, no free-delivery threshold, typical drive 35 minutes
- Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only): fee AED 75, minimum order AED 1,000, no free-delivery threshold, typical drive 50 minutes — catering orders only

## Bayt Al Ghaf delivery rules

- A UAE mobile number is required so the driver can call.
- The address needs the area (which must be in a delivery zone), the building or villa and the street, plus the apartment or floor if there is one. A landmark, Makani number and instructions are welcome.
- The delivery time is when the food arrives; arrival is estimated within a 15-minute window.
- The earliest delivery is the preparation lead time plus the drive time for the zone. No delivery can be faster than that.
- Payment is cash or card on delivery. Drivers carry change up to AED 200.
- If the driver cannot reach the customer at the address within 10 minutes, the delivery is marked as failed and the team calls.
- Breakfast is not delivered.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that are not delivered

Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Friday Family Machboos Platter and Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). These dishes do not travel well and are available for dine-in or pickup only.

## Areas Bayt Al Ghaf does not deliver to

Bayt Al Ghaf delivers only to the areas listed in its delivery zones. Areas not listed — and anywhere outside Dubai — are not served. Customers outside the zones can order for pickup at Al Seef.
