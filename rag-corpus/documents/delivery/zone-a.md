# Bayt Al Ghaf delivery: Zone A — Old Dubai

Zone A — Old Dubai: areas covered, delivery fee, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, drive time and what can be delivered.

## Zone A — Old Dubai in brief

Zone A — Old Dubai is a Bayt Al Ghaf delivery zone. Neighbourhoods within about 3 km of Al Seef on the Bur Dubai side.

- Areas: Al Seef, Al Fahidi, Bur Dubai, Al Souq Al Kabeer, Al Hamriya, Mankhool, Al Raffa, Umm Hurair 1 and Al Ghubaiba
- Delivery fee: AED 7 (7 dirhams)
- Minimum order: AED 50 (50 dirhams)
- Free delivery: on orders of AED 250 or more
- Typical drive time: 15 minutes
- Regular takeaway delivery: yes
- Delivery hours: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) (catering 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm))

## Delivery examples for Zone A — Old Dubai

- An order of AED 40 to Al Seef is below the AED 50 minimum and cannot be delivered until more is added.
- An order of AED 50 to Al Seef pays the AED 7 delivery fee, for a total of AED 57.
- An order of AED 250 or more to Al Seef is delivered free.
- A dish that needs 25 minutes to prepare can arrive in Al Seef at the earliest 40 minutes after ordering.
