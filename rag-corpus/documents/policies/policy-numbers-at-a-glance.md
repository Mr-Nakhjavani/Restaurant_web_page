# Bayt Al Ghaf rules and numbers at a glance

Every limit, time and fee in Bayt Al Ghaf's rules in one place: reservations, orders, delivery, catering, house rules and customer service.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation numbers

- Party size: 1 to 20 guests
- Confirmed instantly: up to 12 guests; 13–20 guests are a request confirmed by phone
- Notice: at least 30 minutes; up to 60 days ahead
- Last reservation: 90 minutes before closing
- Table time: 1–2 guests 90 minutes; 3–6 guests 120 minutes; 7–12 guests 150 minutes; 13–20 guests 180 minutes
- Reset between bookings: 15 minutes
- Majlis: minimum 6 guests, 24 hours' notice, 180 minutes, minimum spend AED 150 per person
- Terrace season: 15 October to 30 April
- Changes: until 2 hours before; cancelling less than 2 hours before is a late cancellation
- Late arrival: table held 15 minutes
- High chairs: 6 in total, up to 4 per booking

## Bayt Al Ghaf ordering numbers

- Slots: every 15 minutes, up to 7 days ahead
- Lead time: longest preparation time + 10 minutes, minimum 20 minutes
- Kitchen capacity: 30 portions and 8 orders per slot
- Order size: up to 10 of one dish and 20 kitchen portions per regular order
- Confirmed by phone: orders above AED 1,500
- Changes: until 30 minutes before or until preparation starts
- Pickup orders held: 60 minutes (hot food kept warm about 30 minutes)
- Late order: free cancellation if more than 20 minutes late

## Bayt Al Ghaf delivery numbers

- Delivery arrivals: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Zone A — Old Dubai: fee AED 7, minimum AED 50, free from AED 250, about 15 minutes
- Zone B — Creek & Deira: fee AED 12, minimum AED 80, free from AED 350, about 25 minutes
- Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud: fee AED 18, minimum AED 120, about 35 minutes
- Zone D — Greater Dubai (catering only): fee AED 75, minimum AED 1,000, about 50 minutes, catering only
- Driver change: up to AED 200
- Driver waits: 10 minutes at the address

## Bayt Al Ghaf catering numbers

- Catering starts above 20 portions; maximum 250 per order and 300 per day
- Notice: 24 hours up to 60 portions; 48 hours up to 150 portions; 72 hours up to 250 portions
- Discounts: 5% from 51 portions; 8% from 101 portions
- Deposit: 50% for orders of AED 2,500 or more
- Quotes valid 48 hours; free cancellation until 24 hours before; team confirms within 4 hours
- Collection or delivery 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm), up to 90 days ahead

## Bayt Al Ghaf customer-service numbers

- Report a missing or incorrect item within 24 hours
- Celebration-cake plating fee: AED 30
- Valet parking: AED 40, Thursday to Saturday 18:00 to midnight
- Booking or order look-up locked for 15 minutes after 5 wrong attempts
- VAT: 5%, included in menu prices
