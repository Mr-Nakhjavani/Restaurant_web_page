# Bayt Al Ghaf personal details, privacy and booking look-ups

What personal details Bayt Al Ghaf asks for, why, how bookings and orders are looked up safely, and what is never asked for.

## Personal details Bayt Al Ghaf asks for

Bayt Al Ghaf asks for a name and a phone number for every reservation and order, an address for deliveries, and optionally an e-mail address. The phone number is used to confirm the booking, to call about changes, and to identify the guest when they call back.

## Looking up a Bayt Al Ghaf booking or order

Staff discuss a reservation or order only with someone who gives its reference (for example RES-482913 or ORD-604215) and the phone number used to make it. After 5 wrong attempts the record is locked for 15 minutes. Details of one guest's booking are never shared with another caller.

## What Bayt Al Ghaf never asks for

Bayt Al Ghaf never asks for card numbers, card security codes or bank passwords by phone, WhatsApp or e-mail. Payment is made in person by cash or card.
