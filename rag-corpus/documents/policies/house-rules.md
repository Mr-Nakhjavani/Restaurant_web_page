# Bayt Al Ghaf house rules

Bayt Al Ghaf house rules on alcohol, halal food, smoking and shisha, dress code, children, pets, outside food and celebration cakes, and photography.

## Alcohol at Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf is an alcohol-free restaurant. Alcohol is not served and may not be brought in. Drinks at Bayt Al Ghaf include gahwa, karak chai, sulaimani, chilled laban, limon na'na (fresh lemon-mint) and date shakes.

## Halal food at Bayt Al Ghaf

All meat and poultry are halal. No pork or pork products are used anywhere in the kitchen.

## Smoking, vaping and shisha at Bayt Al Ghaf

No smoking, vaping or shisha anywhere in the restaurant or on the terrace. Bayt Al Ghaf does not offer shisha.

## Dress code at Bayt Al Ghaf

Smart casual. Modest dress is appreciated, in keeping with the heritage quarter.

## Children at Bayt Al Ghaf

Children are very welcome. High chairs are available (6 in total) and can be requested with a reservation. There is no separate children's menu; half-portion requests are handled by the team on the day for selected rice dishes.

## Pets at Bayt Al Ghaf

Pets are not permitted inside or on the terrace. Assistance dogs are welcome.

## Outside food and celebration cakes at Bayt Al Ghaf

Outside food and drink are not permitted, except a celebration cake with prior notice (a plating fee of AED 30 applies). The celebration-cake plating fee is AED 30. Mention the cake when booking.

## Photography at Bayt Al Ghaf

Personal photography is welcome. Professional shoots require written permission from the management.
