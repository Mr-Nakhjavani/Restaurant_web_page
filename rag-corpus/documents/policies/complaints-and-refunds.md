# Bayt Al Ghaf complaints, missing items and refunds

What Bayt Al Ghaf does when a dish becomes unavailable, an item is missing or wrong, or an order is late — and how refunds work.

## When a dish becomes unavailable after ordering at Bayt Al Ghaf

If a dish sells out after an order was placed, the team removes it, re-totals the order and calls the customer to offer a substitute of similar value or a cancellation. Nothing is charged for removed items.

## Missing or incorrect items from Bayt Al Ghaf

Report a missing or incorrect item within 24 hours by phone (+971 4 555 0142) or WhatsApp (+971 50 555 0142) with the order number; a photo helps. Bayt Al Ghaf replaces the item or refunds or credits it.

## Refunds at Bayt Al Ghaf

Refunds for items paid by card go back to the original card through the restaurant's card terminal; cash payments are refunded in cash or as a credit note. No card details are ever taken over the phone.

## Late orders from Bayt Al Ghaf

If an order is more than 20 minutes late, the customer may cancel it without charge.

## Allergy notes on Bayt Al Ghaf orders

If an order's special instructions mention an allergy, the order is flagged and the duty manager calls the customer. The kitchen still cannot guarantee the absence of any allergen.
