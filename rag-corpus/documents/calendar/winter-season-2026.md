# Winter season 2026–27 at Bayt Al Ghaf

Winter season 2026–27 at Bayt Al Ghaf: dates, opening hours, and the dishes that are added or removed.

## Winter season 2026–27 at Bayt Al Ghaf: dates

Winter season 2026–27 runs from Sunday 1 November 2026 to Sunday 28 February 2027 (40 days).

Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.

## Bayt Al Ghaf opening hours during Winter season 2026–27

Bayt Al Ghaf keeps its normal opening hours during Winter season 2026–27, except where a closure is listed.

- On Sunday 1 November 2026, the dining room is open 08:00 (8 am) to 23:00 (11 pm); kitchen last orders 22:30 (10:30 pm). Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- On Saturday 21 November 2026, the dining room is open 08:00 (8 am) to midnight (24:00); kitchen last orders 23:30 (11:30 pm). Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- On Thursday 10 December 2026, the dining room is open 08:00 (8 am) to 23:00 (11 pm); kitchen last orders 22:30 (10:30 pm). Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.

## Seasonal dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf during Winter season 2026–27

- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34): served 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027. Winter only: warm pumpkin asida with ghee, saffron and cardamom.
