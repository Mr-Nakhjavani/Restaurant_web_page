# Bayt Al Ghaf special days, holidays and seasonal menus

All special periods and closures in the Bayt Al Ghaf calendar with dates, hours and the dishes they bring, plus how public holidays work.

## Bayt Al Ghaf calendar of special periods

- Winter season 2026–27: 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027; normal hours. Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- Eid Al Etihad (UAE National Day) 2026: 1 December 2026 to 3 December 2026; normal hours. Eid Al Etihad Feast available. Normal opening hours. Pre-orders for the feast open on 15 November 2026.
- Ramadan 2027 (provisional): 8 February 2027 to 9 March 2027 (provisional, subject to moon sighting); open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00). Provisional dates subject to moon sighting. During Ramadan we open from 12:00 to 24:00 daily, breakfast is not served, and the Iftar Set Menu is available from sunset.
- Eid Al Fitr 2027 (provisional): 10 March 2027 to 12 March 2027 (provisional, subject to moon sighting); normal hours. Provisional dates. The restaurant opens at 14:00 on the first day of Eid and keeps normal hours on the following days.

## Bayt Al Ghaf closures

- Saturday 14 November 2026: The dining room is closed to the public from 18:00 on Saturday 14 November 2026 for a private event. Takeaway and delivery continue as normal.
- Wednesday 10 March 2027: On the first day of Eid Al Fitr (provisionally 10 March 2027) we open at 14:00.

## Public holidays at Bayt Al Ghaf

On public holidays Bayt Al Ghaf normally keeps its regular hours. Exceptions are listed in this calendar, such as the first day of Eid Al Fitr, when the restaurant opens at 14:00.

## Bayt Al Ghaf limited-time and seasonal dishes

- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34): Limited time: served from 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027 only.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125): Ramadan only (Ramadan 2027 is provisionally 8 February – 9 March 2027). Served or collected between 17:30 and 20:30. Same-day orders must be placed by 14:00. Minimum 2 sets. Dine-in (with a reservation) or pickup; not delivered.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355): Available 1–3 December 2026 only. Pre-orders open on 15 November 2026 and must be placed at least 24 hours ahead. Pickup, delivery or dine-in. Limited to 20 per day.

## Bayt Al Ghaf terrace season

The outdoor creek terrace is seasonal: open from 15 October to 30 April, weather permitting, and closed in summer (1 May to 14 October).
