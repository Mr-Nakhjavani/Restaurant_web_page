# Saturday at Bayt Al Ghaf

Everything that is specific to Saturdays at Bayt Al Ghaf: opening hours, breakfast, specials served and not served, parking and music.

## Bayt Al Ghaf opening hours on Saturday

- Open: 08:00 (8 am) to midnight (24:00)
- Breakfast: 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am) (not during Ramadan)
- Lunch and dinner dishes: from 12:00 (noon)
- Kitchen last orders: 23:30 (11:30 pm)
- Last table reservation: 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Pickup orders ready: 08:30 (8:30 am) to 23:45 (11:45 pm)
- Delivery arrivals: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Catering collection or delivery: 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm)

## Bayt Al Ghaf specials on Saturday

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149): Saturdays and Sundays only, 08:00–12:00 (not during Ramadan). Dine-in, pickup or dine-in pre-order; not delivered. Limited to 25 spreads per day.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes not served on Saturday

- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is only served on Mondays.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is only served on Tuesdays.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is only served on Wednesdays.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) is only served on Thursdays.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter is only served on Fridays.

## Breakfast at Bayt Al Ghaf on Saturday

Breakfast on Saturday is served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am): Balaleet (AED 34), Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36), Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) and Bayd w Tamat (AED 30), plus the Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread until 12:00 (noon). Breakfast is not delivered and is not served during Ramadan.

## Saturday at Bayt Al Ghaf: good to know

- Valet parking at Heritage Quarter Gate 3 from 18:00 (6 pm) to midnight (24:00) (AED 40, paid to the valet operator).
- Saturday lunch is among the busiest times; booking ahead is recommended.
