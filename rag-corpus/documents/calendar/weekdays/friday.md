# Friday at Bayt Al Ghaf

Everything that is specific to Fridays at Bayt Al Ghaf: opening hours, breakfast, specials served and not served, parking and music.

## Bayt Al Ghaf opening hours on Friday

- Open: 08:00 (8 am) to midnight (24:00)
- Breakfast: 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am) (not during Ramadan)
- Lunch and dinner dishes: from 12:00 (noon)
- Kitchen last orders: 23:30 (11:30 pm)
- Last table reservation: 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Pickup orders ready: 08:30 (8:30 am) to 23:45 (11:45 pm)
- Delivery arrivals: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Catering collection or delivery: 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm)

## Bayt Al Ghaf specials on Friday

- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349): Fridays only, for collection or dine-in between 12:00 and 22:00. Order at least 2 hours ahead (pre-orders up to 7 days ahead). Not delivered. Limited to 12 platters per Friday.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes not served on Friday

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is only served on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is only served on Mondays.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is only served on Tuesdays.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is only served on Wednesdays.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) is only served on Thursdays.

## Breakfast at Bayt Al Ghaf on Friday

Breakfast on Friday is served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am): Balaleet (AED 34), Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36), Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) and Bayd w Tamat (AED 30). Breakfast is not delivered and is not served during Ramadan.

## Friday at Bayt Al Ghaf: good to know

- Live oud music on Friday evenings, 20:00 (8 pm) to 22:00 (10 pm).
- Valet parking at Heritage Quarter Gate 3 from 18:00 (6 pm) to midnight (24:00) (AED 40, paid to the valet operator).
- Friday evenings are among the busiest times; booking ahead is recommended. For Friday lunch, ordering a day ahead is recommended.
