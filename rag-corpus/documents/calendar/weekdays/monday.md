# Monday at Bayt Al Ghaf

Everything that is specific to Mondays at Bayt Al Ghaf: opening hours, breakfast, specials served and not served, parking and music.

## Bayt Al Ghaf opening hours on Monday

- Open: 08:00 (8 am) to 23:00 (11 pm)
- Breakfast: 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am) (not during Ramadan)
- Lunch and dinner dishes: from 12:00 (noon)
- Kitchen last orders: 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Last table reservation: 21:30 (9:30 pm)
- Pickup orders ready: 08:30 (8:30 am) to 22:45 (10:45 pm)
- Delivery arrivals: 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm)
- Catering collection or delivery: 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm)

## Bayt Al Ghaf specials on Monday

- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64): Mondays only, from 12:00 until sold out (20 portions). Can be pre-ordered up to 7 days ahead for a Monday.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes not served on Monday

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is only served on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is only served on Tuesdays.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is only served on Wednesdays.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) is only served on Thursdays.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter is only served on Fridays.

## Breakfast at Bayt Al Ghaf on Monday

Breakfast on Monday is served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am): Balaleet (AED 34), Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36), Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) and Bayd w Tamat (AED 30). Breakfast is not delivered and is not served during Ramadan.

## Monday at Bayt Al Ghaf: good to know

- There is no valet parking on this day; paid public parking is available along Al Seef Street and in the Al Seef multi-storey car park.
