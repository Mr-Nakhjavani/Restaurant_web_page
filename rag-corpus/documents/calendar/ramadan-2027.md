# Ramadan 2027 (provisional) at Bayt Al Ghaf

Ramadan 2027 (provisional) at Bayt Al Ghaf: dates (provisional), opening hours, and the dishes that are added or removed.

## Ramadan 2027 (provisional) at Bayt Al Ghaf: dates

Ramadan 2027 (provisional) runs from Monday 8 February 2027 to Tuesday 9 March 2027 (30 days). These dates are provisional: they depend on the moon sighting, and the team confirms them when they are announced.

Provisional dates subject to moon sighting. During Ramadan we open from 12:00 to 24:00 daily, breakfast is not served, and the Iftar Set Menu is available from sunset.

## Bayt Al Ghaf opening hours during Ramadan 2027 (provisional)

During Ramadan 2027 (provisional), Bayt Al Ghaf is open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00) every day.

- On Monday 8 February 2027, the dining room is open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00); kitchen last orders 23:30 (11:30 pm). Ramadan hours apply (provisional dates, subject to moon sighting). Breakfast is not served. Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- On Tuesday 23 February 2027, the dining room is open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00); kitchen last orders 23:30 (11:30 pm). Ramadan hours apply (provisional dates, subject to moon sighting). Breakfast is not served. Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- On Tuesday 9 March 2027, the dining room is open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00); kitchen last orders 23:30 (11:30 pm). Ramadan hours apply (provisional dates, subject to moon sighting). Breakfast is not served.

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): only during Ramadan

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (سفرة الإفطار) costs AED 125 and serves 1. Break your fast the Emirati way. Each set includes three Khalas dates with laban, Shorbat Adas, two sambousa, a main of your choice (lamb machboos, chicken machboos, harees or vegetable machboos), five luqaimat with dibs, and karak or sulaimani. Minimum two sets per order or table.

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is on the menu every day of the week.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is served from 17:30 (5:30 pm) until 20:30 (8:30 pm).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is available only during Ramadan.
- Same-day orders for Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) must be placed by 14:00 (2 pm).
- The minimum order for Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is 2.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) can only be ordered for pickup (takeaway) or a dine-in pre-order with a reservation.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) each day: 120 portions. Once they are gone, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 20 Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person).

Choices: Main course — Lamb machboos, Chicken machboos, Harees or Vegetable machboos; Tea — Karak or Sulaimani.

## Seasonal dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf during Ramadan 2027 (provisional)

- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34): served 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027. Winter only: warm pumpkin asida with ghee, saffron and cardamom.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes not served during Ramadan 2027 (provisional)

Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat and Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread are not served during Ramadan.

## Iftar and reservations at Bayt Al Ghaf during Ramadan

- Iftar seating starts at 17:30. Dates and laban are served at the sunset call to prayer to break the fast.
- Iftar tables are the busiest of the year; booking early is recommended.
- The Iftar Set Menu is ordered as a dine-in pre-order linked to the reservation, or for pickup. It is not delivered.
- The individual dishes of the iftar set (lentil shorba, sambousa, machboos, harees and luqaimat) are on the regular menu all year.
- The Iftar Set Menu is not available outside Ramadan.
- Eating and drinking in public during fasting hours is permitted in Dubai; the dining room is open from 12:00.
