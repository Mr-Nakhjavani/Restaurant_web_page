## Eid Al Fitr 2027 (provisional) at Bayt Al Ghaf: dates

Eid Al Fitr 2027 (provisional) runs from Wednesday 10 March 2027 to Friday 12 March 2027 (3 days). These dates are provisional: they depend on the moon sighting, and the team confirms them when they are announced.

Provisional dates. The restaurant opens at 14:00 on the first day of Eid and keeps normal hours on the following days.
