# Bayt Al Ghaf weekly specials

Which Bayt Al Ghaf dishes are cooked only on certain days of the week, how to pre-order them, and why they cannot be served on other days.

## Bayt Al Ghaf weekly specials by day

- Monday: Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64)
- Tuesday: Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58)
- Wednesday: Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (AED 54)
- Thursday: Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92)
- Friday: Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349)
- Saturday: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149)
- Sunday: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149)

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: weekly special

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is cooked only on Saturdays and Sundays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is served from 08:00 (8 am) until 12:00 (noon).
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is part of the Emirati breakfast menu, served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am).
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is not served during Ramadan.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread each day: 25 portions. Once they are gone, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 6 Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): weekly special

- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is cooked only on Mondays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Saloonat Samak (Monday) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Saloonat Samak (Monday) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Saloonat Samak (Monday).

## Friday Family Machboos Platter: weekly special

- Friday Family Machboos Platter is cooked only on Fridays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter is served from 12:00 (noon) until 22:00 (10 pm).
- Friday Family Machboos Platter must be ordered at least 2 hours in advance.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter can only be ordered for pickup (takeaway) or a dine-in pre-order with a reservation.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Friday Family Machboos Platter each day: 12 portions. Once they are gone, Friday Family Machboos Platter is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 3 Friday Family Machboos Platter.

## Pre-ordering a Bayt Al Ghaf weekly special

A weekly special can be ordered on any day for collection on its own day, up to 7 days ahead. It cannot be served on a different day at any price. For example, the Friday Family Machboos Platter ordered on a Tuesday is collected on the coming Friday.
