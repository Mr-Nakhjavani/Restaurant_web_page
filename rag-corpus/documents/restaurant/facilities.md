# Bayt Al Ghaf facilities

Facilities at Bayt Al Ghaf: dining areas, majlis rooms, Wi-Fi, prayer room, takeaway counter, live music, accessibility and baby facilities.

## Facilities at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Indoor dining hall with creek-view window tables
- Outdoor creek terrace (seasonal, mid-October to end of April)
- Two private majlis rooms with traditional floor seating
- Step-free entrance from the Al Seef promenade and an accessible restroom
- Six high chairs and a baby-changing table in the accessible restroom
- Free Wi-Fi (network “BaytAlGhaf-Guest”, password printed on the menu)
- Prayer room for men and women on the first floor of the Heritage Quarter building (shared facility)
- Takeaway collection counter at the Al Seef Street entrance
- Live oud music on Friday evenings, 20:00–22:00

## Wi-Fi at Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf has free Wi-Fi for guests. The network is called “BaytAlGhaf-Guest”; the password is printed on the menu.

## Prayer room near Bayt Al Ghaf

A prayer room for men and women is on the first floor of the Heritage Quarter building, shared with the other businesses in the building.

## Live music at Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf has live oud music on Friday evenings from 20:00 (8 pm) to 22:00 (10 pm). There is no live music on other days.

## Takeaway collection counter at Bayt Al Ghaf

Takeaway orders are collected at the counter by the Al Seef Street entrance (Heritage Quarter Gate 3), with a 10-minute drop-off bay outside.
