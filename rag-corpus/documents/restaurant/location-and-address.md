# Bayt Al Ghaf location and address

Where Bayt Al Ghaf is: full address, entrance, GPS coordinates, how to describe the location, and distances to nearby landmarks.

## Bayt Al Ghaf address

The full address of Bayt Al Ghaf is: Bayt Al Ghaf, Unit 14, Heritage Quarter, Al Seef Street, Al Seef, Bur Dubai, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

- Unit: Unit 14, Heritage Quarter
- Building: Al Seef Heritage Quarter (creek-side, ground floor)
- Street: Al Seef Street
- Area: Al Seef, Bur Dubai (community: Al Hamriya)
- City and emirate: Dubai, Dubai
- Country: United Arab Emirates
- GPS coordinates: 25.2637, 55.3027 (the Al Seef creek-side promenade)

## Bayt Al Ghaf entrance

The Bayt Al Ghaf entrance faces Al Seef Street at Heritage Quarter Gate 3. A drop-off bay is directly outside the takeaway collection counter at the Al Seef Street entrance. From the promenade side, Bayt Al Ghaf is the coral-stone building with the brass ghaf-tree sign.

## How to describe where Bayt Al Ghaf is

We are on the creek-side promenade of Al Seef, between the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood and the Bur Dubai abra station. The easiest landmark to give a taxi driver is “Al Seef, near Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood”. A good short description is: “Bayt Al Ghaf, Al Seef, Heritage Quarter Gate 3, Bur Dubai — near the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood.”
