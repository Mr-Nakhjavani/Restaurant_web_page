# Accessibility at Bayt Al Ghaf

Wheelchair access at Bayt Al Ghaf: step-free route, accessible restroom, wheelchair-accessible tables by area, majlis seating and accessible parking.

## Step-free access to Bayt Al Ghaf

- Step-free route from Gate 3 and from the promenade, accessible restroom on the ground floor.
- Wheelchair-accessible tables are available in the main hall, at the window and on the terrace (via a ramp). The majlis rooms use floor seating and are not suitable for wheelchairs; Majlis Al Sidr can be set with low chairs on request.

## Wheelchair-accessible tables at Bayt Al Ghaf

13 bookable tables at Bayt Al Ghaf are wheelchair accessible: W1 (window, 1–2 guests), W2 (window, 1–2 guests), W3 (window, 2–4 guests), W4 (window, 2–4 guests), W6 (window, 4–6 guests), M3 (indoor, 1–2 guests), M6 (indoor, 2–4 guests), M10 (indoor, 2–4 guests), M11 (indoor, 4–6 guests), M13 (indoor, 6–8 guests), L1 (indoor, 9–14 guests), T4 (terrace, 2–4 guests) and T6 (terrace, 2–4 guests). Mention a wheelchair when booking and only these tables are offered. Walk-in table M1 and M5 are also accessible.

## Bayt Al Ghaf majlis rooms and wheelchairs

The majlis rooms use traditional floor seating and are not suitable for wheelchairs. Majlis Al Sidr can be set with low chairs on request.

## Accessible parking at Bayt Al Ghaf

Two accessible parking bays are beside Heritage Quarter Gate 3, next to the entrance.

## Assistance dogs at Bayt Al Ghaf

Pets are not permitted inside or on the terrace, but assistance dogs are welcome.
