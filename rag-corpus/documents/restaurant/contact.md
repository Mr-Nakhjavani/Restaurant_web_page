- To order takeaway or delivery: call +971 4 555 0142 or order on the website.
- For a problem with an order (missing or incorrect item): call +971 4 555 0142 or send a WhatsApp message with a photo to +971 50 555 0142 within 24 hours.
- For events, private hire or groups above 20 guests: e-mail events@baytalghaf.example.
