## Bayt Al Ghaf breakfast hours

Bayt Al Ghaf serves Emirati breakfast every day from 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am), except during Ramadan. The Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is served on Saturdays and Sundays from 08:00 (8 am) to 12:00 (noon). Breakfast is not delivered.

## Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen last orders

The Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen takes its last orders 30 minutes before closing: 22:30 (10:30 pm) on Monday to Thursday and Sunday, and 23:30 (11:30 pm) on Friday and Saturday.

## Bayt Al Ghaf last reservation times

The last table reservation at Bayt Al Ghaf starts 90 minutes before closing: 21:30 (9:30 pm) on Monday to Thursday and Sunday, and 22:30 (10:30 pm) on Friday and Saturday. Breakfast tables can be booked from opening time.

## Bayt Al Ghaf pickup, delivery and catering hours

- Pickup orders can be ready from 30 minutes after opening (08:30 (8:30 am)) until 15 minutes before closing (22:45 (10:45 pm) Monday–Thursday and Sunday, 23:45 (11:45 pm) Friday and Saturday).
- Delivery arrives between 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm) every day.
- Catering orders can be collected or delivered between 10:00 (10 am) to 21:00 (9 pm).

## Bayt Al Ghaf hours on holidays and special days

- Winter season 2026–27 (2026-11-01 to 2027-02-28): Winter dessert menu (Aseedat Bobar) is served during this period.
- Eid Al Etihad (UAE National Day) 2026 (2026-12-01 to 2026-12-03): Eid Al Etihad Feast available. Normal opening hours. Pre-orders for the feast open on 15 November 2026.
- Ramadan 2027 (provisional) (2027-02-08 to 2027-03-09, provisional — subject to moon sighting): open 12:00 (noon) to midnight (24:00) every day. Provisional dates subject to moon sighting. During Ramadan we open from 12:00 to 24:00 daily, breakfast is not served, and the Iftar Set Menu is available from sunset.
- Eid Al Fitr 2027 (provisional) (2027-03-10 to 2027-03-12, provisional — subject to moon sighting): Provisional dates. The restaurant opens at 14:00 on the first day of Eid and keeps normal hours on the following days.
- 2026-11-14: The dining room is closed to the public from 18:00 on Saturday 14 November 2026 for a private event. Takeaway and delivery continue as normal.
- 2027-03-10: On the first day of Eid Al Fitr (provisionally 10 March 2027) we open at 14:00.
- On other public holidays the restaurant keeps its normal hours unless a closure is announced.

## Can Bayt Al Ghaf stay open late for a group?

No. Bayt Al Ghaf cannot extend its opening hours for individual guests or groups. For a late private event, the events team (events@baytalghaf.example) can discuss a private booking in advance, but nothing beyond the published hours can be promised by phone.
