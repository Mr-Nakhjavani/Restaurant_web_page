# Families and children at Bayt Al Ghaf

Visiting Bayt Al Ghaf with children: high chairs, baby-changing, children's portions, tables with space for high chairs, non-spicy dishes and family platters.

## Children at Bayt Al Ghaf

Children are very welcome. High chairs are available (6 in total) and can be requested with a reservation. There is no separate children's menu; half-portion requests are handled by the team on the day for selected rice dishes.

## High chairs at Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf has 6 high chairs in total, and up to 4 can be requested with one reservation. Children count towards the party size when booking. Tables with space for high chairs: M6, M7, M11, M12, M13 and L1.

## Baby-changing at Bayt Al Ghaf

A baby-changing table is in the accessible restroom on the ground floor.

## Non-spicy dishes at Bayt Al Ghaf

Dishes that are not spicy at all (spice level 0): Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Shorbat Adas, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Harees, Luqaimat, Khanfaroosh, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Karak Chai, Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na and Date & Cardamom Shake.

## Majlis rooms for families

Families who want privacy can book a majlis room: Majlis Al Sidr (6–10 guests) or Majlis Al Ghaf (10–20 guests), with at least 24 hours' notice and a minimum spend of AED 150 per person.
