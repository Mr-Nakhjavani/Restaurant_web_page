# Payments and prices at Bayt Al Ghaf

How to pay at Bayt Al Ghaf: accepted and refused payment methods, payment for pickup, delivery and pre-orders, VAT, catering deposits and refunds.

## Payment methods accepted at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Cash (AED only)
- Visa, Mastercard and American Express (chip & PIN or contactless)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (contactless at the counter or table)

## Payment methods not accepted at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Online card payment: orders are paid on collection, on delivery or at the table
- Foreign currency cash
- Cheques
- Cryptocurrency

## Paying for orders at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Pickup orders: Cash on collection (AED) or Card or Apple Pay / Google Pay on collection.
- Delivery orders: Cash on delivery (AED) or Card on delivery (driver's card machine). Drivers carry change up to AED 200.
- Dine-in pre-orders: Pay at the table after the meal.
- Catering deposits: Deposit by bank transfer, arranged by the catering team.

## Card details and Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf never takes card numbers over the phone, by WhatsApp or by e-mail. Cards are only used in person on the restaurant's or the driver's card machine.

## VAT and prices at Bayt Al Ghaf

All Bayt Al Ghaf menu prices are in UAE dirhams (AED) and include 5% VAT. Delivery fees are shown separately. For example, a dish priced AED 72 (72 dirhams) includes AED 3.43 VAT.

## Refunds at Bayt Al Ghaf

Refunds for items paid by card go back to the original card through the restaurant's card terminal; cash payments are refunded in cash or as a credit note. No card details are ever taken over the phone.
