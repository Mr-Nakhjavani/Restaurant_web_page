# Glossary of Emirati food and hospitality terms at Bayt Al Ghaf

Meanings of Emirati food, drink and hospitality words used at Bayt Al Ghaf, with Arabic spellings, from gahwa and majlis to every dish name on the menu.

## gahwa (قهوة)

gahwa (Arabic: قهوة): Emirati coffee: lightly roasted Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron, poured from the dallah into small finjan cups and served with dates. On the menu as Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table).

## dallah (دلة)

dallah (Arabic: دلة): The traditional brass coffee pot used to pour gahwa.

## finjan (فنجان)

finjan (Arabic: فنجان): The small handle-less cup gahwa is served in.

## majlis (مجلس)

majlis (Arabic: مجلس): A traditional sitting room for receiving guests, with floor seating.

## bezar (بزار)

bezar (Arabic: بزار): The Emirati spice blend (cumin, coriander, fennel, pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, cardamom, chilli, clove, nutmeg) that seasons machboos and saloona.

## loomi (لومي)

loomi (Arabic: لومي): Dried black lime (sometimes called dried lemon), used whole or ground for a sour, smoky flavour. A citrus fruit, not an allergen.

## regag (رقاق)

regag (Arabic: رقاق): Paper-thin crisp Emirati bread cooked on a hot iron plate.

## iftar (إفطار)

iftar (Arabic: إفطار): The meal that breaks the fast at sunset during Ramadan.

## Khalas dates (تمر خلاص)

Khalas dates (Arabic: تمر خلاص): A prized Emirati date variety, served with gahwa.

## abra (عبرة)

abra (Arabic: عبرة): The traditional wooden water taxi that crosses Dubai Creek.

## ghaf (غاف)

ghaf (Arabic: غاف): Prosopis cineraria, the UAE's national tree and the tree the restaurant is named after.

## Balaleet (بلاليط)

Balaleet (Arabic: بلاليط): Sweet saffron and cardamom vermicelli crowned with a thin omelette. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 34.

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (خبز جباب)

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (Arabic: خبز جباب): Three saffron-cardamom Emirati pancakes with warm dibs and cream cheese. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 36.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese (رقاق بالبيض والجبن)

Regag with Egg & Cheese (Arabic: رقاق بالبيض والجبن): Paper-thin crisp regag folded over egg and melted cheese. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 28.

## Bayd w Tamat (بيض وطماط)

Bayd w Tamat (Arabic: بيض وطماط): Soft eggs scrambled with tomato, onion, green chilli and bezar, with regag. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 30.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (فوالة الصباح)

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (Arabic: فوالة الصباح): Saturdays & Sundays only: a generous traditional breakfast spread for two. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 149.

## Shorbat Adas (شوربة عدس)

Shorbat Adas (Arabic: شوربة عدس): Velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lime and sourdough croutons. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 26.

## Sambousa Trio (سمبوسة)

Sambousa Trio (Arabic: سمبوسة): Six crisp sambousa: spiced lamb, akkawi cheese and potato-vegetable, with dakkus. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 34.

## Nakhi (نخي)

Nakhi (Arabic: نخي): Warm boiled chickpeas with cumin, chilli and lime — an old Emirati snack. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 22.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (حمص باللحم والصنوبر)

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (Arabic: حمص باللحم والصنوبر): Silky tahini hummus topped with bezar-spiced lamb and toasted pine nuts. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 38.

## Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (سلطة البيت)

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (Arabic: سلطة البيت): Tomato, cucumber, jarjeer, radish and pomegranate with loomi-sumac dressing. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 30.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites (رقاق بالمهياوة)

Mehyawa Regag Bites (Arabic: رقاق بالمهياوة): Crisp regag spread with mehyawa, egg and cheese, cut into bites. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 26.

## Harees (هريس)

Harees (Arabic: هريس): Wheat and lamb slow-cooked overnight and beaten smooth, finished with ghee. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 48.

## Lamb Thareed (ثريد لحم)

Lamb Thareed (Arabic: ثريد لحم): Lamb and vegetable saloona ladled over layers of regag bread. On the Bayt Al Ghaf menu at AED 62.

## Saloonat Dajaj (صالونة دجاج)

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (مرقوقة لحم)

## Camel Machboos (Thursday) (مجبوس لحم إبل)
