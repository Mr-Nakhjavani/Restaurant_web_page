# Parking at Bayt Al Ghaf

Where to park near Bayt Al Ghaf: public parking, the multi-storey car park, valet days, times and price, accessible bays and the takeaway drop-off bay.

## Parking near Bayt Al Ghaf

- Paid public parking is available along Al Seef Street and in the Al Seef multi-storey car park (about 3 minutes' walk). Standard Dubai public-parking tariffs apply and are paid through the official parking app, SMS or ticket machines.
- Valet parking is offered by the Heritage Quarter at Gate 3 from 18:00 to 24:00 on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays (AED 40, paid to the valet operator).
- Two accessible parking bays are located beside Gate 3.
- Takeaway customers may use the 10-minute drop-off bay outside the collection counter.

## Valet parking at Bayt Al Ghaf

Valet parking for Bayt Al Ghaf guests is run by the Heritage Quarter at Gate 3, from 18:00 (6 pm) to midnight (24:00) on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays only. It costs AED 40, paid to the valet operator (not to the restaurant). There is no valet on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday.

## Can Bayt Al Ghaf reserve parking?

No. Bayt Al Ghaf cannot reserve parking spaces. Public parking along Al Seef Street and in the Al Seef multi-storey car park is first come, first served and paid at standard Dubai public-parking tariffs.
