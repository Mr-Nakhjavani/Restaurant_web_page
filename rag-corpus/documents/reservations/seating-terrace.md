# Bayt Al Ghaf seating: Creek terrace (outdoor, seasonal)

The creek terrace (outdoor, seasonal) at Bayt Al Ghaf: tables, party sizes, features and booking rules.

## Creek terrace (outdoor, seasonal) at Bayt Al Ghaf

The creek terrace is outdoors and seasonal: it is open from 15/10 to 30/04 (day/month), that is from 15 October to 30 April, weather permitting, and closed in summer. Heaters are not available.

This area has 9 tables for parties of 1 to 8 guests.

## Tables in the creek terrace (outdoor, seasonal) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- T1: 1–2 guests; outdoors, creek view
- T2: 1–2 guests; outdoors, creek view
- T3: 1–2 guests; outdoors
- T4: 2–4 guests; outdoors, creek view, wheelchair accessible
- T5: 2–4 guests; outdoors, creek view
- T6: 2–4 guests; outdoors, wheelchair accessible
- T7: 2–4 guests; outdoors
- T8: 4–6 guests; outdoors, creek view
- T9: 6–8 guests; outdoors, creek view
