# Bayt Al Ghaf reservation statuses explained

What each Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status means.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Requested — awaiting confirmation

Requested — awaiting confirmation (system status pending_confirmation): A table is held while the team reviews the request (groups of 13 to 20). It is not confirmed until the team calls back and confirms it.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Confirmed

Confirmed (system status confirmed): The table is booked.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Seated

Seated (system status seated): The guests have arrived and are seated.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Completed

Completed (system status completed): The visit is finished.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Cancelled

Cancelled (system status cancelled): The reservation was cancelled by the guest or by the restaurant.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: No-show

No-show (system status no_show): The guests did not arrive within the 15-minute grace period and the table was released.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation status: Declined

Declined (system status rejected): The restaurant could not accommodate the request.
