# Bayt Al Ghaf seating: Main dining hall (indoor)

The main dining hall (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf: tables, party sizes, features and booking rules.

## Main dining hall (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf

The main dining hall is air-conditioned, with booths, family tables with space for high chairs and a long table (L1) for 9 to 14 guests.

This area has 14 tables for parties of 1 to 14 guests.

## Tables in the main dining hall (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- M1: 1–2 guests; kept for walk-in guests (not bookable in advance), wheelchair accessible; walk-in only
- M2: 1–2 guests; kept for walk-in guests (not bookable in advance); walk-in only
- M3: 1–2 guests; wheelchair accessible
- M4: 1–2 guests
- M5: 2–4 guests; kept for walk-in guests (not bookable in advance), wheelchair accessible; walk-in only
- M6: 2–4 guests; wheelchair accessible, space for high chairs
- M7: 2–4 guests; space for high chairs
- M8: 2–4 guests; booth seating
- M9: 2–4 guests; booth seating
- M10: 2–4 guests; wheelchair accessible
- M11: 4–6 guests; wheelchair accessible, space for high chairs
- M12: 4–6 guests; space for high chairs
- M13: 6–8 guests; wheelchair accessible, space for high chairs
- L1: 9–14 guests; long table for groups, wheelchair accessible, space for high chairs
