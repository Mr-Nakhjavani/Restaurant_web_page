# Changing or cancelling a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation, late arrival and no-shows

Until when a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation can be changed, cancellation rules, late cancellations, the late-arrival grace period, no-shows and finding a booking.

## Changing a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation

The date, time, party size, seating area and requests can be changed until 2 hours before the booking, subject to availability. After that, guests must call the restaurant on +971 4 555 0142. If a requested change is not possible, the original booking stays as it was.

## Cancelling a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation

Cancelling a reservation is free at any time before the booking time. Cancelling less than 2 hours before is recorded as a late cancellation. Cancelling also cancels any linked dine-in pre-order the kitchen has not started.

## Arriving late for a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation

Tables are held for 15 minutes after the booking time. After that the table may be released and the booking marked as a no-show. Guests running late should call +971 4 555 0142.

## Finding a Bayt Al Ghaf reservation

To check, change or cancel a booking, give the reservation reference (for example RES-482913) and the phone number used to book. After 5 wrong attempts, the booking is locked for 15 minutes to protect the guest.

## Walk-in guests at Bayt Al Ghaf

Walk-ins are welcome whenever a table is free. Tables M1, M2 and M5 are kept for walk-in guests. There is no walk-in waiting list by phone.
