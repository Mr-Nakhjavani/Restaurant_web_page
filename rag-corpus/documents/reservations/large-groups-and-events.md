# Large groups and events at Bayt Al Ghaf

Booking Bayt Al Ghaf for groups of 13 to 20, majlis rooms, groups above 20, private events and celebrations.

## Group bookings at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Up to 12 guests: confirmed instantly if a table is free.
- 13 to 20 guests: a table is held and the booking is a request until the team calls back, usually within 4 hours.
- More than 20 guests: not bookable as a reservation — contact the events team at events@baytalghaf.example.
- Book for the number of guests expected. If numbers are uncertain, book the likely number and update it before the change cut-off; tables for large groups may be reduced if far fewer guests arrive.

## Large tables at Bayt Al Ghaf

The long table L1 in the main hall seats 9 to 14 guests. Majlis Al Sidr seats 6 to 10 and Majlis Al Ghaf seats 10 to 20. Groups too large for any regular table are offered a majlis room.

## Private events at Bayt Al Ghaf

Private hire and full buyouts are arranged by the events team (events@baytalghaf.example). For example, the dining room is closed to the public from 18:00 on Saturday 14 November 2026 for a private event.

## Celebrations at Bayt Al Ghaf

Guests may bring a celebration cake with prior notice; a plating fee of AED 30 applies. Other outside food and drink are not permitted.
