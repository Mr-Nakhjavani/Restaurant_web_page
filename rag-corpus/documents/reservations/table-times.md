# How long a table is booked at Bayt Al Ghaf

Bayt Al Ghaf table times by party size, majlis bookings and the reset time between bookings.

## Bayt Al Ghaf table time by party size

- 1 to 2 guests: 90 minutes (1.5 hours)
- 3 to 6 guests: 120 minutes (2 hours)
- 7 to 12 guests: 150 minutes (2.5 hours)
- 13 to 20 guests: 180 minutes (3 hours)

Majlis room bookings are always 180 minutes. There is a 15-minute reset between bookings on the same table.
