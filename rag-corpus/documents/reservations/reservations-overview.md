# Reservations at Bayt Al Ghaf

How to book a table at Bayt Al Ghaf: party sizes, notice, how far ahead, booking hours, what details are needed, seating preferences, large groups and confirmation.

## Bayt Al Ghaf reservation rules in brief

- Tables can be booked for 1 to 20 guests.
- Bookings can be made from 30 minutes ahead up to 60 days ahead, on 15-minute times (for example 19:00, 19:15, 19:30).
- Groups of up to 12 are confirmed immediately when a table is free.
- Groups of 13 to 20 are accepted as a request: a table is held and the team calls back to confirm, usually within 4 hours.
- Groups of more than 20 cannot be booked as a normal reservation; contact the events team at events@baytalghaf.example.
- A name and a phone number are needed for every reservation (UAE number, or international with the country code).
- Bookings are made by phone on +971 4 555 0142, on the Bayt Al Ghaf website, or by e-mail to reservations@baytalghaf.example.
- Every booking gets a reference such as RES-482913.

## Choosing where to sit at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Guests can ask for the main dining hall (indoor), a creek-view window table, the creek terrace (seasonal) or a private majlis room.
- A requested seating area is respected: if it is full, the team offers other times or another area. Guests are never moved to a different area without being asked.
- With no preference, the team chooses the best-fitting table: the main hall first, then the terrace (in season), then the window tables.
- A specific table number cannot be promised.
- If the window tables are fully booked, one cannot be “made available”; the team offers another time with window availability or a different area.

## Wheelchairs, high chairs and children at Bayt Al Ghaf

- Mention a wheelchair when booking and only step-free, accessible tables are offered (in the main hall, at the window and on the terrace at T4 and T6). The majlis rooms have floor seating.
- Up to 4 high chairs can be requested per booking (6 in the restaurant).
- Children count towards the party size.

## Deposits for Bayt Al Ghaf reservations

No deposit or card guarantee is taken for regular tables. The majlis minimum spend is settled at the end of the meal.

## Busiest times at Bayt Al Ghaf

Friday evenings and Saturday lunch are the busiest regular times. Iftar tables during Ramadan are the busiest of the year and should be booked early.
