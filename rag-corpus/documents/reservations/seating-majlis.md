# Bayt Al Ghaf seating: Private majlis room (floor seating)

The private majlis room (floor seating) at Bayt Al Ghaf: tables, party sizes, features and booking rules.

## Private majlis room (floor seating) at Bayt Al Ghaf

Two private majlis rooms with traditional floor seating: Majlis Al Sidr (6 to 10 guests; low chairs on request) and Majlis Al Ghaf (10 to 20 guests). A majlis booking needs at least 6 guests and 24 hours' notice, lasts 180 minutes, and has a minimum spend of AED 150 per person, settled at the end of the meal.

This area has 2 tables for parties of 6 to 20 guests.

## Tables in the private majlis room (floor seating) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- MJ-SIDR: 6–10 guests; private room, traditional floor seating, low chairs on request
- MJ-GHAF: 10–20 guests; private room, traditional floor seating
