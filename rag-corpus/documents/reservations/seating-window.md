# Bayt Al Ghaf seating: Creek-view window tables (indoor)

The creek-view window tables (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf: tables, party sizes, features and booking rules.

## Creek-view window tables (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf

The window tables are indoors along the glass facing Dubai Creek. They are the most requested tables at Bayt Al Ghaf, so booking early is recommended.

This area has 6 tables for parties of 1 to 6 guests.

## Tables in the creek-view window tables (indoor) at Bayt Al Ghaf

- W2: 1–2 guests; creek view, wheelchair accessible
- W3: 2–4 guests; creek view, wheelchair accessible
- W4: 2–4 guests; creek view, wheelchair accessible
- W5: 2–4 guests; creek view
- W6: 4–6 guests; creek view, wheelchair accessible
