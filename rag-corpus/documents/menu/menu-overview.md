# Bayt Al Ghaf menu overview

An overview of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: 46 dishes in 11 sections, signature dishes, how prices, allergens and availability work.

## The Bayt Al Ghaf menu in brief

Bayt Al Ghaf serves Emirati home cooking: machboos, harees, thareed, saloona, regag, luqaimat, Gulf seafood, charcoal grills and contemporary camel dishes. The menu has 46 dishes in 11 sections. Prices run from AED 10 (Sulaimani) to AED 355 (Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)) for regular dishes; catering items such as the Whole Lamb Ouzi cost more.

All prices are in UAE dirhams (AED) and include 5% VAT. All meat and poultry are halal. Bayt Al Ghaf is alcohol-free and uses no pork or gelatine.

## Sections of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

- Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي): 5 dishes — Served daily from 08:00 to 11:30 (not during Ramadan). The weekend breakfast spread is served on Saturdays and Sundays until 12:00.
- Starters & Mezze (المقبلات): 6 dishes — Small plates to share, from lentil shorba to sambousa and mehyawa regag.
- Traditional Emirati (أطباق تقليدية): 6 dishes — Home-style stews and slow-cooked classics, including our weekday specials.
- Rice & Machboos (المجبوس والأرز): 4 dishes — Machboos and muhammar — spiced and sweet rice dishes at the heart of the Emirati table.
- Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات): 6 dishes — Charcoal grills, Gulf seafood and contemporary camel dishes.
- Vegetarian (أطباق نباتية): 3 dishes — Plant-based saloona and machboos. All three dishes are vegan.
- Desserts (الحلويات): 4 dishes — Luqaimat, khanfaroosh and seasonal sweets.
- Gahwa & Hot Drinks (القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة): 3 dishes — Emirati coffee poured from the dallah, karak and sulaimani.
- Cold Drinks (المشروبات الباردة): 3 dishes — Laban, fresh lemon-mint and date shakes. We are an alcohol-free restaurant.
- Family Platters & Catering (الصواني العائلية والولائم): 4 dishes — Sharing platters for the majlis, the Friday family platter, whole lamb ouzi and office lunch boxes.
- Seasonal & Occasions (المواسم والمناسبات): 2 dishes — Menus served only during Ramadan and UAE National Day (Eid Al Etihad).

## Bayt Al Ghaf signature dishes

The signature dishes of Bayt Al Ghaf are Balaleet (AED 34), Harees (AED 48), Lamb Thareed (AED 62), Lamb Machboos (AED 72), Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69), Luqaimat (AED 32), Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30), Karak Chai (AED 12), Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) and Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450). Featured on the home page: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Harees, Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Hammour Mashwi, Luqaimat, Majlis Sharing Platter and Friday Family Machboos Platter.

## Types of dishes on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

- Permanent dishes (35) are on the menu every day, within breakfast or lunch-and-dinner hours.
- Weekly specials (6) are cooked only on particular days: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (Saturday and Sunday), Saloonat Samak (Monday) (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (Wednesday), Camel Machboos (Thursday) (Thursday) and Friday Family Machboos Platter (Friday).
- Limited-time dishes (3) are served only on certain dates: Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).
- Catering dishes (2) are sold only through catering orders: Whole Lamb Ouzi and Office Machboos Lunch Box.

## How allergens are listed on the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Every Bayt Al Ghaf dish lists the allergens it contains as ingredients and the allergens it may contain as traces. Allergen information is worked out from each ingredient, including the ingredients inside sauces, marinades and spice blends, so a dish's allergens always match its recipe. Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Vegan and vegetarian choices at Bayt Al Ghaf

Vegan dishes: Shorbat Adas, Nakhi, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Sulaimani and Limon Na'na. Vegetarian (not vegan) dishes: Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Luqaimat, Khanfaroosh, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Karak Chai, Chilled Laban with Mint and Date & Cardamom Shake. Pescatarian dishes: Mehyawa Regag Bites, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Muhammar with Fried Safi, Hammour Mashwi and Rubyan Mashwi.
