# Limon Na'na (ليمون بالنعناع)

Limon Na'na is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 22. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Limon Na'na at a glance

Limon Na'na (Arabic: ليمون بالنعناع) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Cold Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Limon Na'na costs AED 22 (22 dirhams) and serves one person. Fresh lemon juice blended with mint and ice.

- Dish: Limon Na'na (ليمون بالنعناع)
- Price: AED 22 (22 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Cold Drinks (المشروبات الباردة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 400 ml; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 4 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever we are open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and delivery
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_limon_nana (web page: /menu/limon-nana)

## What Limon Na'na is

Freshly squeezed lemons blended with mint, sugar and crushed ice until frosty and bright green.

How Limon Na'na is cooked: Blended to order. Style: Gulf café classic.

Guests also ask for Limon Na'na as “lemon mint”, “limonana”, “lemon juice” or “mint lemonade”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Limon Na'na: price and portion

Limon Na'na costs AED 22 (22 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 22 is AED 1.05.

One order of Limon Na'na is 400 ml, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Limon Na'na.

## Limon Na'na: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Limon Na'na as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Lemon: 60 ml juice
- Mint: 6 g
- Sugar: 25 g (can be left out on request)

Limon Na'na is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Limon Na'na: allergens

Limon Na'na contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Limon Na'na has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Limon Na'na contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Limon Na'na: dietary suitability

Limon Na'na is vegan (no animal products). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

## When Limon Na'na is available

- Limon Na'na is on the menu every day of the week.
- Limon Na'na is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Limon Na'na.

## Ordering Limon Na'na: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): no

Ordered for pickup on its own, Limon Na'na can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (4 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Limon Na'na for catering and large orders

Limon Na'na can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 100
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Limon Na'na

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Limon Na'na with Hammour Mashwi (AED 98) and Sambousa Trio (AED 34).
