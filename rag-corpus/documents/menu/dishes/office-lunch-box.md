# Office Machboos Lunch Box (صندوق غداء المجبوس)

Office Machboos Lunch Box is a catering dish, sold only as part of a catering order at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 45. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Office Machboos Lunch Box at a glance

Office Machboos Lunch Box (Arabic: صندوق غداء المجبوس) is a catering dish, sold only as part of a catering order in the Family Platters & Catering section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Office Machboos Lunch Box costs AED 45 (45 dirhams) and serves one person. Catering only, minimum 10: machboos, house salad, three luqaimat and water.

## What Office Machboos Lunch Box is

Individually packed lunch boxes for offices and events: a full portion of machboos (chicken, lamb or vegetable) with dakkus, a small house salad, three luqaimat and a bottle of water. Labelled by box with the main and allergens.

How Office Machboos Lunch Box is cooked: Batch-cooked and packed hot. Style: Catering.

Guests also ask for Office Machboos Lunch Box as “lunch box”, “office lunch”, “corporate lunch”, “meal box” or “box meal”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Office Machboos Lunch Box: price and portion

Office Machboos Lunch Box costs AED 45 (45 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 45 is AED 2.14.

One order of Office Machboos Lunch Box is one box: machboos (150 g dry rice with chicken/lamb/vegetables), 120 g salad, 3 luqaimat, 330 ml water, and serves one person.

## Office Machboos Lunch Box: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Office Machboos Lunch Box. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Office Machboos Lunch Box. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Office Machboos Lunch Box (40 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Office Machboos Lunch Box. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is served on the side with Office Machboos Lunch Box (15 ml). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Office Machboos Lunch Box: allergens

Office Machboos Lunch Box contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee, the milk powder and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Office Machboos Lunch Box may contain traces of eggs, celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Office Machboos Lunch Box contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Office Machboos Lunch Box

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Office Machboos Lunch Box; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Office Machboos Lunch Box; it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame cannot be removed from Office Machboos Lunch Box; it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Office Machboos Lunch Box by choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
