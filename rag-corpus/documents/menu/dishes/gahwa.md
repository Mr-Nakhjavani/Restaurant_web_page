# Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (قهوة عربية)

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 30. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) at a glance

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (Arabic: قهوة عربية) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Gahwa & Hot Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) and serves 3 people. A dallah of lightly roasted Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron, served with dates.

- Dish: Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (قهوة عربية)
- Price: AED 30 (30 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Gahwa & Hot Drinks (القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: One dallah (about 500 ml, around 8 finjan cups) with 6 dates; serves 2–4; serves 3 people
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 8 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever we are open. Takeaway in an insulated flask; not delivered
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is one of Bayt Al Ghaf's signature dishes
- Menu code: mi_gahwa (web page: /menu/gahwa)

## What Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is

Emirati hospitality in a pot: lightly roasted Arabica beans brewed with cardamom, saffron and a hint of clove and rosewater, poured from a brass dallah into small finjan cups. Served with six Khalas dates. Gahwa is unsweetened and contains no milk.

How Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is cooked: Brewed and simmered in the dallah. Style: Traditional Emirati coffee service.

Guests also ask for Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) as “gahwa”, “qahwa”, “kahwa”, “arabic coffee”, “emirati coffee”, “dallah” or “coffee”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): price and portion

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 30 is AED 1.43.

One order of Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is one dallah (about 500 ml, around 8 finjan cups) with 6 dates; serves 2–4, and serves 3 people. That works out at about AED 10 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table).

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Arabic coffee beans (light roast): 25 g
- Green cardamom: 6 g
- Saffron: 0.1 g
- Cloves: 0.5 g
- Rosewater: 2 ml
- Khalas dates: 6 dates

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): allergens

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): how spicy it is

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is available

- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is on the menu every day of the week.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 6 Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table).

## Ordering Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (8 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) for catering and large orders

Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 30
- Catering gahwa is supplied in insulated flasks (1 flask = 1 dallah).
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).
