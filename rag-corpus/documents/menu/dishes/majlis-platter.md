# Majlis Sharing Platter (صينية المجلس)

Majlis Sharing Platter is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 329. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Majlis Sharing Platter at a glance

Majlis Sharing Platter (Arabic: صينية المجلس) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Family Platters & Catering section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Majlis Sharing Platter costs AED 329 (329 dirhams) and serves 4 people. Serves 4: lamb machboos, chicken saloona, sambousa, house salad and luqaimat.

## What Majlis Sharing Platter is

Our everyday feast for the majlis: a large tray of lamb machboos with hashu, a pot of chicken saloona, eight sambousa (lamb, cheese and vegetable), a large Salatat Al Bait, dakkus and twenty luqaimat to finish.

How Majlis Sharing Platter is cooked: Assembled from the machboos, saloona, fryer and salad stations. Style: Emirati sharing tray.

Guests also ask for Majlis Sharing Platter as “majlis platter”, “sharing platter”, “family platter”, “platter for four” or “mixed platter”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Majlis Sharing Platter: price and portion

Majlis Sharing Platter costs AED 329 (329 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 329 is AED 15.67.

## Majlis Sharing Platter: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Majlis Sharing Platter. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Majlis Sharing Platter. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Majlis Sharing Platter (150 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Majlis Sharing Platter. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is spooned on top of Majlis Sharing Platter (60 ml on the luqaimat). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Majlis Sharing Platter: allergens

Majlis Sharing Platter contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the sambousa pastry sheets and the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee, the white brined cheese, the milk powder and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Majlis Sharing Platter may contain traces of eggs, celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Majlis Sharing Platter contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Majlis Sharing Platter

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Majlis Sharing Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Majlis Sharing Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame cannot be removed from Majlis Sharing Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Majlis Sharing Platter by choosing “No hashu”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
