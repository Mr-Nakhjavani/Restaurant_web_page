# Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (مضروبة دجاج)

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 54. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) at a glance

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (Arabic: مضروبة دجاج) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) costs AED 54 (54 dirhams) and serves one person. Wednesday special: chicken and rice beaten to a creamy, spiced porridge.

## What Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is

Comfort in a bowl: chicken and basmati rice cooked with onion, tomato, cardamom, loomi and bezar until the rice breaks down, then beaten (madroob) until creamy and finished with ghee.

How Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is cooked: Slow-cooked and beaten. Style: Traditional Emirati comfort dish.

Guests also ask for Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) as “madrooba”, “madrouba”, “madhrooba”, “mashed rice chicken” or “chicken rice porridge”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): price and portion

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) costs AED 54 (54 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 54 is AED 2.57.

One order of Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is about 450 g bowl with 180 g chicken, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday).

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Chicken (bone-in): 180 g (shredded off the bone)
- Basmati rice: 100 g (dry weight)
- Onion: 40 g
- Tomato: 50 g
- Green cardamom: 1 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 1/2 (ground)
- Ghee (clarified butter): 15 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Chicken stock (house-made): 400 ml
- Salt: 2 g

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (3 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): allergens

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) contains milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday)

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday); it is part of the recipe.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): how spicy it is

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be reduced; the dish is not made hot.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? No.

Options that change the spice of Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)) and “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)).

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): customisation options

**Spice level** for Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)

## When Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is available

- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is cooked only on Wednesdays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday).

## Ordering Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) can be ready at the earliest 25 minutes after the order is placed (15 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) for catering and large orders

Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 8 per order.

## What goes well with Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday)

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) with Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) and Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14).
