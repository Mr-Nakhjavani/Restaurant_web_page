# Rubyan Mashwi (روبيان مشوي)

Rubyan Mashwi is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 105. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Rubyan Mashwi at a glance

Rubyan Mashwi (Arabic: روبيان مشوي) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Rubyan Mashwi costs AED 105 (105 dirhams) and serves one person. Six jumbo Gulf prawns grilled in garlic-lime butter, with rice and House Green.

- Dish: Rubyan Mashwi (روبيان مشوي)
- Price: AED 105 (105 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 6 jumbo prawns (about 300 g shell-on) with 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person; 6 pieces
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 18 minutes
- Diet: pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat)
- Contains allergens: crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) and milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: gluten (wheat), fish, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard and sesame
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_rubyan_mashwi (web page: /menu/rubyan-mashwi)

## What Rubyan Mashwi is

Six jumbo Gulf prawns, split and grilled over charcoal, spooned with garlic-lime butter and served with white basmati rice and our herb-bright Al Ghaf House Green.

How Rubyan Mashwi is cooked: Grilled over charcoal. Style: Gulf seafood grill.

Guests also ask for Rubyan Mashwi as “grilled prawns”, “grilled shrimp”, “rubyan”, “prawns” or “shrimp”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Rubyan Mashwi: price and portion

Rubyan Mashwi costs AED 105 (105 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 105 is AED 5.

One order of Rubyan Mashwi is 6 jumbo prawns (about 300 g shell-on) with 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Rubyan Mashwi.

## Rubyan Mashwi: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Rubyan Mashwi as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Gulf prawns: 6 jumbo (about 300 g shell-on) (contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp))
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed
- Lime: 1/2 lime
- Salt: 2 g

Rubyan Mashwi is also made with Garlic Lime Butter and Al Ghaf House Green; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Rubyan Mashwi: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Garlic Lime Butter** (زبدة الثوم والليمون) is cooked into Rubyan Mashwi. Melted butter with garlic, lime, parsley and a pinch of chilli, spooned over grilled prawns. Garlic Lime Butter is made from butter, garlic, lime, parsley and dried red chilli. Garlic Lime Butter contains milk (dairy).

**Al Ghaf House Green** (صلصة الغاف الخضراء) is served on the side with Rubyan Mashwi (50 g). Our second house sauce: a vivid herb sauce of coriander, parsley, mint and green chilli with lime. It has no nut, dairy, gluten or sesame ingredients, but it is made on the same station as the walnut-based House Red. Al Ghaf House Green is made from coriander leaves, parsley, mint, green chilli, garlic, lime, olive oil, cumin and salt. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of gluten (wheat), tree nuts, celery and mustard.

## Rubyan Mashwi: allergens

Rubyan Mashwi contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Crustaceans (prawns and shrimp): from the gulf prawns
- Milk (dairy): from the Garlic Lime Butter, which contains butter

Rubyan Mashwi may contain traces of gluten (wheat), fish, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard and sesame. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Made in the same blender and on the same station as Al Ghaf House Red, which contains walnuts and wheat; we cannot rule out traces of tree nuts or gluten.
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Rubyan Mashwi contains no molluscs, eggs, peanuts, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Rubyan Mashwi

- Crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) cannot be removed from Rubyan Mashwi; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) can be left out of Rubyan Mashwi by choosing “No butter (grilled with olive oil)”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## Rubyan Mashwi: dietary suitability

Rubyan Mashwi is pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Rubyan Mashwi is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Rubyan Mashwi is not vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Rubyan Mashwi is pescatarian (fish or seafood, no meat).
- Gluten: Rubyan Mashwi has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).
- Dairy: Rubyan Mashwi contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Rubyan Mashwi has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Rubyan Mashwi has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Rubyan Mashwi has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Rubyan Mashwi contains shellfish (crustaceans or molluscs).
- Halal: Rubyan Mashwi is halal.

## Rubyan Mashwi: how spicy it is

Rubyan Mashwi is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. The butter has a pinch of chilli that can be left out; House Green is medium-spicy and can be swapped for toum.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

## Rubyan Mashwi: customisation options

**Dipping sauce** for Rubyan Mashwi — choose one (required):

- Al Ghaf House Green — standard choice: no extra charge (option code sauce_green)
- Toum garlic sauce instead: no extra charge (option code sauce_toum)

**Butter** for Rubyan Mashwi — choose one (required):

- Garlic-lime butter — standard choice: no extra charge (option code butter_yes)
- No butter (grilled with olive oil): no extra charge; removes milk (dairy) from the recipe (option code butter_no)

## When Rubyan Mashwi is available

- Rubyan Mashwi is on the menu every day of the week.
- Rubyan Mashwi is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Rubyan Mashwi.

## Ordering Rubyan Mashwi: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Rubyan Mashwi can be ready at the earliest 28 minutes after the order is placed (18 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Rubyan Mashwi for catering and large orders

Rubyan Mashwi is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 8 per order.

## What goes well with Rubyan Mashwi

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Rubyan Mashwi with Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) and Limon Na'na (AED 22).

## Questions guests ask about Rubyan Mashwi

**Q: Are the prawns peeled?**

A: They are split and grilled in the shell for flavour, with the vein removed; they are easy to peel at the table.

**Q: I am allergic to shellfish — what can I eat?**

A: Avoid Rubyan Mashwi. Our grills are shared with prawns, so grilled dishes may contain traces of crustaceans. Dishes from the stew and rice sections are not cooked on the grill, but the kitchen does handle shellfish.

**Q: How much is Rubyan Mashwi?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi costs AED 105 (105 dirhams), including VAT.

**Q: How many people does Rubyan Mashwi serve?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi serves one person (6 jumbo prawns (about 300 g shell-on) with 120 g (dry) rice).

**Q: Is Rubyan Mashwi vegan?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi is not vegan; it is pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat).

**Q: Does Rubyan Mashwi contain nuts?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi has no nut ingredients, but it may contain traces of tree nuts. The kitchen is not nut-free.

**Q: Does Rubyan Mashwi contain dairy?**

A: Yes, Rubyan Mashwi contains milk (dairy).

**Q: Is Rubyan Mashwi gluten-free?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi has no gluten-containing ingredients, but Bayt Al Ghaf is not a certified gluten-free kitchen and this dish may contain traces of gluten.

**Q: How spicy is Rubyan Mashwi?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi is mild (spice level 1 of 3). The butter has a pinch of chilli that can be left out; House Green is medium-spicy and can be swapped for toum.

**Q: Can I get Rubyan Mashwi delivered?**

A: Yes, Rubyan Mashwi can be delivered within Bayt Al Ghaf's delivery zones, 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm).

**Q: How long does Rubyan Mashwi take?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi takes about 18 minutes to prepare. For a pickup order, allow at least 28 minutes from ordering.
