# Saloonat Samak (Monday) (صالونة سمك)

Saloonat Samak (Monday) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 64. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday) at a glance

Saloonat Samak (Monday) (Arabic: صالونة سمك) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Saloonat Samak (Monday) costs AED 64 (64 dirhams) and serves one person. Monday special: kingfish steaks in a tamarind-tomato saloona with rice.

- Dish: Saloonat Samak (Monday) (صالونة سمك)
- Price: AED 64 (64 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Traditional Emirati (أطباق تقليدية)
- Menu type: a weekly special, cooked only on particular days
- Portion: About 520 g: 220 g kingfish steak (bone-in) in sauce, plus 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)

## What Saloonat Samak (Monday) is

Every Monday, our kingfish (kanaad) steaks are marinated in bezar and lime, then simmered in a tangy tamarind and tomato saloona with loomi, garlic and green chilli. Served with white basmati rice and fresh coriander.

How Saloonat Samak (Monday) is cooked: Marinated, then simmered in sauce. Style: Coastal Emirati fish stew.

Guests also ask for Saloonat Samak (Monday) as “fish saloona”, “fish stew”, “fish curry”, “kingfish stew” or “kanaad saloona”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): price and portion

Saloonat Samak (Monday) costs AED 64 (64 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 64 is AED 3.05.

One order of Saloonat Samak (Monday) is about 520 g: 220 g kingfish steak (bone-in) in sauce, plus 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Saloonat Samak (Monday).

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Saloonat Samak (Monday) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Kingfish (kanaad): 220 g bone-in steak (contains fish)
- Tomato: 100 g
- Tomato paste: 10 g
- Onion: 50 g
- Garlic: 5 g
- Tamarind: 10 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 1 whole (pierced)
- Green chilli: 6 g
- Coriander leaves: 3 g
- Sunflower oil: 15 ml
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed
- Salt: 2 g

Saloonat Samak (Monday) is also made with Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade and Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade** (تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون) is used as the marinade for Saloonat Samak (Monday). Bright marinade for fish: lime juice, bezar, turmeric and garlic. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is made from lime, turmeric, garlic, sunflower oil, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Saloonat Samak (Monday) (2 g in the sauce). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): allergens

Saloonat Samak (Monday) contains fish as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Fish: from the kingfish

Saloonat Samak (Monday) may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Saloonat Samak (Monday) contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Saloonat Samak (Monday)

- Fish cannot be removed from Saloonat Samak (Monday); it is part of the recipe.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): how spicy it is

Saloonat Samak (Monday) is medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) as standard. Can be made mild or hot; the tamarind sourness remains.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Saloonat Samak (Monday): “Mild” (mild (spice level 1 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Saloonat Samak (Monday): customisation options

**Spice level** for Saloonat Samak (Monday) — choose one (required):

- Mild: no extra charge; spice becomes mild (spice level 1 of 3) (option code spice_mild)
- Medium — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

## When Saloonat Samak (Monday) is available

- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is cooked only on Mondays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Saloonat Samak (Monday) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Saloonat Samak (Monday) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Saloonat Samak (Monday).

## Ordering Saloonat Samak (Monday): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Saloonat Samak (Monday) can be ready at the earliest 28 minutes after the order is placed (18 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Saloonat Samak (Monday) for catering and large orders

Saloonat Samak (Monday) is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 8 per order.

## What goes well with Saloonat Samak (Monday)

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Saloonat Samak (Monday) with Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) and Limon Na'na (AED 22).
