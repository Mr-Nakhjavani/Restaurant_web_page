# Camel Machboos (Thursday) (مجبوس لحم إبل)

Camel Machboos (Thursday) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 92. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

Camel Machboos (Thursday) costs AED 92 (92 dirhams) and serves one person. Thursday special: young camel slow-braised for three hours, with spiced rice and hashu.

## What Camel Machboos (Thursday) is

A Thursday-night feast: young camel (hashi) shoulder braised slowly for three hours with loomi, onion, tomato and whole spices, then the basmati is cooked in the rich braising liquid with bezar. Topped with hashu and served with dakkus.

How Camel Machboos (Thursday) is cooked: Braised for 3 hours; rice steamed in the braising liquid. Style: Traditional Emirati festive machboos.

Guests also ask for Camel Machboos (Thursday) as “camel machboos”, “machboos laham ibil”, “camel rice” or “camel kabsa”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Camel Machboos (Thursday): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Camel Machboos (Thursday) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Camel meat: 280 g (raw weight)
- Basmati rice: 150 g (dry weight)
- Onion: 60 g

Camel Machboos (Thursday) is also made with Bezar Spice Blend, Hashu Topping and Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Camel Machboos (Thursday): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Camel Machboos (Thursday) (5 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Camel Machboos (Thursday) (35 g). Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Camel Machboos (Thursday) (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Camel Machboos (Thursday): allergens

Camel Machboos (Thursday) contains milk (dairy) and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the ghee and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Camel Machboos (Thursday) may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Camel Machboos (Thursday) contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Camel Machboos (Thursday)

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Camel Machboos (Thursday); it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Camel Machboos (Thursday) by choosing “Hashu without raisins”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## Camel Machboos (Thursday): how spicy it is

Camel Machboos (Thursday) is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Machboos is mild (warm spice rather than chilli heat) and cannot be made milder: the rice is cooked in stock seasoned with bezar. The heat comes from the dakkus served on the side, which can be skipped or doubled.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

## When Camel Machboos (Thursday) is available

- Camel Machboos (Thursday) is cooked only on Thursdays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Camel Machboos (Thursday) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Camel Machboos (Thursday) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Camel Machboos (Thursday).

## Ordering Camel Machboos (Thursday): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Camel Machboos (Thursday) can be ready at the earliest 25 minutes after the order is placed (15 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.
