## What Muhammar with Fried Safi is

A seafaring classic of the pearl-diving coast: basmati rice cooked with date syrup, sugar, saffron, cardamom and rosewater until amber and fragrant, served with a whole safi (rabbitfish) marinated in bezar and lime and fried until crisp. Dakkus on the side cuts the sweetness.

How Muhammar with Fried Safi is cooked: Rice steamed with date syrup; fish deep-fried in the dedicated seafood fryer. Style: Traditional coastal Emirati dish.

Guests also ask for Muhammar with Fried Safi as “muhammar”, “mhammar”, “sweet rice”, “date rice”, “safi” or “rabbitfish”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Muhammar with Fried Safi: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Muhammar with Fried Safi as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Basmati rice: 150 g (dry weight)
- Date syrup (dibs): 30 ml
- Sugar: 10 g
- Saffron: 0.1 g
- Green cardamom: 1 g
- Rosewater: 3 ml
- Ghee (clarified butter): 10 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Safi (rabbitfish): 1 whole fish (about 300 g) (contains fish)
- Sunflower oil: for frying
- Salt: 2 g

Muhammar with Fried Safi is also made with Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade and Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Muhammar with Fried Safi: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade** (تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون) is used as the marinade for Muhammar with Fried Safi. Bright marinade for fish: lime juice, bezar, turmeric and garlic. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is made from lime, turmeric, garlic, sunflower oil, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Muhammar with Fried Safi (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Muhammar with Fried Safi: allergens

Muhammar with Fried Safi contains fish and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Fish: from the safi
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Muhammar with Fried Safi may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Muhammar with Fried Safi contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Muhammar with Fried Safi

- Fish cannot be removed from Muhammar with Fried Safi; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Muhammar with Fried Safi; it is part of the recipe.

## Ordering Muhammar with Fried Safi: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes
