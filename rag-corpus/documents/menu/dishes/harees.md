# Harees (هريس)

Harees is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 48. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Harees at a glance

Harees (Arabic: هريس) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Harees costs AED 48 (48 dirhams) and serves one person. Wheat and lamb slow-cooked overnight and beaten smooth, finished with ghee.

- Dish: Harees (هريس)
- Price: AED 48 (48 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Traditional Emirati (أطباق تقليدية)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 400 g bowl; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 8 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Served from 12:00 until sold out. Limited to 30 portions per day
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Harees is one of Bayt Al Ghaf's signature dishes
- Menu code: mi_harees (web page: /menu/harees)

## What Harees is

One of the oldest Emirati dishes: whole hulled wheat and lamb cooked slowly overnight in a sealed pot, then beaten with a wooden madhrab until smooth and elastic. Served with a pool of warm ghee and a dusting of cinnamon. Made in a single batch each night, so quantities are limited.

How Harees is cooked: Slow-cooked for 10 hours, then beaten by hand. Style: Traditional Emirati festive and Ramadan dish.

Guests also ask for Harees as “harees”, “harris”, “hareis”, “haris” or “wheat and meat porridge”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Harees: price and portion

Harees costs AED 48 (48 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 48 is AED 2.29.

One order of Harees is about 400 g bowl, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Harees:

- Extra ghee: +AED 3 (total AED 51)

## Harees: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Harees as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Hulled wheat (harees wheat): 120 g (dry weight) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Lamb shoulder: 100 g boneless
- Ghee (clarified butter): 20 g (10 g beaten in, 10 g poured on top) (contains milk (dairy))
- Cinnamon: 0.5 g
- Salt: 2 g

Harees is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Harees: allergens

Harees contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the hulled wheat
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Harees has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Harees contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Harees

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Harees; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Harees; it is part of the recipe.

## Harees: dietary suitability

Harees is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Harees is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Harees is not vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Harees is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Harees contains gluten.
- Dairy: Harees contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Harees has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Harees has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Harees has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Harees has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Harees is halal.

## Harees: how spicy it is

Harees is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Harees is not spiced with chilli and cannot be made spicy.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Harees is available

- Harees is on the menu every day of the week.
- Harees is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Harees each day: 30 portions. Once they are gone, Harees is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 6 Harees.

## Ordering Harees: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Harees can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (8 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Harees for catering and large orders

Harees can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 40
- Notice needed: at least 48 hours
- Bulk harees is cooked in a dedicated overnight batch and needs at least 48 hours' notice.
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Harees

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Harees with Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14) and Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30).
