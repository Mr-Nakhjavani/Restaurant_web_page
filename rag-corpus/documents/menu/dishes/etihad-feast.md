# Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (وليمة عيد الاتحاد)

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 355. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) at a glance

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (Arabic: وليمة عيد الاتحاد) is a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates in the Seasonal & Occasions section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) costs AED 355 (355 dirhams) and serves 4 people. 1–3 December only. Serves 4: camel machboos, harees, chebab, luqaimat and gahwa.

- Dish: Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (وليمة عيد الاتحاد)
- Price: AED 355 (355 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Seasonal & Occasions (المواسم والمناسبات)
- Menu type: a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates
- Portion: Serves 4: 2 camel machboos, 2 harees, 4 chebab, large salad, 20 luqaimat, 1 dallah of gahwa; serves 4 people
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 30 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs, milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Available 1–3 December 2026 only. Pre-orders open on 15 November 2026 and must be placed at least 24 hours ahead. Pickup, delivery or dine-in. Limited to 20 per day
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_etihad_feast (web page: /menu/etihad-feast)

## What Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is

Our celebration of the Union, available only for Eid Al Etihad (UAE National Day): two portions of Thursday's camel machboos, two bowls of harees, four chebab with dibs, a large house salad, twenty luqaimat and a dallah of gahwa with dates — served on a brass tray.

How Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is cooked: Assembled from the festive kitchen production. Style: Emirati celebration tray.

Guests also ask for Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) as “national day”, “national day special”, “eid al etihad”, “uae national day”, “national day feast” or “union feast”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): price and portion

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) costs AED 355 (355 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 355 is AED 16.90.

One order of Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is serves 4: 2 camel machboos, 2 harees, 4 chebab, large salad, 20 luqaimat, 1 dallah of gahwa, and serves 4 people. That works out at about AED 88.75 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

## Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is spooned on top of Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): allergens

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) contains gluten (wheat), eggs, milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the hulled wheat and the wheat flour
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee, the full-cream milk, the milk powder and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## When Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is available

- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is on the menu every day of the week.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is available only during UAE National Day (Eid Al Etihad).
- Orders for Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) open on 15 November 2026; earlier orders cannot be accepted.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) must be ordered at least 24 hours in advance.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 4 Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
