# Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (حمص باللحم والصنوبر)

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 38. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts at a glance

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (Arabic: حمص باللحم والصنوبر) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts costs AED 38 (38 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Silky tahini hummus topped with bezar-spiced lamb and toasted pine nuts.

## What Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is

Smooth chickpea hummus whipped with tahini, lemon and garlic, crowned with minced lamb sizzled with bezar, toasted pine nuts, sumac and parsley. Served with warm regag for scooping.

How Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is cooked: Hummus blended fresh daily; lamb pan-fried to order. Style: Levantine-Gulf mezze.

Guests also ask for Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts as “hummus”, “houmous”, “hummus lahm” or “hummus with meat”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: price and portion

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts costs AED 38 (38 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 38 is AED 1.81.

One order of Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is about 300 g with 1 regag, and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 19 per person.

Options that change the price of Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts:

- No lamb (vegetarian hummus): −AED 8 (cheaper) (total AED 30)

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Chickpeas: 150 g
- Tahini: 30 g (contains sesame)
- Lemon: 15 ml juice
- Garlic: 3 g
- Olive oil: 15 ml
- Minced lamb: 70 g (can be left out on request)
- Pine nuts: 8 g (can be left out on request; contains tree nuts)
- Sumac: 0.5 g
- Parsley: 2 g
- Salt: 2 g
- Regag bread (house-made): 1 sheet, on the side (can be left out on request; contains gluten (wheat))

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (seasons the lamb). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: allergens

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts contains gluten (wheat), tree nuts and sesame as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread
- Tree nuts: from the pine nuts
- Sesame: from the tahini

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts

- Gluten (wheat) can be left out of Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts by choosing “No regag”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Tree nuts can be left out of Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts by choosing “No pine nuts”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Sesame cannot be removed from Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts; it is part of the recipe.

Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Mildly spiced; extra chilli on request.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.
