# Nakhi (نخي)

Nakhi is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 22. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Nakhi at a glance

Nakhi (Arabic: نخي) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Nakhi costs AED 22 (22 dirhams) and serves one person. Warm boiled chickpeas with cumin, chilli and lime — an old Emirati snack.

- Dish: Nakhi (نخي)
- Price: AED 22 (22 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Starters & Mezze (المقبلات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 220 g; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 6 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_nakhi (web page: /menu/nakhi)

## What Nakhi is

Chickpeas simmered until soft, then tossed with toasted cumin, a pinch of dried chilli, sea salt, lime and fresh coriander. A humble, much-loved snack sold by Emirati street vendors for generations.

How Nakhi is cooked: Boiled and tossed warm. Style: Traditional Emirati snack.

Guests also ask for Nakhi as “nakhi”, “dango”, “chickpeas”, “spiced chickpeas” or “nikhi”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Nakhi: price and portion

Nakhi costs AED 22 (22 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 22 is AED 1.05.

One order of Nakhi is about 220 g, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Nakhi.

## Nakhi: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Nakhi as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Chickpeas: 180 g (cooked weight)
- Cumin: 2 g
- Dried red chilli: 1 g (can be left out on request)
- Lime: 1/4 lime
- Coriander leaves: 3 g
- Salt: 2 g

Nakhi is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Nakhi: allergens

Nakhi contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Nakhi may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Nakhi contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Nakhi: how spicy it is

Nakhi is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be left out or increased.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Nakhi: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)), “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Nakhi: customisation options

**Spice level** for Nakhi — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

## When Nakhi is available

- Nakhi is on the menu every day of the week.
- Nakhi is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Nakhi.

## Ordering Nakhi: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Nakhi can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (6 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Nakhi for catering and large orders

Nakhi can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 80
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Nakhi

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Nakhi with Karak Chai (AED 12) and Sambousa Trio (AED 34).
