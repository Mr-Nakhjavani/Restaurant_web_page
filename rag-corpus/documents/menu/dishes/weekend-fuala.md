# Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (فوالة الصباح)

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 149. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread at a glance

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (Arabic: فوالة الصباح) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days in the Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread costs AED 149 (149 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Saturdays & Sundays only: a generous traditional breakfast spread for two.

- Dish: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (فوالة الصباح)
- Price: AED 149 (149 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي)
- Menu type: a weekly special, cooked only on particular days
- Portion: Serves 2: balaleet (1 portion), 2 chebab, 2 regag with egg & cheese, bayd w tamat (1 portion), 8 dates, 80 g white cheese, 2 karak; serves 2 people
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 20 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Saturdays and Sundays only, 08:00–12:00 (not during Ramadan). Dine-in, pickup or dine-in pre-order; not delivered. Limited to 25 spreads per day
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_weekend_fuala (web page: /menu/weekend-fuala)

## What Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is

Our weekend tradition, inspired by the fuala hospitality spread offered to guests: balaleet, two chebab with dibs, regag with egg and cheese, bayd w tamat, Khalas dates, white akkawi cheese and two glasses of karak.

How Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is cooked: Assembled to order from the breakfast station. Style: Traditional Emirati hospitality spread.

Guests also ask for Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread as “weekend breakfast”, “fuala”, “fowala”, “traditional breakfast”, “breakfast platter” or “breakfast for two”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: price and portion

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread costs AED 149 (149 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 149 is AED 7.10.

One order of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is serves 2: balaleet (1 portion), 2 chebab, 2 regag with egg & cheese, bayd w tamat (1 portion), 8 dates, 80 g white cheese, 2 karak, and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 74.50 per person.

Options that change the price of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread:

- Dallah of gahwa instead: +AED 12 (total AED 161)

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Wheat vermicelli: 120 g (balaleet) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Wheat flour: 80 g (chebab) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Regag bread (house-made): 2 sheets (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggs: 6 eggs across the dishes (contains eggs)
- Full-cream milk: 50 ml (chebab batter) (contains milk (dairy))
- Ghee (clarified butter): 25 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Processed cheese: 30 g (contains milk (dairy))
- White brined cheese (akkawi): 80 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Tomato: 150 g
- Onion: 50 g
- Green chilli: 5 g (can be left out on request)
- Khalas dates: 8 dates
- Saffron: 0.15 g
- Green cardamom: 2 g
- Rosewater: 5 ml
- Sugar: 45 g (balaleet, chebab and karak)
- Dried yeast: 1 g
- Black tea: 10 g (karak)
- Evaporated milk: 120 ml (karak) (contains milk (dairy))

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is also made with Dibs (Date Syrup) and Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is served on the side with Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread. Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (in the bayd w tamat). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: allergens

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat vermicelli, the wheat flour and the regag bread
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Milk (dairy): from the full-cream milk, the ghee, the processed cheese, the white brined cheese and the evaporated milk

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs cannot be removed from Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread; it is part of the recipe.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: how spicy it is

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Only the bayd w tamat contains chilli; it can be made without chilli.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? No.

Options that change the spice of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)) and “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)).

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: customisation options

**Spice level** for Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)

**Drinks** for Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread — choose one (required):

- 2 karak chai — standard choice: no extra charge (option code drinks_karak)
- 2 sulaimani (no milk): no extra charge (option code drinks_sulaimani)
- Dallah of gahwa instead: +AED 12 (option code drinks_gahwa)

## When Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is available

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is cooked only on Saturdays and Sundays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is served from 08:00 (8 am) until 12:00 (noon).
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is part of the Emirati breakfast menu, served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am).
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is not served during Ramadan.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread each day: 25 portions. Once they are gone, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 6 Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread.

## Ordering Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread can be ready at the earliest 30 minutes after the order is placed (20 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread for catering and large orders

Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 6 per order.

## What goes well with Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread with Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) and Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14).
