# Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (سفرة الإفطار)

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 125. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) at a glance

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (Arabic: سفرة الإفطار) is a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates in the Seasonal & Occasions section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) costs AED 125 (125 dirhams) and serves one person. Ramadan only: dates & laban, lentil shorba, sambousa, a main of your choice, luqaimat and tea.

- Dish: Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (سفرة الإفطار)
- Price: AED 125 (125 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Seasonal & Occasions (المواسم والمناسبات)
- Menu type: a limited-time dish, served only on certain dates
- Portion: Per person: 3 dates, 200 ml laban, 250 ml soup, 2 sambousa, 1 main, 5 luqaimat, 1 tea; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 20 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), celery, sesame and sulphites
- May contain traces of: eggs and mustard
- When: Ramadan only (Ramadan 2027 is provisionally 8 February – 9 March 2027). Served or collected between 17:30 and 20:30. Same-day orders must be placed by 14:00. Minimum 2 sets. Dine-in (with a reservation) or pickup; not delivered
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_iftar_set (web page: /menu/iftar-set)

## What Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is

Break your fast the Emirati way. Each set includes three Khalas dates with laban, Shorbat Adas, two sambousa, a main of your choice (lamb machboos, chicken machboos, harees or vegetable machboos), five luqaimat with dibs, and karak or sulaimani. Minimum two sets per order or table.

How Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is cooked: Prepared in sittings timed to sunset. Style: Ramadan iftar set.

Guests also ask for Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) as “iftar”, “iftar menu”, “ramadan menu”, “iftar set”, “ramadan set menu” or “fast breaking”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): price and portion

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) costs AED 125 (125 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 125 is AED 5.95.

One order of Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) is per person: 3 dates, 200 ml laban, 250 ml soup, 2 sambousa, 1 main, 5 luqaimat, 1 tea, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person).

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is spooned on top of Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): allergens

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), celery, sesame and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the sourdough croutons, the sambousa pastry sheets and the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the laban, the white brined cheese, the ghee, the milk powder, the evaporated milk and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Celery: from the vegetable stock and the celery
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) may contain traces of eggs and mustard. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.
