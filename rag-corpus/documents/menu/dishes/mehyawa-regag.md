# Mehyawa Regag Bites (رقاق بالمهياوة)

Mehyawa Regag Bites is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 26. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites at a glance

Mehyawa Regag Bites (Arabic: رقاق بالمهياوة) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Mehyawa Regag Bites costs AED 26 (26 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Crisp regag spread with mehyawa, egg and cheese, cut into bites.

- Dish: Mehyawa Regag Bites (رقاق بالمهياوة)
- Price: AED 26 (26 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Starters & Mezze (المقبلات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 8 bites from 2 regag (about 150 g); serves 2 people; 8 pieces
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 8 minutes
- Diet: pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs, fish, milk (dairy) and mustard
- May contain traces of: celery
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open; not delivered because regag loses its crispness
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_mehyawa_regag (web page: /menu/mehyawa-regag)

## What Mehyawa Regag Bites is

A Gulf-coast favourite: wafer-thin regag brushed with mehyawa — our fermented fish sauce with mustard seed and spices — then layered with egg and melted cheese and cut into crisp bites for the table.

How Mehyawa Regag Bites is cooked: Baked to order on the iron plate. Style: Traditional Emirati coastal snack.

Guests also ask for Mehyawa Regag Bites as “mehyawa”, “mahyawa”, “regag mehyawa” or “fish sauce bread”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites: price and portion

Mehyawa Regag Bites costs AED 26 (26 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 26 is AED 1.24.

One order of Mehyawa Regag Bites is 8 bites from 2 regag (about 150 g), and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 13 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Mehyawa Regag Bites.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Mehyawa Regag Bites as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Regag bread (house-made): 2 sheets (about 50 g) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggs: 1 egg (contains eggs)
- Processed cheese: 20 g (contains milk (dairy))

Mehyawa Regag Bites is also made with Mehyawa; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Mehyawa** (مهياوة) is cooked into Mehyawa Regag Bites (15 g). A pungent, savoury fermented fish sauce from the Gulf coast, traditionally spread on regag with egg and cheese. Mehyawa is made from small salted fish (sardine/anchovy), salt, mustard seeds, cumin, fennel seed and coriander seed. Mehyawa contains fish and mustard and may contain traces of celery.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites: allergens

Mehyawa Regag Bites contains gluten (wheat), eggs, fish, milk (dairy) and mustard as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Fish: from the Mehyawa, which contains small salted fish
- Milk (dairy): from the processed cheese
- Mustard: from the Mehyawa, which contains mustard seeds

Mehyawa Regag Bites may contain traces of celery. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Mehyawa Regag Bites contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Mehyawa Regag Bites

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Mehyawa Regag Bites; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs cannot be removed from Mehyawa Regag Bites; it is part of the recipe.
- Fish cannot be removed from Mehyawa Regag Bites; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Mehyawa Regag Bites; it is part of the recipe.
- Mustard cannot be removed from Mehyawa Regag Bites; it is part of the recipe.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites: how spicy it is

Mehyawa Regag Bites is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Mehyawa is savoury and pungent rather than hot; it cannot be removed from this dish.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Mehyawa Regag Bites is available

- Mehyawa Regag Bites is on the menu every day of the week.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Mehyawa Regag Bites.

## Ordering Mehyawa Regag Bites: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Mehyawa Regag Bites can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (8 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Mehyawa Regag Bites is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Mehyawa Regag Bites for catering and large orders

Mehyawa Regag Bites is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Mehyawa Regag Bites
