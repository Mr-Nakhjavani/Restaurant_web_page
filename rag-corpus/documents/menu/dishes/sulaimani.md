# Sulaimani (شاي سليماني)

Sulaimani is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 10. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Sulaimani at a glance

Sulaimani (Arabic: شاي سليماني) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Gahwa & Hot Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Sulaimani costs AED 10 (10 dirhams) and serves one person. Clear black tea with lime and mint.

- Dish: Sulaimani (شاي سليماني)
- Price: AED 10 (10 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Gahwa & Hot Drinks (القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 200 ml glass or takeaway cup; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 4 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever we are open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and delivery
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_sulaimani (web page: /menu/sulaimani)

## What Sulaimani is

Light, clear black tea brewed with fresh mint and served with a squeeze of lime. Sweetened to your taste.

How Sulaimani is cooked: Brewed. Style: Gulf tea.

Guests also ask for Sulaimani as “sulaimani”, “suleimani”, “black tea”, “lemon tea” or “tea”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Sulaimani: price and portion

Sulaimani costs AED 10 (10 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 10 is AED 0.48.

One order of Sulaimani is 200 ml glass or takeaway cup, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Sulaimani.

## Sulaimani: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Sulaimani as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Black tea: 3 g
- Lime: 1 wedge
- Mint: 2 g
- Sugar: 10 g (can be left out on request)

Sulaimani is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Sulaimani: allergens

Sulaimani contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Sulaimani has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Sulaimani contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Sulaimani: dietary suitability

Sulaimani is vegan (no animal products). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Sulaimani is vegan.
- Vegetarian: Sulaimani is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Sulaimani is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Sulaimani has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).
- Dairy: Sulaimani has no dairy ingredients.
- Eggs: Sulaimani has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Sulaimani has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Sulaimani has no sesame ingredients.

## Sulaimani: how spicy it is

Sulaimani is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Sulaimani is available

- Sulaimani is on the menu every day of the week.
- Sulaimani is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Sulaimani.

## Ordering Sulaimani: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): no

Ordered for pickup on its own, Sulaimani can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (4 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Sulaimani for catering and large orders

Sulaimani is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Sulaimani

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Sulaimani with Luqaimat (AED 32).
