# Lamb Thareed (ثريد لحم)

Lamb Thareed is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 62. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

Lamb Thareed (Arabic: ثريد لحم) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Lamb Thareed costs AED 62 (62 dirhams) and serves one person. Lamb and vegetable saloona ladled over layers of regag bread.

## What Lamb Thareed is

A dish of the Prophet's tradition and of every Emirati Ramadan table: a rich lamb and vegetable saloona with potato, zucchini, carrot and tomato, seasoned with loomi and bezar, poured over torn regag so the bread soaks up the broth.

How Lamb Thareed is cooked: Lamb braised for 2.5 hours; assembled to order. Style: Traditional Emirati stew.

Guests also ask for Lamb Thareed as “thareed”, “tharid”, “threed”, “fareed” or “lamb stew with bread”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Lamb Thareed: price and portion

Lamb Thareed costs AED 62 (62 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 62 is AED 2.95.

One order of Lamb Thareed is about 550 g: 200 g bone-in lamb, vegetables and 80 g regag, and serves one person.

## Lamb Thareed: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Lamb Thareed as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Lamb shoulder: 200 g bone-in
- Regag bread (house-made): 80 g (contains gluten (wheat))

Lamb Thareed is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Lamb Thareed: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Lamb Thareed (3 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Lamb Thareed: allergens

Lamb Thareed contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Lamb Thareed may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Lamb Thareed contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Lamb Thareed

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Lamb Thareed; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Lamb Thareed; it is part of the recipe.

## Lamb Thareed: how spicy it is

Lamb Thareed is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. The chilli in the saloona can be reduced; extra dakkus can be added for heat.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Lamb Thareed: “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).
