# Sambousa Trio (سمبوسة)

Sambousa Trio is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 34. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Sambousa Trio at a glance

Sambousa Trio (Arabic: سمبوسة) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Sambousa Trio costs AED 34 (34 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Six crisp sambousa: spiced lamb, akkawi cheese and potato-vegetable, with dakkus.

## What Sambousa Trio is

Hand-folded triangles of thin pastry, fried until crisp: two filled with bezar-spiced minced lamb and onion, two with akkawi cheese and coriander, and two with potato, carrot and green chilli. Served with dakkus for dipping.

How Sambousa Trio is cooked: Deep-fried in sunflower oil. Style: Gulf street snack, a Ramadan staple.

Guests also ask for Sambousa Trio as “sambousa”, “samosa”, “sambusa” or “samboosa”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Sambousa Trio: price and portion

Sambousa Trio costs AED 34 (34 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 34 is AED 1.62.

One order of Sambousa Trio is 6 pieces (2 lamb, 2 cheese, 2 vegetable), about 240 g, and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 17 per person.

Options that change the price of Sambousa Trio:

- All lamb (6 pieces): +AED 4 (total AED 38)
- Extra dakkus: +AED 4 (total AED 38)

Sambousa Trio is also made with Bezar Spice Blend and Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Sambousa Trio: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Sambousa Trio (seasons the lamb filling). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Sambousa Trio. The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Sambousa Trio: allergens

Sambousa Trio contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the sambousa pastry sheets
- Milk (dairy): from the white brined cheese

Sambousa Trio may contain traces of eggs, celery, mustard and sesame. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Sambousa Trio contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Sambousa Trio

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Sambousa Trio; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) can be left out of Sambousa Trio by choosing “All lamb (6 pieces)”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

Sambousa Trio is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Sambousa Trio is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Sambousa Trio is not vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Sambousa Trio is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Sambousa Trio contains gluten.
- Dairy: Sambousa Trio contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Sambousa Trio has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Sambousa Trio has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Sambousa Trio has no sesame ingredients.

- Halal: Sambousa Trio is halal.

## Sambousa Trio: how spicy it is

Sambousa Trio is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. The fillings are pre-seasoned; ask for extra dakkus for more heat.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.
