# Bamia Saloona (صالونة بامية)

Bamia Saloona is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 44. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Bamia Saloona at a glance

Bamia Saloona (Arabic: صالونة بامية) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Vegetarian section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Bamia Saloona costs AED 44 (44 dirhams) and serves one person. Tender okra in a garlicky tomato saloona with coriander and loomi; with rice.

- Dish: Bamia Saloona (صالونة بامية)
- Price: AED 44 (44 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Vegetarian (أطباق نباتية)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 500 g with 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 12 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_bamia_saloona (web page: /menu/bamia-saloona)

## What Bamia Saloona is

Small, tender okra simmered in a tomato sauce with plenty of garlic, fresh coriander, loomi and a little bezar, finished with olive oil. Served with white basmati rice.

How Bamia Saloona is cooked: Simmered. Style: Gulf home-style stew.

Guests also ask for Bamia Saloona as “bamia”, “okra”, “okra stew” or “bamya”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Bamia Saloona: price and portion

Bamia Saloona costs AED 44 (44 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 44 is AED 2.10.

One order of Bamia Saloona is about 500 g with 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Bamia Saloona.

## Bamia Saloona: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Bamia Saloona as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Okra: 200 g
- Tomato: 120 g
- Tomato paste: 10 g
- Onion: 40 g
- Garlic: 10 g
- Coriander leaves: 5 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 1/2 (pierced)
- Olive oil: 15 ml
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed
- Salt: 2 g

Bamia Saloona is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Bamia Saloona: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Bamia Saloona (2 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Bamia Saloona: allergens

Bamia Saloona contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Bamia Saloona may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Bamia Saloona contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Bamia Saloona: how spicy it is

Bamia Saloona is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be left out or increased.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Bamia Saloona: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)), “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Bamia Saloona: customisation options

**Spice level** for Bamia Saloona — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

## When Bamia Saloona is available

- Bamia Saloona is on the menu every day of the week.
- Bamia Saloona is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Bamia Saloona.

## Ordering Bamia Saloona: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Bamia Saloona can be ready at the earliest 22 minutes after the order is placed (12 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Bamia Saloona for catering and large orders

Bamia Saloona can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 60
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Bamia Saloona
