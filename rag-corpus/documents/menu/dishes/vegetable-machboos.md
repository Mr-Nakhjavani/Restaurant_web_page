# Vegetable Machboos (مجبوس خضار)

Vegetable Machboos is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 48. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Vegetable Machboos at a glance

Vegetable Machboos (Arabic: مجبوس خضار) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Vegetarian section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Vegetable Machboos costs AED 48 (48 dirhams) and serves one person. Vegan machboos with chickpeas, eggplant, zucchini, carrot and potato.

- Dish: Vegetable Machboos (مجبوس خضار)
- Price: AED 48 (48 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Vegetarian (أطباق نباتية)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 600 g with 150 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 15 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: celery
- May contain traces of: mustard
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_vegetable_machboos (web page: /menu/vegetable-machboos)

## What Vegetable Machboos is

All the warmth of machboos without the meat: chickpeas, potato, carrot, zucchini and eggplant cooked with onion, tomato, garlic, ginger and loomi, then basmati rice steamed in our vegetable stock with bezar and whole spices. Cooked with sunflower oil rather than ghee, so it is vegan. Served with dakkus.

How Vegetable Machboos is cooked: Vegetables braised; rice steamed in vegetable stock. Style: Emirati machboos, vegan.

Guests also ask for Vegetable Machboos as “vegetable majboos”, “veg machboos”, “vegan machboos”, “vegetable rice” or “vegetable kabsa”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Vegetable Machboos: price and portion

Vegetable Machboos costs AED 48 (48 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 48 is AED 2.29.

One order of Vegetable Machboos is about 600 g with 150 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Vegetable Machboos:

- Add hashu (contains ghee and raisins — no longer vegan): +AED 5 (total AED 53)
- Extra dakkus: +AED 4 (total AED 52)

## Vegetable Machboos: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Vegetable Machboos (5 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Vegetable Machboos (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Vegetable Machboos: allergens

Vegetable Machboos contains celery as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Celery: from the vegetable stock

Vegetable Machboos may contain traces of mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Vegetable Machboos contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Vegetable Machboos

- Celery cannot be removed from Vegetable Machboos; it is part of the recipe.

## Vegetable Machboos: dietary suitability

Vegetable Machboos is vegan (no animal products). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Vegetable Machboos is vegan.
- Vegetarian: Vegetable Machboos is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Vegetable Machboos is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Vegetable Machboos has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).
- Dairy: Vegetable Machboos has no dairy ingredients.
- Eggs: Vegetable Machboos has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Vegetable Machboos has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Vegetable Machboos has no sesame ingredients.

- Halal: Vegetable Machboos is halal.

- Choosing “Add hashu (contains ghee and raisins — no longer vegan)” makes Vegetable Machboos vegetarian.

## Vegetable Machboos: how spicy it is

Vegetable Machboos is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Mild; the rice is cooked in spiced stock. Add dakkus for heat.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

## Ordering Vegetable Machboos: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Vegetable Machboos can be ready at the earliest 25 minutes after the order is placed (15 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Vegetable Machboos for catering and large orders
