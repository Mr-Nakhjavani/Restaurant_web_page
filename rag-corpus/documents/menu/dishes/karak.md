# Karak Chai (شاي كرك)

Karak Chai is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 12. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Karak Chai at a glance

Karak Chai (Arabic: شاي كرك) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Gahwa & Hot Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Karak Chai costs AED 12 (12 dirhams) and serves one person. Strong black tea simmered with evaporated milk, cardamom and saffron.

- Dish: Karak Chai (شاي كرك)
- Price: AED 12 (12 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Gahwa & Hot Drinks (القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 200 ml glass or takeaway cup; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 5 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever we are open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and delivery
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Karak Chai is one of Bayt Al Ghaf's signature dishes
- Menu code: mi_karak (web page: /menu/karak)

## What Karak Chai is

The UAE's everyday ritual: strong black tea boiled with evaporated milk, sugar, crushed cardamom and a few threads of saffron until rich and caramel-coloured.

How Karak Chai is cooked: Simmered. Style: Gulf karak.

Guests also ask for Karak Chai as “karak”, “chai karak”, “karak tea”, “masala tea”, “milk tea” or “chai”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Karak Chai: price and portion

Karak Chai costs AED 12 (12 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 12 is AED 0.57.

One order of Karak Chai is 200 ml glass or takeaway cup, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Karak Chai:

- Camel milk: +AED 4 (total AED 16)

## Karak Chai: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Karak Chai as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Black tea: 6 g
- Evaporated milk: 60 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Sugar: 15 g (can be left out on request)
- Green cardamom: 0.5 g
- Saffron: 0.02 g

Karak Chai is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Karak Chai: allergens

Karak Chai contains milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the evaporated milk

Karak Chai has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Karak Chai contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Karak Chai

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Karak Chai; it is part of the recipe.

## Karak Chai: dietary suitability

Karak Chai is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegetarian: Karak Chai is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Karak Chai is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Karak Chai has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).

- Eggs: Karak Chai has no egg ingredients.

## Karak Chai: how spicy it is

Karak Chai is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Karak Chai is available

- Karak Chai is on the menu every day of the week.
- Karak Chai is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Karak Chai.

## Ordering Karak Chai: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): no

Ordered for pickup on its own, Karak Chai can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (5 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Karak Chai for catering and large orders

Karak Chai can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 100
- Catering karak is supplied in flasks of 8 cups; each unit counts as one cup.
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Karak Chai

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Karak Chai with Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) and Luqaimat (AED 32).
