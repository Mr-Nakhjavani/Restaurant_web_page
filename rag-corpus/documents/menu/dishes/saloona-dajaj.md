# Saloonat Dajaj (صالونة دجاج)

Saloonat Dajaj is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 52. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Saloonat Dajaj at a glance

Saloonat Dajaj (Arabic: صالونة دجاج) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Saloonat Dajaj costs AED 52 (52 dirhams) and serves one person. Chicken and vegetable stew with loomi and bezar, served with white rice.

- Dish: Saloonat Dajaj (صالونة دجاج)
- Price: AED 52 (52 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Traditional Emirati (أطباق تقليدية)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 550 g: 250 g bone-in chicken with vegetables, plus 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 15 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities

## What Saloonat Dajaj is

The everyday Emirati stew: bone-in chicken simmered with potato, carrot, zucchini and tomato, seasoned with ginger, garlic, loomi and bezar. Served with steamed basmati rice.

How Saloonat Dajaj is cooked: Simmered for 45 minutes. Style: Home-style Emirati stew.

Guests also ask for Saloonat Dajaj as “chicken saloona”, “saloona”, “salona”, “chicken stew” or “chicken curry”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Saloonat Dajaj: price and portion

Saloonat Dajaj costs AED 52 (52 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 52 is AED 2.48.

One order of Saloonat Dajaj is about 550 g: 250 g bone-in chicken with vegetables, plus 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Saloonat Dajaj.

Saloonat Dajaj is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Saloonat Dajaj: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Saloonat Dajaj (3 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Saloonat Dajaj: allergens

Saloonat Dajaj contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Saloonat Dajaj may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Saloonat Dajaj contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Saloonat Dajaj: how spicy it is

Saloonat Dajaj is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be reduced to none or increased to hot.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Saloonat Dajaj: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)), “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Saloonat Dajaj: customisation options

**Spice level** for Saloonat Dajaj — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

**Served with** for Saloonat Dajaj — choose one (required):

- White basmati rice — standard choice: no extra charge (option code side_rice)
- Regag bread instead of rice: no extra charge; adds gluten (wheat) (option code side_regag)

## When Saloonat Dajaj is available

- Saloonat Dajaj is on the menu every day of the week.

## Ordering Saloonat Dajaj: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Saloonat Dajaj can be ready at the earliest 25 minutes after the order is placed (15 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Saloonat Dajaj for catering and large orders

Saloonat Dajaj can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 120
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).
