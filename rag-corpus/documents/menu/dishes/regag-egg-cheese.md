# Regag with Egg & Cheese (رقاق بالبيض والجبن)

Regag with Egg & Cheese is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 28. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese at a glance

Regag with Egg & Cheese (Arabic: رقاق بالبيض والجبن) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Regag with Egg & Cheese costs AED 28 (28 dirhams) and serves one person. Paper-thin crisp regag folded over egg and melted cheese.

- Dish: Regag with Egg & Cheese (رقاق بالبيض والجبن)
- Price: AED 28 (28 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 2 folded regag (about 30 cm each) with 1 egg and 30 g cheese; serves one person; 2 pieces
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 10 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30). Best eaten immediately; it softens within about 15 minutes if taken away
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_regag_egg_cheese (web page: /menu/regag-egg-cheese)

## What Regag with Egg & Cheese is

Our bakers spread regag batter on a hot iron plate in front of you, crack an egg over it, add melted cheese and fold it into a crisp, wafer-thin parcel. Add a smear of mehyawa for the true Gulf-coast taste.

How Regag with Egg & Cheese is cooked: Baked to order on a flat iron plate (tawa). Style: Emirati street-breakfast, made in front of guests at the bread station.

Guests also ask for Regag with Egg & Cheese as “regag”, “rgag”, “raqaq”, “regag bread” or “egg cheese bread”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese: price and portion

Regag with Egg & Cheese costs AED 28 (28 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 28 is AED 1.33.

One order of Regag with Egg & Cheese is 2 folded regag (about 30 cm each) with 1 egg and 30 g cheese, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Regag with Egg & Cheese:

- Add mehyawa (fermented fish sauce): +AED 4 (total AED 32)
- Add date syrup drizzle: +AED 3 (total AED 31)

## Regag with Egg & Cheese: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Regag with Egg & Cheese as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Regag bread (house-made): 2 sheets (about 60 g) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggs: 1 egg (can be left out on request; contains eggs)
- Processed cheese: 30 g (can be left out on request; contains milk (dairy))
- Ghee (clarified butter): 5 g (contains milk (dairy))

Regag with Egg & Cheese is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese: allergens

Regag with Egg & Cheese contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Milk (dairy): from the processed cheese and the ghee

Regag with Egg & Cheese has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Regag with Egg & Cheese contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Regag with Egg & Cheese

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Regag with Egg & Cheese; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs can be left out of Regag with Egg & Cheese by choosing “No egg”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Regag with Egg & Cheese; it is part of the recipe.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese: how spicy it is

Regag with Egg & Cheese is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy. Mehyawa can be added for a savoury tang.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## Ordering Regag with Egg & Cheese: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Regag with Egg & Cheese can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (10 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Regag with Egg & Cheese is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Regag with Egg & Cheese for catering and large orders
