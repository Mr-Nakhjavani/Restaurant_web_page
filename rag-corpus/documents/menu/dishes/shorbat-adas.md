# Shorbat Adas (شوربة عدس)

Shorbat Adas is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 26. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Shorbat Adas at a glance

Shorbat Adas (Arabic: شوربة عدس) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Shorbat Adas costs AED 26 (26 dirhams) and serves one person. Velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lime and sourdough croutons.

- Dish: Shorbat Adas (شوربة عدس)
- Price: AED 26 (26 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Starters & Mezze (المقبلات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 350 ml bowl with 15 g croutons; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 8 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and celery
- May contain traces of: mustard
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_shorbat_adas (web page: /menu/shorbat-adas)

## What Shorbat Adas is

Red lentils simmered with onion, carrot, celery and garlic in our vegetable stock, blended until velvety and seasoned with cumin and turmeric. Finished with olive oil, a wedge of lime and crunchy sourdough croutons.

How Shorbat Adas is cooked: Simmered and blended. Style: Gulf home-style soup; an iftar favourite.

Guests also ask for Shorbat Adas as “lentil soup”, “adas”, “shorba” or “soup”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Shorbat Adas: price and portion

Shorbat Adas costs AED 26 (26 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 26 is AED 1.24.

One order of Shorbat Adas is 350 ml bowl with 15 g croutons, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Shorbat Adas.

## Shorbat Adas: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Shorbat Adas as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Red lentils: 70 g
- Onion: 40 g
- Carrot: 30 g
- Celery: 15 g (contains celery)
- Garlic: 5 g
- Cumin: 1 g
- Turmeric: 0.5 g
- Vegetable stock (house-made): 300 ml (contains celery)
- Olive oil: 10 ml
- Lime: 1 wedge
- Salt: 2 g
- Sourdough croutons: 15 g (can be left out on request; contains gluten (wheat))

Shorbat Adas is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Shorbat Adas: allergens

Shorbat Adas contains gluten (wheat) and celery as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the sourdough croutons
- Celery: from the celery and the vegetable stock

Shorbat Adas may contain traces of mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Shorbat Adas contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Shorbat Adas

- Gluten (wheat) can be left out of Shorbat Adas by choosing “No croutons”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Celery cannot be removed from Shorbat Adas; it is part of the recipe.

## Shorbat Adas: dietary suitability

Shorbat Adas is vegan (no animal products). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Shorbat Adas is vegan.
- Vegetarian: Shorbat Adas is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Shorbat Adas is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Shorbat Adas contains gluten.
- Dairy: Shorbat Adas has no dairy ingredients.
- Eggs: Shorbat Adas has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Shorbat Adas has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Shorbat Adas has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Shorbat Adas has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Shorbat Adas is halal.

## Shorbat Adas: how spicy it is

Shorbat Adas is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy; chilli flakes can be served on the side.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

## Shorbat Adas: customisation options

**Croutons** for Shorbat Adas — choose one (required):

- With croutons — standard choice: no extra charge (option code croutons_yes)
- No croutons: no extra charge; removes gluten (wheat) from the recipe (option code croutons_no)

## When Shorbat Adas is available

- Shorbat Adas is on the menu every day of the week.
- Shorbat Adas is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Shorbat Adas.

## Ordering Shorbat Adas: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Shorbat Adas can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (8 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Shorbat Adas for catering and large orders

Shorbat Adas can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 80
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).
