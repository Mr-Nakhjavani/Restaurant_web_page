# Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (ريش غنم)

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 118. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## What Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) is

French-trimmed lamb chops marinated overnight in laban, garlic and bezar, grilled over charcoal and brushed with the chef's Sauce No. 7. Served with charred tomato, grilled onion and toum.

How Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) is cooked: Marinated 12–18 hours; grilled over charcoal. Style: Gulf grill with a contemporary glaze.

Guests also ask for Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) as “lamb chops”, “rish”, “lamb ribs”, “grilled lamb” or “lamb cutlets”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): price and portion

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) costs AED 118 (118 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 118 is AED 5.62.

None of the customisation options change the price of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops).

These are the ingredients in one portion of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

## Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Laban Marinade** (تتبيلة اللبن) is used as the marinade for Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops). Yogurt marinade that tenderises our lamb chops overnight. Laban Marinade is made from natural yogurt, garlic, onion, lemon, olive oil, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Laban Marinade contains milk (dairy) and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)** (صلصة سبعة) is brushed as a glaze on Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (20 ml). The chef's signature glaze, named for its seven key flavours: tamarind, date, soy, sesame, garlic, ginger and bezar. It contains soy, wheat (gluten, from the soy sauce) and sesame. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is made from tamarind, date syrup (dibs), soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, garlic, ginger, loomi (dried black lime), sugar and Bezar Spice Blend. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Toum Garlic Sauce** (ثومية) is served on the side with Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (40 ml). Fluffy whipped garlic sauce made without egg or dairy. Toum Garlic Sauce is made from garlic, sunflower oil, lemon and salt. Toum Garlic Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): allergens

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) contains gluten (wheat), soy, milk (dairy) and sesame as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains soy sauce
- Soy: from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains soy sauce
- Milk (dairy): from the Laban Marinade, which contains natural yogurt
- Sesame: from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains toasted sesame oil

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) may contain traces of crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) contains no molluscs, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops)

- Gluten (wheat) can be left out of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) by choosing “No glaze”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Soy can be left out of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) by choosing “No glaze”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops); it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame can be left out of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) by choosing “No glaze”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): how spicy it is

Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Ordering without the Sauce No. 7 glaze reduces the warmth.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? No.

Options that change the spice of Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): “No glaze” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)).
