## What Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) is

A winter tradition from Emirati grandmothers' kitchens: orange pumpkin (bobar) cooked down with toasted flour, sugar, saffron and cardamom, stirred with ghee until glossy and finished with a few drops of rosewater. Served warm.

How Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) is cooked: Stirred on the stove for 40 minutes. Style: Traditional Emirati winter sweet.

Guests also ask for Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) as “aseeda”, “asida”, “aseedat bobar”, “pumpkin asida”, “pumpkin pudding”, “winter dessert” or “seasonal dessert”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Pumpkin: 200 g
- Wheat flour: 40 g, toasted (contains gluten (wheat))
- Sugar: 25 g

- Green cardamom: 1 g
- Rosewater: 3 ml

Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): allergens

Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.
