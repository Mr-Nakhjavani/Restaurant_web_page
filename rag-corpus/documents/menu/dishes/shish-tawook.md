# Saffron Shish Tawook (شيش طاووق بالزعفران)

Saffron Shish Tawook is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 68. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Saffron Shish Tawook at a glance

Saffron Shish Tawook (Arabic: شيش طاووق بالزعفران) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Saffron Shish Tawook costs AED 68 (68 dirhams) and serves one person. Chicken skewers in saffron-yogurt marinade with grilled peppers and toum.

- Dish: Saffron Shish Tawook (شيش طاووق بالزعفران)
- Price: AED 68 (68 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 2 skewers (about 250 g chicken) with 120 g (dry) rice or 1 regag; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 18 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy and sesame
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_shish_tawook (web page: /menu/shish-tawook)

## What Saffron Shish Tawook is

Cubes of chicken breast marinated for six hours in yogurt, saffron, lemon and paprika, grilled on skewers with peppers and onion. Served with toum and your choice of rice or regag.

How Saffron Shish Tawook is cooked: Marinated 6 hours; grilled over charcoal. Style: Levantine grill, Gulf style.

Guests also ask for Saffron Shish Tawook as “shish tawook”, “tawook”, “chicken skewers”, “chicken kebab” or “chicken tikka”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Saffron Shish Tawook: price and portion

Saffron Shish Tawook costs AED 68 (68 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 68 is AED 3.24.

One order of Saffron Shish Tawook is 2 skewers (about 250 g chicken) with 120 g (dry) rice or 1 regag, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Saffron Shish Tawook:

- Extra toum: +AED 4 (total AED 72)
- Extra dakkus: +AED 4 (total AED 72)

## Saffron Shish Tawook: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Saffron Shish Tawook as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Chicken breast: 250 g
- Bell pepper and onion (grilled): 60 g
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed
- Salt: 2 g

Saffron Shish Tawook is also made with Saffron Tawook Marinade and Toum Garlic Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Saffron Shish Tawook: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Saffron Tawook Marinade** (تتبيلة الطاووق بالزعفران) is used as the marinade for Saffron Shish Tawook. Yogurt, saffron, lemon and paprika marinade for chicken tawook. Saffron Tawook Marinade is made from natural yogurt, saffron, garlic, lemon, paprika, tomato paste, olive oil and salt. Saffron Tawook Marinade contains milk (dairy).

**Toum Garlic Sauce** (ثومية) is served on the side with Saffron Shish Tawook (40 ml). Fluffy whipped garlic sauce made without egg or dairy. Toum Garlic Sauce is made from garlic, sunflower oil, lemon and salt. Toum Garlic Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Saffron Shish Tawook: allergens

Saffron Shish Tawook contains milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the Saffron Tawook Marinade, which contains natural yogurt

Saffron Shish Tawook may contain traces of gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy and sesame. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).

Saffron Shish Tawook contains no molluscs, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Saffron Shish Tawook

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Saffron Shish Tawook; it is part of the recipe.

## Saffron Shish Tawook: dietary suitability

Saffron Shish Tawook is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Saffron Shish Tawook is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Saffron Shish Tawook is not vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Saffron Shish Tawook is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Saffron Shish Tawook has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).
- Dairy: Saffron Shish Tawook contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Saffron Shish Tawook has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Saffron Shish Tawook has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Saffron Shish Tawook has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Saffron Shish Tawook has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Saffron Shish Tawook is halal.

## Saffron Shish Tawook: how spicy it is

Saffron Shish Tawook is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. The marinade is pre-made and mildly spiced; add dakkus for heat.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

## Ordering Saffron Shish Tawook: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Saffron Shish Tawook can be ready at the earliest 28 minutes after the order is placed (18 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.
