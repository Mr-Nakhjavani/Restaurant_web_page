# Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (صالونة قرع وحمص)

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 44. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona at a glance

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (Arabic: صالونة قرع وحمص) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Vegetarian section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona costs AED 44 (44 dirhams) and serves one person. Sweet pumpkin and chickpeas in a turmeric-ginger saloona; with rice.

- Dish: Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (صالونة قرع وحمص)
- Price: AED 44 (44 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Vegetarian (أطباق نباتية)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 500 g with 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 12 minutes
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Contains allergens: celery
- May contain traces of: mustard
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_qara_saloona (web page: /menu/qara-saloona)

## What Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is

Chunks of orange pumpkin and chickpeas simmered with onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, turmeric, loomi and bezar in vegetable stock, finished with coriander. Served with white basmati rice.

How Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is cooked: Simmered. Style: Gulf home-style stew.

Guests also ask for Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona as “pumpkin stew”, “pumpkin curry”, “qara” or “chickpea stew”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: price and portion

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona costs AED 44 (44 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 44 is AED 2.10.

One order of Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is about 500 g with 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Pumpkin: 200 g
- Chickpeas: 80 g
- Onion: 40 g
- Tomato: 60 g
- Ginger: 4 g
- Garlic: 5 g
- Turmeric: 1 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 1/2 (pierced)
- Coriander leaves: 4 g
- Sunflower oil: 15 ml
- Vegetable stock (house-made): 200 ml (contains celery)
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed
- Salt: 2 g

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (2 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: allergens

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona contains celery as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Celery: from the vegetable stock

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona may contain traces of mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona

- Celery cannot be removed from Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona; it is part of the recipe.

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: how spicy it is

Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be left out or increased.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)), “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: customisation options

**Spice level** for Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)
