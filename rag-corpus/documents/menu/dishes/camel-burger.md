# Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (برجر لحم الإبل)

Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 72. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli at a glance

Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (Arabic: برجر لحم الإبل) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli costs AED 72 (72 dirhams) and serves one person. Camel patty, Sauce No. 7, cheese and loomi aioli in brioche, with spiced wedges.

- Dish: Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (برجر لحم الإبل)
- Price: AED 72 (72 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 180 g patty in a brioche bun with 150 g potato wedges; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 15 minutes

- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs, soy, milk (dairy), mustard and sesame

- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes

## What Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli is

Our contemporary signature: a 180 g camel patty brushed with Sauce No. 7, topped with melted cheese, caramelised onion, lettuce, tomato and loomi aioli in a sesame brioche bun. Served with bezar-spiced potato wedges.

How Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli is cooked: Patty chargrilled; wedges fried in the general fryer. Style: Contemporary Emirati.

Guests also ask for Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli as “camel burger”, “burger”, “camel” or “camelburger”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

These are the ingredients in one portion of Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

## Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)** (صلصة سبعة) is brushed as a glaze on Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (15 ml). The chef's signature glaze, named for its seven key flavours: tamarind, date, soy, sesame, garlic, ginger and bezar. It contains soy, wheat (gluten, from the soy sauce) and sesame. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is made from tamarind, date syrup (dibs), soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, garlic, ginger, loomi (dried black lime), sugar and Bezar Spice Blend. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Loomi Aioli** (أيولي باللومي) is cooked into Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (25 ml). House-made egg-yolk aioli seasoned with ground dried black lime. Loomi Aioli is made from egg yolk, rapeseed (canola) oil, loomi (dried black lime), garlic, lemon, dijon mustard and salt. Loomi Aioli contains eggs and mustard.

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (on the wedges). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli: allergens

Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli contains gluten (wheat), eggs, soy, milk (dairy), mustard and sesame as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the brioche bun and the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains soy sauce
- Eggs: from the brioche bun and the Loomi Aioli, which contains egg yolk
- Soy: from the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains soy sauce
- Milk (dairy): from the brioche bun and the processed cheese
- Mustard: from the Loomi Aioli, which contains dijon mustard
- Sesame: from the brioche bun and the Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), which contains toasted sesame oil

Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli may contain traces of crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish and celery. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli contains no molluscs, peanuts, tree nuts, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs cannot be removed from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli; it is part of the recipe.
- Soy can be left out of Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli by choosing “No Sauce No. 7”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli; it is part of the recipe.
- Mustard cannot be removed from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli; it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame cannot be removed from Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli; it is part of the recipe.
