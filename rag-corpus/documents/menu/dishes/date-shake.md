# Date & Cardamom Shake (ميلك شيك التمر بالهيل)

Date & Cardamom Shake is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 28. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Date & Cardamom Shake at a glance

Date & Cardamom Shake (Arabic: ميلك شيك التمر بالهيل) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Cold Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Date & Cardamom Shake costs AED 28 (28 dirhams) and serves one person. Khalas dates blended with cold milk, date syrup and cardamom.

- Dish: Date & Cardamom Shake (ميلك شيك التمر بالهيل)
- Price: AED 28 (28 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Cold Drinks (المشروبات الباردة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 400 ml; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 4 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: tree nuts
- When: Available whenever we are open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and delivery
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_date_shake (web page: /menu/date-shake)

## What Date & Cardamom Shake is

A naturally sweet shake of Khalas dates, cold milk, a spoon of date syrup and a pinch of cardamom.

How Date & Cardamom Shake is cooked: Blended to order. Style: Contemporary Emirati café drink.

Guests also ask for Date & Cardamom Shake as “date shake”, “date milkshake”, “date smoothie” or “milkshake”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Date & Cardamom Shake: price and portion

Date & Cardamom Shake costs AED 28 (28 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 28 is AED 1.33.

One order of Date & Cardamom Shake is 400 ml, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Date & Cardamom Shake:

- Camel milk: +AED 4 (total AED 32)
- Pistachio crumb: +AED 4 (total AED 32)

## Date & Cardamom Shake: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Date & Cardamom Shake as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Khalas dates: 60 g (pitted)
- Full-cream milk: 250 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Date syrup (dibs): 15 ml
- Green cardamom: 0.3 g

Date & Cardamom Shake is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Date & Cardamom Shake: allergens

Date & Cardamom Shake contains milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the full-cream milk

Date & Cardamom Shake may contain traces of tree nuts. Reasons:

- Blended in the drinks blender, which is also used for drinks with pistachio crumb.

Date & Cardamom Shake contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Date & Cardamom Shake

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Date & Cardamom Shake; it is part of the recipe.

## When Date & Cardamom Shake is available

- Date & Cardamom Shake is on the menu every day of the week.
- Date & Cardamom Shake is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Date & Cardamom Shake.

## Ordering Date & Cardamom Shake: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): no

Ordered for pickup on its own, Date & Cardamom Shake can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (4 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Date & Cardamom Shake for catering and large orders

Date & Cardamom Shake is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Date & Cardamom Shake
