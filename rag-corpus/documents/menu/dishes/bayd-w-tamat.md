# Bayd w Tamat (بيض وطماط)

Bayd w Tamat is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 30. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Bayd w Tamat at a glance

Bayd w Tamat (Arabic: بيض وطماط) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Bayd w Tamat costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) and serves one person. Soft eggs scrambled with tomato, onion, green chilli and bezar, with regag.

- Dish: Bayd w Tamat (بيض وطماط)
- Price: AED 30 (30 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 320 g with 1 regag on the side; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 12 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and eggs
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- When: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30)
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_bayd_tamat (web page: /menu/bayd-w-tamat)

## What Bayd w Tamat is

The Emirati home breakfast: onions and tomatoes cooked down with green chilli and a pinch of bezar, with three eggs stirred through until just set. Served with crisp regag on the side.

How Bayd w Tamat is cooked: Pan-cooked to order. Style: Home-style Emirati breakfast.

Guests also ask for Bayd w Tamat as “eggs and tomato”, “bayd tamat”, “baid tamat”, “scrambled eggs tomato” or “shakshuka”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Bayd w Tamat: price and portion

Bayd w Tamat costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 30 is AED 1.43.

One order of Bayd w Tamat is about 320 g with 1 regag on the side, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Bayd w Tamat.

## Bayd w Tamat: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Bayd w Tamat as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Eggs: 3 eggs (contains eggs)
- Tomato: 150 g
- Onion: 50 g
- Green chilli: 5 g (can be left out on request)
- Turmeric: 0.5 g
- Sunflower oil: 15 ml
- Salt: 2 g
- Regag bread (house-made): 1 sheet (about 30 g), on the side (can be left out on request; contains gluten (wheat))

Bayd w Tamat is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Bayd w Tamat: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Bayd w Tamat (1 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Bayd w Tamat: allergens

Bayd w Tamat contains gluten (wheat) and eggs as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread
- Eggs: from the eggs

Bayd w Tamat may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Bayd w Tamat contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Bayd w Tamat

- Gluten (wheat) can be left out of Bayd w Tamat by choosing “No bread”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Eggs cannot be removed from Bayd w Tamat; it is part of the recipe.

## Bayd w Tamat: dietary suitability

Bayd w Tamat is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Bayd w Tamat is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Bayd w Tamat is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Bayd w Tamat is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Bayd w Tamat contains gluten.
- Dairy: Bayd w Tamat has no dairy ingredients.
- Eggs: Bayd w Tamat contains egg.
- Nuts: Bayd w Tamat has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Bayd w Tamat has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Bayd w Tamat has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Bayd w Tamat is halal.

## Bayd w Tamat: how spicy it is

Bayd w Tamat is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Green chilli can be left out entirely or increased; the bezar adds warmth but little heat.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Bayd w Tamat: “No chilli” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)), “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Bayd w Tamat: customisation options

**Spice level** for Bayd w Tamat — choose one (required):

- No chilli: no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code spice_none)
- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

**Bread** for Bayd w Tamat — choose one (required):

- With regag — standard choice: no extra charge (option code bread_regag)
- No bread: no extra charge; removes gluten (wheat) from the recipe (option code bread_none)

## When Bayd w Tamat is available

- Bayd w Tamat is on the menu every day of the week.
- Bayd w Tamat is served from 08:00 (8 am) until 11:30 (11:30 am).
- Bayd w Tamat is part of the Emirati breakfast menu, served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am).
- Bayd w Tamat is not served during Ramadan.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Bayd w Tamat.

## Ordering Bayd w Tamat: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Bayd w Tamat can be ready at the earliest 22 minutes after the order is placed (12 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Bayd w Tamat is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Bayd w Tamat for catering and large orders

Bayd w Tamat is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Bayd w Tamat

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Bayd w Tamat with Karak Chai (AED 12) and Sulaimani (AED 10).
