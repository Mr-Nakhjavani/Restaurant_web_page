# Luqaimat (لقيمات)

Luqaimat is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 32. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Luqaimat at a glance

Luqaimat (Arabic: لقيمات) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Desserts section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Luqaimat costs AED 32 (32 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Crisp saffron-cardamom dumplings, warm dibs and toasted sesame.

- Dish: Luqaimat (لقيمات)
- Price: AED 32 (32 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Desserts (الحلويات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 10 pieces (about 200 g) with 30 ml dibs; serves 2 people; 10 pieces
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 10 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), milk (dairy) and sesame
- May contain traces of: eggs
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open. For takeaway or delivery we recommend dibs on the side
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Luqaimat is one of Bayt Al Ghaf's signature dishes
- Menu code: mi_luqaimat (web page: /menu/luqaimat)

## What Luqaimat is

The Emirates' favourite sweet: a light yeasted batter flavoured with saffron and cardamom, dropped by hand into hot oil, fried until crisp outside and fluffy inside, then drenched in warm date syrup and scattered with toasted sesame.

How Luqaimat is cooked: Deep-fried to order in the general fryer. Style: Traditional Emirati sweet.

Guests also ask for Luqaimat as “luqaimat”, “lugaimat”, “luqeimat”, “lokma”, “dumplings”, “sweet dumplings” or “date syrup balls”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Luqaimat: price and portion

Luqaimat costs AED 32 (32 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 32 is AED 1.52.

One order of Luqaimat is 10 pieces (about 200 g) with 30 ml dibs, and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 16 per person.

Options that change the price of Luqaimat:

- 20 pieces (sharing): +AED 28 (total AED 60)

## Luqaimat: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Luqaimat as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Wheat flour: 90 g (contains gluten (wheat))
- Dried yeast: 2 g
- Sugar: 10 g
- Milk powder: 6 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Saffron: 0.05 g
- Green cardamom: 0.5 g
- Sunflower oil: for frying
- Sesame seeds: 3 g (can be left out on request; contains sesame)

Luqaimat is also made with Dibs (Date Syrup); their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Luqaimat: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is spooned on top of Luqaimat (30 ml). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Luqaimat: allergens

Luqaimat contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy) and sesame as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the milk powder
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds

Luqaimat may contain traces of eggs. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).

Luqaimat contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Luqaimat

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Luqaimat; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Luqaimat; it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame can be left out of Luqaimat by choosing “No sesame seeds”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

Luqaimat is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Gluten: Luqaimat contains gluten.
- Dairy: Luqaimat contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Luqaimat has no egg ingredients.

- Sesame: Luqaimat contains sesame.

- Halal: Luqaimat is halal.

Luqaimat is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy.

## When Luqaimat is available

- Luqaimat is on the menu every day of the week.
- Luqaimat is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Luqaimat.

## Ordering Luqaimat: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Luqaimat can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (10 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Luqaimat for catering and large orders

Luqaimat can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 120
- Catering luqaimat are packed with dibs on the side; quantity is counted in portions of 10 pieces.
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Luqaimat

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Luqaimat with Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) and Karak Chai (AED 12).
