# Khanfaroosh (خنفروش)

Khanfaroosh is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 30. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Khanfaroosh at a glance

Khanfaroosh (Arabic: خنفروش) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Desserts section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Khanfaroosh costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) and serves 2 people. Little saffron and rosewater cakes, crisp-edged and fragrant.

- Dish: Khanfaroosh (خنفروش)
- Price: AED 30 (30 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Desserts (الحلويات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 6 cakes (about 180 g) with 20 ml syrup; serves 2 people; 6 pieces
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 12 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and eggs
- May contain traces of: milk (dairy) and sesame
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_khanfaroosh (web page: /menu/khanfaroosh)

## What Khanfaroosh is

An heirloom Emirati sweet: a batter of rice flour and wheat flour with eggs, saffron, cardamom and rosewater, fried in small rounds until the edges crisp and the centres stay soft. Served with saffron cardamom syrup.

How Khanfaroosh is cooked: Shallow-fried in the general fryer. Style: Traditional Emirati sweet.

Guests also ask for Khanfaroosh as “khanfaroosh”, “khanfarush”, “saffron cakes” or “rice flour cakes”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Khanfaroosh: price and portion

Khanfaroosh costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 30 is AED 1.43.

One order of Khanfaroosh is 6 cakes (about 180 g) with 20 ml syrup, and serves 2 people. That works out at about AED 15 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Khanfaroosh.

## Khanfaroosh: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Khanfaroosh as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Rice flour: 60 g
- Wheat flour: 30 g (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggs: 1 egg (contains eggs)
- Sugar: 30 g
- Saffron: 0.05 g
- Green cardamom: 0.5 g
- Rosewater: 5 ml
- Dried yeast: 1 g
- Sunflower oil: for frying

Khanfaroosh is also made with Saffron Cardamom Syrup; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Khanfaroosh: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Saffron Cardamom Syrup** (شيرة الزعفران والهيل) is served on the side with Khanfaroosh (20 ml). Light sugar syrup infused with saffron, cardamom and rosewater. Saffron Cardamom Syrup is made from sugar, saffron, green cardamom, rosewater and lemon. Saffron Cardamom Syrup contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Khanfaroosh: allergens

Khanfaroosh contains gluten (wheat) and eggs as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Eggs: from the eggs

Khanfaroosh may contain traces of milk (dairy) and sesame. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).

Khanfaroosh contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Khanfaroosh

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Khanfaroosh; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs cannot be removed from Khanfaroosh; it is part of the recipe.

## Khanfaroosh: how spicy it is

Khanfaroosh is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Khanfaroosh is available

- Khanfaroosh is on the menu every day of the week.
- Khanfaroosh is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Khanfaroosh.

## Ordering Khanfaroosh: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Khanfaroosh can be ready at the earliest 22 minutes after the order is placed (12 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Khanfaroosh for catering and large orders

Khanfaroosh can be included in catering orders for groups and events.

- Maximum per day across all catering orders: 60
- Catering orders of more than 20 portions follow the catering notice periods (24 hours up to 60 portions, 48 hours up to 150 portions and 72 hours up to 250 portions) and volume discounts (5% from 51 portions and 8% from 101 portions).

## What goes well with Khanfaroosh
