# Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (مرقوقة لحم)

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 58. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday) at a glance

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (Arabic: مرقوقة لحم) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days in the Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Margooga Laham (Tuesday) costs AED 58 (58 dirhams) and serves one person. Tuesday special: lamb and vegetable stew cooked with hand-stretched dough sheets.

## What Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is

A hearty winter-in-the-desert dish: lamb, eggplant, potato and tomato simmered with loomi and bezar, then hand-stretched sheets of dough are laid into the pot to cook in the broth until silky.

How Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is cooked: Braised, then finished with dough sheets. Style: Traditional Emirati stew.

Guests also ask for Margooga Laham (Tuesday) as “margooga”, “marqooqa”, “margoogat”, “marqouq” or “lamb stew with dough”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): price and portion

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) costs AED 58 (58 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 58 is AED 2.76.

One order of Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is about 550 g: 180 g lamb, vegetables and 70 g dough, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Margooga Laham (Tuesday).

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Margooga Laham (Tuesday) as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Lamb shoulder: 180 g
- Margooga dough sheets: 70 g (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggplant (aubergine): 60 g
- Potato: 60 g
- Tomato: 80 g
- Tomato paste: 10 g
- Onion: 40 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 1 whole (pierced)
- Lamb stock (house-made): 300 ml
- Salt: 2 g

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is also made with Bezar Spice Blend; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (3 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): allergens

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) contains gluten (wheat) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the margooga dough sheets

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Margooga Laham (Tuesday)

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Margooga Laham (Tuesday); it is part of the recipe.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): how spicy it is

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Chilli can be reduced or increased.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Margooga Laham (Tuesday): “Medium” (medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)) and “Hot” (hot (spice level 3 of 3)).

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday): customisation options

**Spice level** for Margooga Laham (Tuesday) — choose one (required):

- Mild — standard choice: no extra charge (option code spice_mild)
- Medium: no extra charge; spice becomes medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) (option code spice_medium)
- Hot: no extra charge; spice becomes hot (spice level 3 of 3) (option code spice_hot)

## When Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is available

- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is cooked only on Tuesdays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Margooga Laham (Tuesday) each day: 20 portions. Once they are gone, Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Margooga Laham (Tuesday).

## Ordering Margooga Laham (Tuesday): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Margooga Laham (Tuesday) can be ready at the earliest 30 minutes after the order is placed (20 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Margooga Laham (Tuesday) for catering and large orders

Margooga Laham (Tuesday) is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 8 per order.
