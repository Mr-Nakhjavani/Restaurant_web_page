# Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (آيس كريم حليب النوق بالتمر)

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 36. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream at a glance

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (Arabic: آيس كريم حليب النوق بالتمر) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Desserts section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream costs AED 36 (36 dirhams) and serves one person. Three scoops of house-churned camel-milk and date ice cream with pistachio crumb.

- Dish: Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (آيس كريم حليب النوق بالتمر)
- Price: AED 36 (36 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Desserts (الحلويات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 3 scoops (about 180 g) with 10 g pistachio crumb; serves one person; 3 pieces
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 4 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: eggs, milk (dairy) and tree nuts
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever the kitchen is open. Not delivered (it would melt); takeaway is packed in an insulated cup
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_camel_milk_ice_cream (web page: /menu/camel-milk-ice-cream)

## What Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is

Churned in-house from UAE camel milk, cream and date paste, with an egg-yolk custard base. Three scoops finished with our pistachio cardamom crumb.

How Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is cooked: Custard base cooked, chilled and churned. Style: Contemporary Emirati dessert.

Guests also ask for Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream as “camel milk ice cream”, “ice cream” or “date ice cream”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: price and portion

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream costs AED 36 (36 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 36 is AED 1.71.

One order of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is 3 scoops (about 180 g) with 10 g pistachio crumb, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Camel milk: 150 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Fresh cream: 50 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Egg yolk: 20 g (contains eggs)
- Sugar: 25 g
- Date paste: 30 g

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is also made with Pistachio Cardamom Crumb; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Pistachio Cardamom Crumb** (فتات الفستق بالهيل) is spooned on top of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (10 g). Crushed pistachios with sugar and cardamom, scattered over desserts. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is made from pistachios, sugar and green cardamom. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb contains tree nuts.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: allergens

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream contains eggs, milk (dairy) and tree nuts as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Eggs: from the egg yolk
- Milk (dairy): from the camel milk and the fresh cream
- Tree nuts: from the Pistachio Cardamom Crumb, which contains pistachios

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream

- Eggs cannot be removed from Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream; it is part of the recipe.
- Tree nuts can be left out of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream by choosing “No pistachio crumb”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

**Topping** for Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream — choose one (required):

- With pistachio crumb — standard choice: no extra charge (option code crumb_yes)
- No pistachio crumb: no extra charge; removes tree nuts from the recipe (option code crumb_no)

## When Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is available

- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is on the menu every day of the week.
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is served all day, from opening at 08:00 (8 am) until the kitchen's last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- A single regular order can include up to 10 Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream.

## Ordering Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (4 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream for catering and large orders

Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream
