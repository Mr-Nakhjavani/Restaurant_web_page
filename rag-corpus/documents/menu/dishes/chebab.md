# Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (خبز جباب)

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 36. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese at a glance

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (Arabic: خبز جباب) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese costs AED 36 (36 dirhams) and serves one person. Three saffron-cardamom Emirati pancakes with warm dibs and cream cheese.

## What Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese is

Soft, yeasted Emirati pancakes scented with saffron and cardamom, cooked on a flat griddle with a touch of ghee and served with warm date syrup (dibs) and whipped cream cheese.

How Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese is cooked: Batter rested for 90 minutes, then griddled with ghee. Style: Traditional Emirati breakfast bread.

Guests also ask for Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese as “chebab”, “jabab”, “chabab”, “emirati pancakes” or “saffron pancakes”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: price and portion

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese costs AED 36 (36 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 36 is AED 1.71.

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Wheat flour: 120 g (contains gluten (wheat))
- Eggs: 1 egg (contains eggs)
- Full-cream milk: 80 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Dried yeast: 2 g
- Sugar: 15 g
- Saffron: 0.05 g
- Green cardamom: 0.5 g
- Ghee (clarified butter): 10 g (griddle) (contains milk (dairy))
- Cream cheese: 40 g (can be left out on request; contains milk (dairy))

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese is also made with Dibs (Date Syrup); their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: sauces, marinades and spice blends

## Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: allergens

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Milk (dairy): from the full-cream milk, the ghee and the cream cheese

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.
