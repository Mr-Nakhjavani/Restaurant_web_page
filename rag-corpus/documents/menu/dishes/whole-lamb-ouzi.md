# Whole Lamb Ouzi (أوزي)

Whole Lamb Ouzi is a catering dish, sold only as part of a catering order at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 1,450. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

Whole Lamb Ouzi costs AED 1,450 (1450 dirhams) and serves 10 people. Catering only. Serves 10–12: whole slow-roasted lamb on spiced rice with nuts.

## What Whole Lamb Ouzi is

The centrepiece of Emirati celebrations: a whole young lamb marinated in bezar and loomi and slow-roasted for six hours, served on 2.5 kg of spiced basmati with hashu, fried almonds and pine nuts. Comes with dakkus and a large house salad. Ordered through our catering team with at least 48 hours' notice.

How Whole Lamb Ouzi is cooked: Marinated overnight and slow-roasted for 6 hours. Style: Emirati celebration dish.

Guests also ask for Whole Lamb Ouzi as “ouzi”, “quzi”, “ghuzi”, “whole lamb”, “roast lamb” or “kharoof”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Whole Lamb Ouzi: price and portion

Whole Lamb Ouzi costs AED 1,450 (1450 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 1,450 is AED 69.05.

One order of Whole Lamb Ouzi is serves 10–12: one whole lamb (8–9 kg dressed weight) on 2.5 kg (dry) rice, and serves 10 people. That works out at about AED 145 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Whole Lamb Ouzi.

## Whole Lamb Ouzi: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is used as the marinade for Whole Lamb Ouzi. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Whole Lamb Ouzi. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Whole Lamb Ouzi (500 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Whole Lamb Ouzi. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Whole Lamb Ouzi: allergens

Whole Lamb Ouzi contains milk (dairy), tree nuts and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the ghee and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Tree nuts: from the almonds and the pine nuts
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Whole Lamb Ouzi may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Whole Lamb Ouzi contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, sesame or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Whole Lamb Ouzi

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Whole Lamb Ouzi; it is part of the recipe.
- Tree nuts can be left out of Whole Lamb Ouzi by choosing “No nut garnish”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Sulphites cannot be removed from Whole Lamb Ouzi; it is part of the recipe.
