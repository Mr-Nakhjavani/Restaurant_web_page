# Balaleet (بلاليط)

Balaleet is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 34. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Balaleet at a glance

Balaleet (Arabic: بلاليط) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Balaleet costs AED 34 (34 dirhams) and serves one person. Sweet saffron and cardamom vermicelli crowned with a thin omelette.

- Dish: Balaleet (بلاليط)
- Price: AED 34 (34 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: About 280 g — 200 g of vermicelli with a two-egg omelette; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 12 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30)
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Balaleet is one of Bayt Al Ghaf's signature dishes
- Menu code: mi_balaleet (web page: /menu/balaleet)

## What Balaleet is

The classic Emirati breakfast of sweet and savoury: fine wheat vermicelli toasted in ghee, steamed with saffron, cardamom, rosewater and sugar, and topped with a thin, lightly salted omelette.

How Balaleet is cooked: Vermicelli toasted in ghee then steamed; omelette cooked on the plancha. Style: Traditional Emirati breakfast, cooked to order.

Guests also ask for Balaleet as “balalit”, “balaleet”, “sweet vermicelli”, “vermicelli with egg” or “vermicelli omelette”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Balaleet: price and portion

Balaleet costs AED 34 (34 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 34 is AED 1.62.

One order of Balaleet is about 280 g — 200 g of vermicelli with a two-egg omelette, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Balaleet:

- Extra omelette (4 eggs): +AED 6 (total AED 40)

## Balaleet: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Balaleet as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Wheat vermicelli: 120 g (dry weight) (contains gluten (wheat))
- Sugar: 25 g
- Ghee (clarified butter): 15 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Saffron: 0.1 g
- Green cardamom: 1 g, ground
- Rosewater: 5 ml
- Eggs: 2 eggs (omelette) (can be left out on request; contains eggs)
- Sunflower oil: 5 ml (omelette pan)
- Salt: 1 g

Balaleet is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Balaleet: allergens

Balaleet contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat vermicelli
- Eggs: from the eggs
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee

Balaleet has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Balaleet contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Balaleet

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Balaleet; it is part of the recipe.
- Eggs can be left out of Balaleet by choosing “No omelette”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Balaleet; it is part of the recipe.

## Balaleet: dietary suitability

Balaleet is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Balaleet is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Balaleet is vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Balaleet is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Balaleet contains gluten.
- Dairy: Balaleet contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Balaleet contains egg.
- Nuts: Balaleet has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Balaleet has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Balaleet has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Balaleet is halal.

## Balaleet: how spicy it is

Balaleet is not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) as standard. Not spicy. Sweetness can be reduced (choose “Less sweet”).

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? No.

## When Balaleet is available

- Balaleet is on the menu every day of the week.
- Balaleet is served from 08:00 (8 am) until 11:30 (11:30 am).
- Balaleet is part of the Emirati breakfast menu, served 08:00 (8 am) to 11:30 (11:30 am).

## Ordering Balaleet: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Balaleet can be ready at the earliest 22 minutes after the order is placed (12 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Balaleet is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

## Balaleet for catering and large orders

Balaleet is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 10 per order.

## What goes well with Balaleet

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Balaleet with Karak Chai (AED 12) and Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28).
