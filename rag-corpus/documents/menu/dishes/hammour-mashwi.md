# Hammour Mashwi (هامور مشوي)

Hammour Mashwi is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 98. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Hammour Mashwi at a glance

Hammour Mashwi (Arabic: هامور مشوي) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Hammour Mashwi costs AED 98 (98 dirhams) and serves one person. Charcoal-grilled hammour fillet in bezar and lime, with rice and dakkus.

- Dish: Hammour Mashwi (هامور مشوي)
- Price: AED 98 (98 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 250 g hammour fillet (raw weight) with 120 g (dry) rice; serves one person
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Preparation time: 20 minutes
- Diet: pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat)
- Contains allergens: fish
- May contain traces of: gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), soy, celery, mustard and sesame
- When: Served from 12:00, subject to supply. Hammour is sourced outside the UAE seasonal fishing ban; when unavailable it is marked sold out
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: not available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_hammour_mashwi (web page: /menu/hammour-mashwi)

## What Hammour Mashwi is

A thick fillet of hammour, the Gulf's most prized fish, marinated in bezar, lime, turmeric and garlic and grilled over charcoal until the skin crisps. Served with white basmati rice, a lime cheek and dakkus.

How Hammour Mashwi is cooked: Marinated for 30–60 minutes and grilled over charcoal. Style: Gulf seafood grill.

Guests also ask for Hammour Mashwi as “grilled hammour”, “hamour”, “grouper”, “grilled fish” or “hammour”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Hammour Mashwi: price and portion

Hammour Mashwi costs AED 98 (98 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 98 is AED 4.67.

One order of Hammour Mashwi is 250 g hammour fillet (raw weight) with 120 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

Options that change the price of Hammour Mashwi:

- House salad instead of rice: +AED 4 (total AED 102)

## Hammour Mashwi: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Hammour Mashwi as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Hammour (orange-spotted grouper) fillet: 250 g fillet (raw weight) (contains fish)
- Basmati rice: 120 g (dry weight), steamed (can be left out on request)
- Lime: 1/2 lime
- Salt: 2 g

Hammour Mashwi is also made with Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade and Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Hammour Mashwi: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade** (تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون) is used as the marinade for Hammour Mashwi. Bright marinade for fish: lime juice, bezar, turmeric and garlic. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is made from lime, turmeric, garlic, sunflower oil, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Hammour Mashwi (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Hammour Mashwi: allergens

Hammour Mashwi contains fish as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Fish: from the hammour (orange-spotted grouper) fillet

Hammour Mashwi may contain traces of gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), soy, celery, mustard and sesame. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Fish may contain small bones.
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Hammour Mashwi contains no molluscs, eggs, peanuts, milk (dairy), tree nuts, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Hammour Mashwi

- Fish cannot be removed from Hammour Mashwi; it is part of the recipe.

## Hammour Mashwi: how spicy it is

Hammour Mashwi is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. The marinade is mild; the fish can be grilled with lime and salt only, or served with extra dakkus.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Hammour Mashwi: “Lime and salt only (not spicy)” (not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)).

- Lime and salt only (not spicy): no extra charge; spice becomes not spicy (spice level 0 of 3) (option code marinade_plain)

**Side** for Hammour Mashwi — choose one (required):

- White basmati rice — standard choice: no extra charge (option code side_rice)
- House salad instead of rice: +AED 4 (option code side_salad)

## When Hammour Mashwi is available

- Hammour Mashwi is on the menu every day of the week.
- Hammour Mashwi is served from 12:00 (noon) until the kitchen closes.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Hammour Mashwi each day: 25 portions. Once they are gone, Hammour Mashwi is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 8 Hammour Mashwi.

## Ordering Hammour Mashwi: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Hammour Mashwi can be ready at the earliest 30 minutes after the order is placed (20 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

## Hammour Mashwi for catering and large orders

Hammour Mashwi is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 8 per order.

## What goes well with Hammour Mashwi

The Bayt Al Ghaf team suggests pairing Hammour Mashwi with Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) and Limon Na'na (AED 22).
