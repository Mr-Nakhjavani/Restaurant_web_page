# Chicken Machboos (مجبوس دجاج)

Chicken Machboos is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 58. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Chicken Machboos at a glance

Chicken Machboos (Arabic: مجبوس دجاج) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Rice & Machboos section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Chicken Machboos costs AED 58 (58 dirhams) and serves one person. Half a chicken and spiced basmati cooked in loomi stock, topped with hashu.

## What Chicken Machboos is

Bone-in chicken quarters simmered with onion, tomato, garlic and loomi, then roasted until golden while the basmati cooks in the spiced chicken stock with bezar, cinnamon, cardamom and cloves. Finished with hashu and served with dakkus.

How Chicken Machboos is cooked: Simmered, then oven-roasted; rice steamed in the stock. Style: Traditional Emirati machboos.

Guests also ask for Chicken Machboos as “chicken majboos”, “chicken kabsa”, “chicken rice” or “machboos dajaj”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

Chicken Machboos costs AED 58 (58 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 58 is AED 2.76.

One order of Chicken Machboos is about 650 g: half a chicken (about 350 g raw, bone-in) and 150 g (dry) rice, and serves one person.

## Chicken Machboos: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Chicken Machboos as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Chicken (bone-in): 350 g bone-in (half chicken, raw weight)
- Basmati rice: 150 g (dry weight)
- Onion: 60 g
- Tomato: 70 g
- Garlic: 6 g

Chicken Machboos is also made with Bezar Spice Blend, Hashu Topping and Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Chicken Machboos: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Chicken Machboos (5 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Chicken Machboos (35 g). Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Chicken Machboos (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Chicken Machboos: allergens

Chicken Machboos contains milk (dairy) and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the ghee and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Chicken Machboos may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Chicken Machboos contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Chicken Machboos

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Chicken Machboos; it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Chicken Machboos by choosing “Hashu without raisins”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## Chicken Machboos: dietary suitability

Chicken Machboos is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

- Vegan: Chicken Machboos is not vegan.
- Vegetarian: Chicken Machboos is not vegetarian.
- Pescatarian: Chicken Machboos is not a pescatarian dish.
- Gluten: Chicken Machboos has no gluten-containing ingredients (the kitchen is not certified gluten-free).
- Dairy: Chicken Machboos contains dairy (milk).
- Eggs: Chicken Machboos has no egg ingredients.
- Nuts: Chicken Machboos has no nut ingredients (the kitchen is not nut-free).
- Sesame: Chicken Machboos has no sesame ingredients.
- Shellfish: Chicken Machboos has no crustaceans or molluscs.
- Halal: Chicken Machboos is halal.

## Chicken Machboos: how spicy it is

Chicken Machboos is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Machboos is mild (warm spice rather than chilli heat) and cannot be made milder: the rice is cooked in stock seasoned with bezar. The heat comes from the dakkus served on the side, which can be skipped or doubled.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.
