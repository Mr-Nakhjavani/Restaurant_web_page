## What Lamb Machboos is

The national dish of the Emirates, cooked our way: bone-in lamb shoulder braised until tender with onion, tomato, ginger, garlic and whole loomi, then basmati rice is cooked in the same spiced stock with bezar, cinnamon, cardamom and cloves. Crowned with hashu (caramelised onions and golden raisins) and served with dakkus.

How Lamb Machboos is cooked: Lamb braised for 2.5 hours; rice steamed in the braising stock. Style: Traditional Emirati machboos.

Guests also ask for Lamb Machboos as “machboos”, “majboos”, “machbous”, “makbous”, “kabsa”, “lamb rice”, “rice with lamb” or “lamb kabsa”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Lamb Machboos: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Lamb Machboos (5 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Lamb Machboos (35 g). Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Lamb Machboos (50 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Lamb Machboos: allergens

Lamb Machboos contains milk (dairy) and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the ghee and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Lamb Machboos may contain traces of celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Lamb Machboos contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, sesame or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Lamb Machboos

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Lamb Machboos; it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Lamb Machboos by choosing “Hashu without raisins”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## Lamb Machboos: how spicy it is

Lamb Machboos is mild (spice level 1 of 3) as standard. Machboos is mild (warm spice rather than chilli heat) and cannot be made milder: the rice is cooked in stock seasoned with bezar. The heat comes from the dakkus served on the side, which can be skipped or doubled.

Can the spice be reduced? No. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

A: Machboos is the Emirati national rice dish: meat (here, lamb) and basmati rice cooked together in stock spiced with bezar and dried black lime (loomi).

A: Yes, it is a bone-in piece of lamb shoulder — about 280 g raw, roughly 180 g cooked.

**Q: Can I have it without raisins?**

A: Yes. Choose “Hashu without raisins” or “No hashu”. Note that the raisins are the only source of sulphites in this dish.

**Q: Does lamb machboos contain nuts?**

A: No nut ingredients are used in lamb machboos. Our kitchen does handle nuts, so we cannot guarantee it is free from traces.

**Q: Is it spicy?**

A: It is mild — warm spice rather than chilli heat. Dakkus on the side adds heat if you want it.

**Q: How much is Lamb Machboos?**

A: Lamb Machboos costs AED 72 (72 dirhams), including VAT.

**Q: How many people does Lamb Machboos serve?**

A: Lamb Machboos serves one person (about 650 g: 280 g bone-in lamb (raw weight, about 180 g cooked) and 150 g (dry) basmati rice).

**Q: Is Lamb Machboos vegan?**

A: Lamb Machboos is not vegan; it is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry).

**Q: Does Lamb Machboos contain nuts?**

**Q: How spicy is Lamb Machboos?**
