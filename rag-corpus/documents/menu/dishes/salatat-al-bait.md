# Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (سلطة البيت)

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 30. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) and serves one person. Tomato, cucumber, jarjeer, radish and pomegranate with loomi-sumac dressing.

## What Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is

A crisp salad of local tomatoes, cucumber, jarjeer (rocket), radish and red onion with pomegranate seeds and mint, dressed with our loomi and sumac dressing.

How Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is cooked: Raw, dressed to order. Style: Fresh garden salad.

Guests also ask for Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) as “house salad”, “salad”, “arabic salad”, “jarjeer salad” or “rocket salad”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): price and portion

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) costs AED 30 (30 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 30 is AED 1.43.

One order of Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is about 250 g, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Salatat Al Bait (House Salad).

## Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (30 ml). Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): allergens

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients in its standard recipe.

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (dairy), tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## When Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) is available

## Ordering Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (6 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.
