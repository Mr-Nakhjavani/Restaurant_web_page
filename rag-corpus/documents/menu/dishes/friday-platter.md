# Friday Family Machboos Platter (صينية مجبوس الجمعة)

Friday Family Machboos Platter is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 349. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Friday Family Machboos Platter at a glance

Friday Family Machboos Platter (Arabic: صينية مجبوس الجمعة) is a weekly special, cooked only on particular days in the Family Platters & Catering section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Friday Family Machboos Platter costs AED 349 (349 dirhams) and serves 5 people. Fridays only. Serves 5: lamb shoulder and whole chicken machboos on one great tray.

## What Friday Family Machboos Platter is

Friday lunch is family time in the Emirates. Our Friday tray carries a whole slow-braised lamb shoulder and a whole roast chicken over 750 g of spiced machboos rice with hashu, with dakkus, a large house salad, five laban and twenty luqaimat. Made only on Fridays in limited numbers.

How Friday Family Machboos Platter is cooked: Lamb braised for 3 hours; chicken roasted; rice steamed in the combined stock. Style: Emirati Friday family tray.

Guests also ask for Friday Family Machboos Platter as “friday platter”, “friday family platter”, “family machboos”, “friday lunch” or “jumaa platter”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Friday Family Machboos Platter: price and portion

Friday Family Machboos Platter costs AED 349 (349 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 349 is AED 16.62.

One order of Friday Family Machboos Platter is serves 5: 1.2 kg bone-in lamb shoulder (raw), 1 whole chicken (about 1.2 kg), 750 g (dry) rice, large salad, 5 laban (300 ml), 20 luqaimat, and serves 5 people. That works out at about AED 69.80 per person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Friday Family Machboos Platter.

## Friday Family Machboos Platter: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Friday Family Machboos Platter. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Hashu Topping** (حشو) is spooned on top of Friday Family Machboos Platter. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping is made from onion, golden raisins, ghee (clarified butter), sugar, green cardamom, salt and Bezar Spice Blend. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce** (دقوس) is served on the side with Friday Family Machboos Platter (200 ml). The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made from tomato, tomato paste, garlic, green chilli, dried red chilli, lime, cumin, sunflower oil and salt. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Loomi & Sumac Dressing** (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is cooked into Friday Family Machboos Platter. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made from olive oil, lime, loomi (dried black lime), sumac, pomegranate molasses, salt and black pepper. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens.

**Dibs (Date Syrup)** (دبس) is spooned on top of Friday Family Machboos Platter (60 ml on the luqaimat). Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) is made from date syrup (dibs). Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens.

## Friday Family Machboos Platter: allergens

Friday Family Machboos Platter contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the wheat flour
- Milk (dairy): from the ghee, the laban, the milk powder and the Hashu Topping, which contains ghee
- Sesame: from the sesame seeds
- Sulphites: from the Hashu Topping, which contains golden raisins

Friday Family Machboos Platter may contain traces of eggs, celery and mustard. Reasons:

- Fried in the general fryer, which is shared with sambousa, luqaimat, khanfaroosh and potato wedges (wheat, milk, eggs and sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Friday Family Machboos Platter contains no crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Friday Family Machboos Platter

- Gluten (wheat) cannot be removed from Friday Family Machboos Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Friday Family Machboos Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Sesame cannot be removed from Friday Family Machboos Platter; it is part of the recipe.
- Sulphites can be left out of Friday Family Machboos Platter by choosing “Hashu without raisins”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

## When Friday Family Machboos Platter is available

- Friday Family Machboos Platter is cooked only on Fridays. It is not available on any other day of the week, and paying more does not change that.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter is served from 12:00 (noon) until 22:00 (10 pm).
- Friday Family Machboos Platter must be ordered at least 2 hours in advance.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter can only be ordered for pickup (takeaway) or a dine-in pre-order with a reservation.
- The kitchen makes a limited number of Friday Family Machboos Platter each day: 12 portions. Once they are gone, Friday Family Machboos Platter is sold out until the next day it is served.
- A single regular order can include up to 3 Friday Family Machboos Platter.

## Ordering Friday Family Machboos Platter: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: no, it is not delivered
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Friday Family Machboos Platter can be ready at the earliest 40 minutes after the order is placed (30 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.

Friday Family Machboos Platter is not delivered because it does not travel well; it can be dine-in, pickup (takeaway) or dine-in pre-order.

Friday Family Machboos Platter is not available in catering quantities because it is cooked to order. It can still be ordered in a regular order, up to 3 per order.
