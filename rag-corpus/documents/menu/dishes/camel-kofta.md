# Camel Kofta (كفتة لحم الإبل)

Camel Kofta is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 76. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Camel Kofta at a glance

Camel Kofta (Arabic: كفتة لحم الإبل) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Camel Kofta costs AED 76 (76 dirhams) and serves one person. Charcoal-grilled camel kofta with Al Ghaf House Red and regag.

- Dish: Camel Kofta (كفتة لحم الإبل)
- Price: AED 76 (76 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 3 skewers (about 220 g camel mince) with 1 regag; serves one person; 3 pieces
- Spice: medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)
- Preparation time: 18 minutes
- Diet: a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and tree nuts
- May contain traces of: crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy, celery, mustard and sesame
- When: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway), delivery and dine-in pre-order
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_camel_kofta (web page: /menu/camel-kofta)

## What Camel Kofta is

Minced young camel blended with onion, parsley, sumac and bezar, shaped onto skewers and grilled over charcoal. Served with our walnut-and-roasted-pepper Al Ghaf House Red, regag and sliced onion.

How Camel Kofta is cooked: Grilled over charcoal. Style: Contemporary Emirati grill.

Guests also ask for Camel Kofta as “camel kofta”, “camel kebab”, “kofta”, “kebab” or “camel”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Camel Kofta: price and portion

Camel Kofta costs AED 76 (76 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 76 is AED 3.62.

One order of Camel Kofta is 3 skewers (about 220 g camel mince) with 1 regag, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Camel Kofta.

## Camel Kofta: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Camel Kofta as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Minced camel meat: 220 g
- Onion: 40 g
- Parsley: 5 g
- Sumac: 1 g
- Salt: 2 g
- Regag bread (house-made): 1 sheet (can be left out on request; contains gluten (wheat))

Camel Kofta is also made with Bezar Spice Blend and Al Ghaf House Red; their own ingredients are listed in the next section.

## Camel Kofta: sauces, marinades and spice blends

**Bezar Spice Blend** (بزار) is cooked into Camel Kofta (3 g). The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend is made from coriander seed, cumin, fennel seed, black pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, green cardamom, dried red chilli, cloves and nutmeg. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

**Al Ghaf House Red** (صلصة الغاف الحمراء) is served on the side with Camel Kofta (50 g). One of our two house sauces: a thick roasted-pepper and walnut sauce sharpened with pomegranate molasses. It contains walnuts and wheat breadcrumbs. Al Ghaf House Red is made from roasted red pepper, walnuts, breadcrumbs, pomegranate molasses, olive oil, garlic, dried red chilli, cumin, lemon and salt. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts and may contain traces of celery and mustard.

## Camel Kofta: allergens

Camel Kofta contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Gluten (wheat): from the regag bread and the Al Ghaf House Red, which contains breadcrumbs
- Tree nuts: from the Al Ghaf House Red, which contains walnuts

Camel Kofta may contain traces of crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy, celery, mustard and sesame. Reasons:

- Cooked on the shared charcoal grill used for lamb, camel, chicken, fish and prawns; some grill items are brushed with Sauce No. 7 (soy, wheat, sesame).
- Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

Camel Kofta contains no molluscs, eggs, peanuts, milk (dairy), sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Camel Kofta

- Gluten (wheat) can be left out of Camel Kofta by choosing “Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)” and “No bread”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.
- Tree nuts can be left out of Camel Kofta by choosing “Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)”. Traces may still be present because the kitchen is shared.

Camel Kofta is a meat dish (contains halal meat or poultry). All meat and poultry at Bayt Al Ghaf are halal, and no pork, alcohol or gelatine is used.

## Camel Kofta: how spicy it is

Camel Kofta is medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3) as standard. The kofta itself is mild; the medium heat comes from House Red, which can be swapped for toum.

Can the spice be reduced? Yes. Can it be made spicier? Yes.

Options that change the spice of Camel Kofta: “Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)” (mild (spice level 1 of 3)).

## Camel Kofta: customisation options

**Sauce** for Camel Kofta — choose one (required):

- Al Ghaf House Red (contains walnuts) — standard choice: no extra charge (option code sauce_red)
- Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients): no extra charge; removes tree nuts from the recipe; spice becomes mild (spice level 1 of 3) (option code sauce_toum)

**Bread** for Camel Kofta — choose one (required):

- With regag — standard choice: no extra charge (option code bread_regag)
- No bread: no extra charge (option code bread_none)

## Ordering Camel Kofta: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): yes

Ordered for pickup on its own, Camel Kofta can be ready at the earliest 28 minutes after the order is placed (18 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.
