# Chilled Laban with Mint (لبن بارد بالنعناع)

Chilled Laban with Mint is a permanent dish on the everyday menu at Bayt Al Ghaf, priced at AED 14. This document covers its ingredients with quantities, sauces, allergens, dietary suitability, spice, customisations, availability, ordering and catering rules.

## Chilled Laban with Mint at a glance

Chilled Laban with Mint (Arabic: لبن بارد بالنعناع) is a permanent dish on the everyday menu in the Cold Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu. Chilled Laban with Mint costs AED 14 (14 dirhams) and serves one person. Cold, lightly salted cultured buttermilk with fresh and dried mint.

- Dish: Chilled Laban with Mint (لبن بارد بالنعناع)
- Price: AED 14 (14 dirhams), including 5% VAT
- Menu section: Cold Drinks (المشروبات الباردة)
- Menu type: a permanent dish on the everyday menu
- Portion: 300 ml; serves one person
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Preparation time: 2 minutes
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement
- When: Available whenever we are open
- Ways to order: dine-in, pickup (takeaway) and delivery
- Catering: available in catering quantities
- Menu code: mi_laban_barid (web page: /menu/laban-barid)

## What Chilled Laban with Mint is

Ice-cold laban whisked with a pinch of salt and fresh mint — the classic partner to machboos and harees.

How Chilled Laban with Mint is cooked: Chilled and whisked. Style: Traditional Gulf drink.

Guests also ask for Chilled Laban with Mint as “laban”, “buttermilk”, “ayran” or “yogurt drink”. These are common spellings and descriptions of the same dish.

## Chilled Laban with Mint: price and portion

Chilled Laban with Mint costs AED 14 (14 dirhams) at Bayt Al Ghaf. Menu prices include 5% VAT; the VAT contained in AED 14 is AED 0.67.

One order of Chilled Laban with Mint is 300 ml, and serves one person.

None of the customisation options change the price of Chilled Laban with Mint.

## Chilled Laban with Mint: full ingredient list with quantities

These are the ingredients in one portion of Chilled Laban with Mint as served by default at Bayt Al Ghaf:

- Laban (cultured buttermilk): 300 ml (contains milk (dairy))
- Mint: 2 g
- Salt: 1 g

Chilled Laban with Mint is not made with any separate sauce or marinade.

## Chilled Laban with Mint: allergens

Chilled Laban with Mint contains milk (dairy) as ingredients in its standard recipe. Where each allergen comes from:

- Milk (dairy): from the laban

Chilled Laban with Mint has no specific trace warnings beyond the general kitchen statement below.

Chilled Laban with Mint contains no gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), molluscs, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites or lupin.

Kitchen allergen statement: Our single kitchen handles cereals containing gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns/shrimp), eggs, fish, soy, milk / dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites. We do not use molluscs, peanuts, lupin as ingredients, but supplier products may carry traces. We take care to limit cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen. Guests with severe allergies should speak to the duty manager before ordering.

## Removing allergens from Chilled Laban with Mint

- Milk (dairy) cannot be removed from Chilled Laban with Mint; it is part of the recipe.

## When Chilled Laban with Mint is available

- Chilled Laban with Mint is on the menu every day of the week.

## Ordering Chilled Laban with Mint: dine-in, pickup and delivery

- Dine-in at the restaurant: yes
- Pickup / takeaway: yes
- Delivery: yes, to Zone A — Old Dubai, Zone B — Creek & Deira and Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud
- Dine-in pre-order (ordered ahead with a table reservation): no

Ordered for pickup on its own, Chilled Laban with Mint can be ready at the earliest 20 minutes after the order is placed (2 minutes of cooking plus 10 minutes of packing, and never less than 20 minutes). Pickup and delivery times are booked in 15-minute slots. For delivery, add the drive time: 15 minutes to Zone A, 25 minutes to Zone B and 35 minutes to Zone C.
