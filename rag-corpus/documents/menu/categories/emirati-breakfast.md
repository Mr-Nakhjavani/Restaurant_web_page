# Emirati Breakfast (الفطور الإماراتي)

The Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Emirati Breakfast section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Emirati Breakfast (Arabic: الفطور الإماراتي) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 5 dishes. Served daily from 08:00 to 11:30 (not during Ramadan). The weekend breakfast spread is served on Saturdays and Sundays until 12:00.

Prices in Emirati Breakfast range from AED 28 to AED 149.

## All dishes in Emirati Breakfast with prices

- Balaleet (بلاليط) — AED 34; serves one person; vegetarian; Sweet saffron and cardamom vermicelli crowned with a thin omelette.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (خبز جباب) — AED 36; serves one person; vegetarian; Three saffron-cardamom Emirati pancakes with warm dibs and cream cheese.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese (رقاق بالبيض والجبن) — AED 28; serves one person; vegetarian; Paper-thin crisp regag folded over egg and melted cheese.
- Bayd w Tamat (بيض وطماط) — AED 30; serves one person; vegetarian; Soft eggs scrambled with tomato, onion, green chilli and bezar, with regag.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (فوالة الصباح) — AED 149; serves 2 people; vegetarian; Saturdays & Sundays only: a generous traditional breakfast spread for two.

## Emirati Breakfast: allergens and diets at a glance

- Balaleet: contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy); vegetarian
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy); vegetarian
- Regag with Egg & Cheese: contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy); vegetarian
- Bayd w Tamat: contains gluten (wheat) and eggs; may contain celery and mustard; vegetarian
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: contains gluten (wheat), eggs and milk (dairy); may contain celery and mustard; vegetarian

## Emirati Breakfast: when the dishes are served

- Balaleet: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30)
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30)
- Regag with Egg & Cheese: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30). Best eaten immediately; it softens within about 15 minutes if taken away
- Bayd w Tamat: Breakfast menu: daily 08:00–11:30, not served during Ramadan. Not available for delivery (delivery runs 12:00–22:30)
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: Saturdays and Sundays only, 08:00–12:00 (not during Ramadan). Dine-in, pickup or dine-in pre-order; not delivered. Limited to 25 spreads per day
