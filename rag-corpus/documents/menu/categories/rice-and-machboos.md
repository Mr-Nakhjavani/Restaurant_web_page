# Rice & Machboos (المجبوس والأرز)

The Rice & Machboos section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Rice & Machboos section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Rice & Machboos (Arabic: المجبوس والأرز) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 4 dishes. Machboos and muhammar — spiced and sweet rice dishes at the heart of the Emirati table.

Prices in Rice & Machboos range from AED 58 to AED 92.

## All dishes in Rice & Machboos with prices

- Lamb Machboos (مجبوس لحم) — AED 72; serves one person; meat; Our signature: bone-in lamb shoulder and spiced basmati with loomi and hashu.
- Chicken Machboos (مجبوس دجاج) — AED 58; serves one person; meat; Half a chicken and spiced basmati cooked in loomi stock, topped with hashu.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (محمر وصافي) — AED 69; serves one person; pescatarian; Sweet saffron and date-syrup rice with a whole fried rabbitfish.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (مجبوس لحم إبل) — AED 92; serves one person; meat; Thursday special: young camel slow-braised for three hours, with spiced rice and hashu.

## Rice & Machboos: allergens and diets at a glance

- Lamb Machboos: contains milk (dairy) and sulphites; may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Chicken Machboos: contains milk (dairy) and sulphites; may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Muhammar with Fried Safi: contains fish and milk (dairy); may contain celery and mustard; pescatarian
- Camel Machboos (Thursday): contains milk (dairy) and sulphites; may contain celery and mustard; meat

## Rice & Machboos: when the dishes are served

- Lamb Machboos: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Chicken Machboos: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Muhammar with Fried Safi: Served from 12:00, subject to the day's catch (about 25 fish per day)
- Camel Machboos (Thursday): Thursdays only, from 12:00 until sold out (20 portions). Catering orders on other days need 72 hours' notice
