# Cold Drinks (المشروبات الباردة)

The Cold Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Cold Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Cold Drinks (Arabic: المشروبات الباردة) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 3 dishes. Laban, fresh lemon-mint and date shakes. We are an alcohol-free restaurant.

Prices in Cold Drinks range from AED 14 to AED 28.

## All dishes in Cold Drinks with prices

- Chilled Laban with Mint (لبن بارد بالنعناع) — AED 14; serves one person; vegetarian; Cold, lightly salted cultured buttermilk with fresh and dried mint.
- Limon Na'na (ليمون بالنعناع) — AED 22; serves one person; vegan; Fresh lemon juice blended with mint and ice.
- Date & Cardamom Shake (ميلك شيك التمر بالهيل) — AED 28; serves one person; vegetarian; Khalas dates blended with cold milk, date syrup and cardamom.

## Cold Drinks: allergens and diets at a glance

- Chilled Laban with Mint: contains milk (dairy); vegetarian
- Limon Na'na: contains none of the 14 major allergens; vegan
- Date & Cardamom Shake: contains milk (dairy); may contain tree nuts; vegetarian

## Cold Drinks: when the dishes are served

- Chilled Laban with Mint: Available whenever we are open
- Limon Na'na: Available whenever we are open
- Date & Cardamom Shake: Available whenever we are open
