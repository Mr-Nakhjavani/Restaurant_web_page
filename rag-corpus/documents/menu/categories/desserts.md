# Desserts (الحلويات)

The Desserts section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Desserts section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Desserts (Arabic: الحلويات) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 4 dishes. Luqaimat, khanfaroosh and seasonal sweets.

Prices in Desserts range from AED 30 to AED 36.

## All dishes in Desserts with prices

- Luqaimat (لقيمات) — AED 32; serves 2 people; vegetarian; Crisp saffron-cardamom dumplings, warm dibs and toasted sesame.
- Khanfaroosh (خنفروش) — AED 30; serves 2 people; vegetarian; Little saffron and rosewater cakes, crisp-edged and fragrant.
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (آيس كريم حليب النوق بالتمر) — AED 36; serves one person; vegetarian; Three scoops of house-churned camel-milk and date ice cream with pistachio crumb.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (عصيدة بوبر) — AED 34; serves one person; vegetarian; Winter only: warm pumpkin asida with ghee, saffron and cardamom.

## Desserts: allergens and diets at a glance

- Luqaimat: contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy) and sesame; may contain eggs; vegetarian
- Khanfaroosh: contains gluten (wheat) and eggs; may contain milk (dairy) and sesame; vegetarian
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: contains eggs, milk (dairy) and tree nuts; vegetarian
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy); vegetarian

## Desserts: when the dishes are served

- Luqaimat: Available whenever the kitchen is open. For takeaway or delivery we recommend dibs on the side
- Khanfaroosh: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: Available whenever the kitchen is open. Not delivered (it would melt); takeaway is packed in an insulated cup
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): Limited time: served from 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027 only
