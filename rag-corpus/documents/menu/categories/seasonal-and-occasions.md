# Seasonal & Occasions (المواسم والمناسبات)

The Seasonal & Occasions section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Seasonal & Occasions section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Seasonal & Occasions (Arabic: المواسم والمناسبات) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 2 dishes. Menus served only during Ramadan and UAE National Day (Eid Al Etihad).

Prices in Seasonal & Occasions range from AED 125 to AED 355.

## All dishes in Seasonal & Occasions with prices

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (سفرة الإفطار) — AED 125; serves one person; meat; Ramadan only: dates & laban, lentil shorba, sambousa, a main of your choice, luqaimat and tea.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (وليمة عيد الاتحاد) — AED 355; serves 4 people; meat; 1–3 December only. Serves 4: camel machboos, harees, chebab, luqaimat and gahwa.

## Seasonal & Occasions: allergens and diets at a glance

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), celery, sesame and sulphites; may contain eggs and mustard; meat
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): contains gluten (wheat), eggs, milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites; may contain celery and mustard; meat

## Seasonal & Occasions: when the dishes are served

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): Ramadan only (Ramadan 2027 is provisionally 8 February – 9 March 2027). Served or collected between 17:30 and 20:30. Same-day orders must be placed by 14:00. Minimum 2 sets. Dine-in (with a reservation) or pickup; not delivered
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): Available 1–3 December 2026 only. Pre-orders open on 15 November 2026 and must be placed at least 24 hours ahead. Pickup, delivery or dine-in. Limited to 20 per day
