# Family Platters & Catering (الصواني العائلية والولائم)

The Family Platters & Catering section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Family Platters & Catering section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Family Platters & Catering (Arabic: الصواني العائلية والولائم) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 4 dishes. Sharing platters for the majlis, the Friday family platter, whole lamb ouzi and office lunch boxes.

Prices in Family Platters & Catering range from AED 45 to AED 1,450.

## All dishes in Family Platters & Catering with prices

- Majlis Sharing Platter (صينية المجلس) — AED 329; serves 4 people; meat; Serves 4: lamb machboos, chicken saloona, sambousa, house salad and luqaimat.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (صينية مجبوس الجمعة) — AED 349; serves 5 people; meat; Fridays only. Serves 5: lamb shoulder and whole chicken machboos on one great tray.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (أوزي) — AED 1,450; serves 10 people; meat; Catering only. Serves 10–12: whole slow-roasted lamb on spiced rice with nuts.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (صندوق غداء المجبوس) — AED 45; serves one person; meat; Catering only, minimum 10: machboos, house salad, three luqaimat and water.

## Family Platters & Catering: allergens and diets at a glance

- Majlis Sharing Platter: contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites; may contain eggs, celery and mustard; meat
- Friday Family Machboos Platter: contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites; may contain eggs, celery and mustard; meat
- Whole Lamb Ouzi: contains milk (dairy), tree nuts and sulphites; may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: contains gluten (wheat), milk (dairy), sesame and sulphites; may contain eggs, celery and mustard; meat

## Family Platters & Catering: when the dishes are served

- Majlis Sharing Platter: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes; delivery available
- Friday Family Machboos Platter: Fridays only, for collection or dine-in between 12:00 and 22:00. Order at least 2 hours ahead (pre-orders up to 7 days ahead). Not delivered. Limited to 12 platters per Friday
- Whole Lamb Ouzi: Catering order only, with at least 48 hours' notice. Maximum 3 per day. Pickup, catering delivery in Dubai, or served in the majlis
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: Catering order only: minimum 10 boxes, at least 24 hours' notice, collection or delivery between 10:00 and 21:00
