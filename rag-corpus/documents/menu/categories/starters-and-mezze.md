# Starters & Mezze (المقبلات)

The Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Starters & Mezze section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Starters & Mezze (Arabic: المقبلات) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 6 dishes. Small plates to share, from lentil shorba to sambousa and mehyawa regag.

Prices in Starters & Mezze range from AED 22 to AED 38.

## All dishes in Starters & Mezze with prices

- Shorbat Adas (شوربة عدس) — AED 26; serves one person; vegan; Velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lime and sourdough croutons.
- Sambousa Trio (سمبوسة) — AED 34; serves 2 people; meat; Six crisp sambousa: spiced lamb, akkawi cheese and potato-vegetable, with dakkus.
- Nakhi (نخي) — AED 22; serves one person; vegan; Warm boiled chickpeas with cumin, chilli and lime — an old Emirati snack.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (حمص باللحم والصنوبر) — AED 38; serves 2 people; meat; Silky tahini hummus topped with bezar-spiced lamb and toasted pine nuts.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (سلطة البيت) — AED 30; serves one person; vegan; Tomato, cucumber, jarjeer, radish and pomegranate with loomi-sumac dressing.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites (رقاق بالمهياوة) — AED 26; serves 2 people; pescatarian; Crisp regag spread with mehyawa, egg and cheese, cut into bites.

## Starters & Mezze: allergens and diets at a glance

- Shorbat Adas: contains gluten (wheat) and celery; may contain mustard; vegan
- Sambousa Trio: contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy); may contain eggs, celery, mustard and sesame; meat
- Nakhi: contains none of the 14 major allergens; may contain celery and mustard; vegan
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: contains gluten (wheat), tree nuts and sesame; may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): contains none of the 14 major allergens; vegan
- Mehyawa Regag Bites: contains gluten (wheat), eggs, fish, milk (dairy) and mustard; may contain celery; pescatarian

## Starters & Mezze: when the dishes are served

- Shorbat Adas: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Sambousa Trio: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Nakhi: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): Available whenever the kitchen is open
- Mehyawa Regag Bites: Available whenever the kitchen is open; not delivered because regag loses its crispness
