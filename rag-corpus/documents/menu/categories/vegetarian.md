# Vegetarian (أطباق نباتية)

The Vegetarian section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Vegetarian section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Vegetarian (Arabic: أطباق نباتية) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 3 dishes. Plant-based saloona and machboos. All three dishes are vegan.

Prices in Vegetarian range from AED 44 to AED 48.

## All dishes in Vegetarian with prices

- Vegetable Machboos (مجبوس خضار) — AED 48; serves one person; vegan; Vegan machboos with chickpeas, eggplant, zucchini, carrot and potato.
- Bamia Saloona (صالونة بامية) — AED 44; serves one person; vegan; Tender okra in a garlicky tomato saloona with coriander and loomi; with rice.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (صالونة قرع وحمص) — AED 44; serves one person; vegan; Sweet pumpkin and chickpeas in a turmeric-ginger saloona; with rice.

## Vegetarian: allergens and diets at a glance

- Vegetable Machboos: contains celery; may contain mustard; vegan
- Bamia Saloona: contains none of the 14 major allergens; may contain celery and mustard; vegan
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: contains celery; may contain mustard; vegan

## Vegetarian: when the dishes are served

- Vegetable Machboos: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Bamia Saloona: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
