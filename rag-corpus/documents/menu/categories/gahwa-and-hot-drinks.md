# Gahwa & Hot Drinks (القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة)

The Gahwa & Hot Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Gahwa & Hot Drinks section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Gahwa & Hot Drinks (Arabic: القهوة والمشروبات الساخنة) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 3 dishes. Emirati coffee poured from the dallah, karak and sulaimani.

Prices in Gahwa & Hot Drinks range from AED 10 to AED 30.

## All dishes in Gahwa & Hot Drinks with prices

- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (قهوة عربية) — AED 30; serves 3 people; vegan; A dallah of lightly roasted Arabic coffee with cardamom and saffron, served with dates.
- Karak Chai (شاي كرك) — AED 12; serves one person; vegetarian; Strong black tea simmered with evaporated milk, cardamom and saffron.
- Sulaimani (شاي سليماني) — AED 10; serves one person; vegan; Clear black tea with lime and mint.

## Gahwa & Hot Drinks: allergens and diets at a glance

- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): contains none of the 14 major allergens; vegan
- Karak Chai: contains milk (dairy); vegetarian
- Sulaimani: contains none of the 14 major allergens; vegan

## Gahwa & Hot Drinks: when the dishes are served

- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): Available whenever we are open. Takeaway in an insulated flask; not delivered
- Karak Chai: Available whenever we are open
- Sulaimani: Available whenever we are open
