# Grills & Seafood (المشاوي والبحريات)

The Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Grills & Seafood section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Grills & Seafood (Arabic: المشاوي والبحريات) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 6 dishes. Charcoal grills, Gulf seafood and contemporary camel dishes.

Prices in Grills & Seafood range from AED 68 to AED 118.

## All dishes in Grills & Seafood with prices

- Hammour Mashwi (هامور مشوي) — AED 98; serves one person; pescatarian; Charcoal-grilled hammour fillet in bezar and lime, with rice and dakkus.
- Rubyan Mashwi (روبيان مشوي) — AED 105; serves one person; pescatarian; Six jumbo Gulf prawns grilled in garlic-lime butter, with rice and House Green.
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (ريش غنم) — AED 118; serves one person; meat; Five laban-marinated lamb chops glazed with Sauce No. 7, with grilled tomato and toum.
- Saffron Shish Tawook (شيش طاووق بالزعفران) — AED 68; serves one person; meat; Chicken skewers in saffron-yogurt marinade with grilled peppers and toum.
- Camel Kofta (كفتة لحم الإبل) — AED 76; serves one person; meat; Charcoal-grilled camel kofta with Al Ghaf House Red and regag.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (برجر لحم الإبل) — AED 72; serves one person; meat; Camel patty, Sauce No. 7, cheese and loomi aioli in brioche, with spiced wedges.

## Grills & Seafood: allergens and diets at a glance

- Hammour Mashwi: contains fish; may contain gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), soy, celery, mustard and sesame; pescatarian
- Rubyan Mashwi: contains crustaceans (prawns and shrimp) and milk (dairy); may contain gluten (wheat), fish, soy, tree nuts, celery, mustard and sesame; pescatarian
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): contains gluten (wheat), soy, milk (dairy) and sesame; may contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, celery and mustard; meat
- Saffron Shish Tawook: contains milk (dairy); may contain gluten (wheat), crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy and sesame; meat
- Camel Kofta: contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts; may contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish, soy, celery, mustard and sesame; meat
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli: contains gluten (wheat), eggs, soy, milk (dairy), mustard and sesame; may contain crustaceans (prawns and shrimp), fish and celery; meat
