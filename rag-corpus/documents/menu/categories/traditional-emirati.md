# Traditional Emirati (أطباق تقليدية)

The Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu: every dish with price, portion, diet, allergens and serving times.

## Traditional Emirati section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu

Traditional Emirati (Arabic: أطباق تقليدية) is a section of the Bayt Al Ghaf menu with 6 dishes. Home-style stews and slow-cooked classics, including our weekday specials.

Prices in Traditional Emirati range from AED 48 to AED 64.

## All dishes in Traditional Emirati with prices

- Harees (هريس) — AED 48; serves one person; meat; Wheat and lamb slow-cooked overnight and beaten smooth, finished with ghee.
- Lamb Thareed (ثريد لحم) — AED 62; serves one person; meat; Lamb and vegetable saloona ladled over layers of regag bread.
- Saloonat Dajaj (صالونة دجاج) — AED 52; serves one person; meat; Chicken and vegetable stew with loomi and bezar, served with white rice.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (صالونة سمك) — AED 64; serves one person; pescatarian; Monday special: kingfish steaks in a tamarind-tomato saloona with rice.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (مرقوقة لحم) — AED 58; serves one person; meat; Tuesday special: lamb and vegetable stew cooked with hand-stretched dough sheets.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (مضروبة دجاج) — AED 54; serves one person; meat; Wednesday special: chicken and rice beaten to a creamy, spiced porridge.

## Traditional Emirati: allergens and diets at a glance

- Harees: contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy); meat
- Lamb Thareed: contains gluten (wheat) and milk (dairy); may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Saloonat Dajaj: contains none of the 14 major allergens; may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Saloonat Samak (Monday): contains fish; may contain celery and mustard; pescatarian
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday): contains gluten (wheat); may contain celery and mustard; meat
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): contains milk (dairy); may contain celery and mustard; meat

## Traditional Emirati: when the dishes are served

- Harees: Served from 12:00 until sold out. Limited to 30 portions per day
- Lamb Thareed: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Saloonat Dajaj: Served from 12:00 until the kitchen closes
- Saloonat Samak (Monday): Mondays only, from 12:00 until sold out (20 portions). Can be pre-ordered up to 7 days ahead for a Monday
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday): Tuesdays only, from 12:00 until sold out (20 portions)
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): Wednesdays only, from 12:00 until sold out (20 portions)
