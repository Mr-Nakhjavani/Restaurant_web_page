# Bayt Al Ghaf spice guide

How spicy each Bayt Al Ghaf dish is (levels 0 to 3), which dishes can be made milder or hotter, and where the heat comes from.

## How Bayt Al Ghaf rates spice

Bayt Al Ghaf rates each dish from 0 to 3: 0 = not spicy, 1 = mild (warm spices such as bezar rather than chilli heat), 2 = medium-hot, 3 = hot. Emirati food is generally fragrant rather than fiery; most heat comes from dakkus, the tomato-chilli sauce served on the side.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that are not spicy (level 0)

Bayt Al Ghaf dishes rated not spicy (spice level 0 of 3): Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Shorbat Adas, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Harees, Luqaimat, Khanfaroosh, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Karak Chai, Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na and Date & Cardamom Shake.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that are mild (level 1)

Bayt Al Ghaf dishes rated mild (spice level 1 of 3): Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Sambousa Trio, Nakhi, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Saffron Shish Tawook, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that are medium-hot (level 2)

Bayt Al Ghaf dishes rated medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3): Saloonat Samak (Monday) and Camel Kofta.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that are hot (level 3)

No Bayt Al Ghaf dish is rated hot (spice level 3 of 3) as standard; guests who want more heat can ask for extra dakkus or choose “Hot” where a spice choice is offered.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made milder

These Bayt Al Ghaf dishes can be made milder on request: Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Nakhi, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Muhammar with Fried Safi, Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Camel Kofta, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Bamia Saloona and Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that can be made spicier

These Bayt Al Ghaf dishes can be made spicier on request: Bayd w Tamat, Shorbat Adas, Sambousa Trio, Nakhi, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Saffron Shish Tawook, Camel Kofta, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes whose spice cannot be changed

- Balaleet: Not spicy. Sweetness can be reduced (choose “Less sweet”).
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: Not spicy.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese: Not spicy. Mehyawa can be added for a savoury tang.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): Not spicy.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites: Mehyawa is savoury and pungent rather than hot; it cannot be removed from this dish.
- Harees: Harees is not spiced with chilli and cannot be made spicy.
- Luqaimat: Not spicy.
- Khanfaroosh: Not spicy.
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream: Not spicy.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): Not spicy.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): Not spicy.
- Karak Chai: Not spicy.
- Sulaimani: Not spicy.
- Chilled Laban with Mint: Not spicy.
- Limon Na'na: Not spicy.
- Date & Cardamom Shake: Not spicy.
