# Mehyawa (مهياوة)

Mehyawa is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Mehyawa at a glance

Mehyawa (Arabic: مهياوة) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. A pungent, savoury fermented fish sauce from the Gulf coast, traditionally spread on regag with egg and cheese.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Intensely savoury, salty and tangy, with mustard-seed warmth
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: fish and mustard
- May contain traces of: celery
- Diet: pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: Mehyawa Regag Bites
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: mehyawa, mahyawa, fish sauce, fermented fish sauce

## Recipe of Mehyawa: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Mehyawa yields about 300 ml (20 portions of 15 g) and is made from:

- Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy): 200 g (contains fish)
- Salt: 40 g
- Mustard seeds: 10 g (contains mustard)
- Cumin: 4 g
- Fennel seed: 4 g
- Coriander seed: 4 g

## How Mehyawa is made

Salted small fish are fermented, then blended with toasted mustard seed, cumin, fennel and coriander and strained.

Batch size: about 300 ml (20 portions of 15 g). Mehyawa is served room temperature.

## Mehyawa: allergens and dietary information

Mehyawa contains fish and mustard:

- Fish from small salted fish (sardine/anchovy)
- Mustard from mustard seeds

Mehyawa may contain traces of celery.

Mehyawa is pescatarian (contains fish or seafood, no meat).

## Dishes served with Mehyawa

- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26): Mehyawa is cooked into Mehyawa Regag Bites (15 g)

Mehyawa can be added as an extra to: Regag with Egg & Cheese (“Add mehyawa (fermented fish sauce)”, +AED 4).

A: Mehyawa is made from small salted fish (sardine/anchovy), salt, mustard seeds, cumin, fennel seed and coriander seed.

**Q: Is Mehyawa vegan?**

**Q: Does Mehyawa contain nuts?**

A: Mehyawa has no nut ingredients.

**Q: Can I buy extra Mehyawa?**
