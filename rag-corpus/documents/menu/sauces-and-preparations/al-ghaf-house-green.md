# Al Ghaf House Green (صلصة الغاف الخضراء)

Al Ghaf House Green is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Al Ghaf House Green at a glance

Al Ghaf House Green (Arabic: صلصة الغاف الخضراء) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen and one of its named house preparations. Our second house sauce: a vivid herb sauce of coriander, parsley, mint and green chilli with lime. It has no nut, dairy, gluten or sesame ingredients, but it is made on the same station as the walnut-based House Red.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: yes
- Flavour: Fresh, herbal and zesty with a medium green-chilli kick
- Spice: medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: gluten (wheat), tree nuts, celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Chilled
- Used in: Rubyan Mashwi
- Extra portion: AED 5 where offered as an extra
- Also called: house green, house sauce, green house sauce, ghaf green, green sauce, herb sauce

## Recipe of Al Ghaf House Green: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Al Ghaf House Green yields about 420 g (12 portions) and is made from:

- Coriander leaves: 120 g
- Parsley: 60 g
- Mint: 30 g
- Green chilli: 25 g
- Garlic: 10 g
- Lime: 50 ml juice
- Olive oil: 150 ml
- Cumin: 2 g
- Salt: 5 g

## How Al Ghaf House Green is made

All ingredients are blended to a loose sauce in small batches twice daily.

Batch size: about 420 g (12 portions). Al Ghaf House Green is served chilled.

## Al Ghaf House Green: allergens and dietary information

Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Al Ghaf House Green may contain traces of gluten (wheat), tree nuts, celery and mustard.

Cross-contact notes for Al Ghaf House Green:

- Made in the same blender and on the same station as Al Ghaf House Red, which contains walnuts and wheat; we cannot rule out traces of tree nuts or gluten.

Al Ghaf House Green is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Al Ghaf House Green

- Rubyan Mashwi (AED 105): Al Ghaf House Green is served on the side with Rubyan Mashwi (50 g)

## Al Ghaf House Green and the other Bayt Al Ghaf house preparations

Bayt Al Ghaf has 4 named house preparations: Bezar Spice Blend, Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a). When a guest asks for “the house sauce”, it is worth checking which one they mean. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens.
