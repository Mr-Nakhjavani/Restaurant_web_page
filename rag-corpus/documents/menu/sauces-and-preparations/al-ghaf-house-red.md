# Al Ghaf House Red (صلصة الغاف الحمراء)

Al Ghaf House Red is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Al Ghaf House Red at a glance

Al Ghaf House Red (Arabic: صلصة الغاف الحمراء) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen and one of its named house preparations. One of our two house sauces: a thick roasted-pepper and walnut sauce sharpened with pomegranate molasses. It contains walnuts and wheat breadcrumbs.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: yes
- Flavour: Smoky, sweet-sour and nutty with a medium chilli warmth
- Spice: medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat) and tree nuts
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: Camel Kofta
- Extra portion: AED 5 where offered as an extra
- Also called: house red, house sauce, red house sauce, ghaf red, red sauce

## Recipe of Al Ghaf House Red: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Al Ghaf House Red yields about 700 g (14 portions of 50 g) and is made from:

- Roasted red pepper: 400 g
- Walnuts: 120 g (contains tree nuts)
- Breadcrumbs: 60 g (contains gluten (wheat))
- Pomegranate molasses: 40 ml
- Olive oil: 60 ml
- Garlic: 10 g
- Dried red chilli: 4 g
- Cumin: 3 g
- Lemon: 15 ml juice
- Salt: 5 g

## How Al Ghaf House Red is made

Peppers are flame-roasted and peeled, then blended coarsely with toasted walnuts, breadcrumbs and the remaining ingredients.

Batch size: about 700 g (14 portions of 50 g). Al Ghaf House Red is served room temperature.

## Al Ghaf House Red: allergens and dietary information

Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts:

- Gluten (wheat) from breadcrumbs
- Tree nuts from walnuts

Al Ghaf House Red may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Cross-contact notes for Al Ghaf House Red:

- Prepared in the same blender and on the same station as Al Ghaf House Green; equipment is washed between batches.

Al Ghaf House Red is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Al Ghaf House Red

- Camel Kofta (AED 76): Al Ghaf House Red is served on the side with Camel Kofta (50 g)

## Al Ghaf House Red and the other Bayt Al Ghaf house preparations

Bayt Al Ghaf has 4 named house preparations: Bezar Spice Blend, Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a). When a guest asks for “the house sauce”, it is worth checking which one they mean. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts.
