# Garlic Lime Butter (زبدة الثوم والليمون)

Garlic Lime Butter is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Garlic Lime Butter at a glance

Garlic Lime Butter (Arabic: زبدة الثوم والليمون) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Melted butter with garlic, lime, parsley and a pinch of chilli, spooned over grilled prawns.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Rich, citrusy and garlicky
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Hot
- Used in: Rubyan Mashwi
- Extra portion: not sold separately
- Also called: garlic butter

## Recipe of Garlic Lime Butter: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Garlic Lime Butter yields about 6 portions and is made from:

- Butter: 100 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Garlic: 20 g
- Lime: 20 ml juice
- Parsley: 5 g
- Dried red chilli: 2 g

## How Garlic Lime Butter is made

Butter is melted with garlic until fragrant and finished with lime, parsley and chilli.

Batch size: about 6 portions. Garlic Lime Butter is served hot.

## Garlic Lime Butter: allergens and dietary information

Garlic Lime Butter contains milk (dairy):

- Milk (dairy) from butter

Garlic Lime Butter is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).

## Dishes served with Garlic Lime Butter

- Rubyan Mashwi (AED 105): Garlic Lime Butter is cooked into Rubyan Mashwi
