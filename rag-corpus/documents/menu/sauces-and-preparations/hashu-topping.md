# Hashu Topping (حشو)

Hashu Topping is a topping made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Hashu Topping at a glance

Hashu Topping (Arabic: حشو) is a topping made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos.

- Type: topping
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Sweet, buttery and gently spiced
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy) and sulphites
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Warm
- Used in: Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)
- Extra portion: AED 5 where offered as an extra
- Also called: hashu, hashwa, fried onion raisin topping

## Recipe of Hashu Topping: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Hashu Topping yields about 10 portions (35 g each) and is made from:

- Onion: 500 g, thinly sliced
- Golden raisins: 80 g (contains sulphites)
- Ghee (clarified butter): 40 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Sugar: 10 g
- Green cardamom: 2 g, ground
- Salt: 3 g
- Bezar Spice Blend: 3 g (a Bayt Al Ghaf preparation with its own recipe)

Hashu Topping includes Bezar Spice Blend, so everything in Bezar Spice Blend is also in Hashu Topping.

## How Hashu Topping is made

Onions are cooked slowly in ghee for about 45 minutes until deep golden, then raisins, sugar, cardamom and a pinch of bezar are folded through.

Batch size: about 10 portions (35 g each). Hashu Topping is served warm.

## Hashu Topping: allergens and dietary information

Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites:

- Milk (dairy) from ghee (clarified butter)
- Sulphites from golden raisins

Hashu Topping may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Hashu Topping is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).
