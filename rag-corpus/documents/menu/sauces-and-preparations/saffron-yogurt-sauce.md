# Saffron Yogurt Sauce (صلصة الزبادي بالزعفران)

Saffron Yogurt Sauce is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Saffron Yogurt Sauce at a glance

Saffron Yogurt Sauce (Arabic: صلصة الزبادي بالزعفران) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Cool, garlicky yogurt sauce scented with saffron and mint.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Creamy, tangy and floral
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Chilled
- Used in: offered as an extra with Lamb Machboos and Chicken Machboos
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: yogurt sauce, saffron yoghurt

## Recipe of Saffron Yogurt Sauce: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Saffron Yogurt Sauce yields about 520 g (10 portions) and is made from:

- Natural yogurt: 500 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Saffron: 0.3 g, bloomed in warm water
- Garlic: 8 g
- Lemon: 20 ml juice
- Mint: 5 g
- Salt: 4 g

## How Saffron Yogurt Sauce is made

Saffron is bloomed, then whisked into yogurt with grated garlic, lemon juice, chopped mint and salt.

Batch size: about 520 g (10 portions). Saffron Yogurt Sauce is served chilled.

## Saffron Yogurt Sauce: allergens and dietary information

Saffron Yogurt Sauce contains milk (dairy):

- Milk (dairy) from natural yogurt

Saffron Yogurt Sauce is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).

Saffron Yogurt Sauce can be added as an extra to: Lamb Machboos (“Extra saffron yogurt sauce”, +AED 4) and Chicken Machboos (“Extra saffron yogurt sauce”, +AED 4).
