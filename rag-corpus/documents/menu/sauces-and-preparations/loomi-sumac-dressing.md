# Loomi & Sumac Dressing (صلصة اللومي والسماق)

Loomi & Sumac Dressing is a dressing made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Loomi & Sumac Dressing at a glance

Loomi & Sumac Dressing (Arabic: صلصة اللومي والسماق) is a dressing made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses.

- Type: dressing
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Sour, fruity and smoky
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)
- Extra portion: AED 3 where offered as an extra
- Also called: salad dressing, loomi dressing

## Recipe of Loomi & Sumac Dressing: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Loomi & Sumac Dressing yields about 200 ml (7 portions) and is made from:

- Olive oil: 120 ml
- Lime: 50 ml juice
- Loomi (dried black lime): 4 g, ground
- Sumac: 4 g
- Pomegranate molasses: 20 ml
- Salt: 3 g
- Black pepper: 1 g

## How Loomi & Sumac Dressing is made

Whisked together and shaken before each use.

Batch size: about 200 ml (7 portions). Loomi & Sumac Dressing is served room temperature.

## Loomi & Sumac Dressing: allergens and dietary information

Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Loomi & Sumac Dressing is vegan (no animal products).
