# Tahini Lemon Sauce (صلصة الطحينة)

Tahini Lemon Sauce is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Tahini Lemon Sauce at a glance

Tahini Lemon Sauce (Arabic: صلصة الطحينة) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Silky sesame sauce with lemon and garlic.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Nutty, rich and tangy
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: sesame
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: no dish
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: tahini, tahina

## Recipe of Tahini Lemon Sauce: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Tahini Lemon Sauce yields about 450 ml with water and is made from:

- Tahini: 250 g (contains sesame)
- Lemon: 80 ml juice
- Garlic: 6 g
- Cumin: 1 g
- Salt: 4 g

## How Tahini Lemon Sauce is made

Tahini is whisked with lemon juice, garlic, cumin, salt and iced water until smooth.

Batch size: about 450 ml with water. Tahini Lemon Sauce is served room temperature.
