# Pistachio Cardamom Crumb (فتات الفستق بالهيل)

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is a topping made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Pistachio Cardamom Crumb at a glance

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb (Arabic: فتات الفستق بالهيل) is a topping made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Crushed pistachios with sugar and cardamom, scattered over desserts.

- Type: topping
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Nutty, sweet and aromatic
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: tree nuts
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: pistachio crumb, pistachio topping

## Recipe of Pistachio Cardamom Crumb: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Pistachio Cardamom Crumb yields about 12 portions of 10 g and is made from:

- Pistachios: 100 g (contains tree nuts)
- Sugar: 20 g
- Green cardamom: 1 g

## How Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is made

Pistachios are toasted, cooled and pulsed with sugar and cardamom.

Batch size: about 12 portions of 10 g. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is served room temperature.

## Pistachio Cardamom Crumb: allergens and dietary information

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb contains tree nuts:

- Tree nuts from pistachios

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Pistachio Cardamom Crumb

- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36): Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is spooned on top of Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (10 g)

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb can be added as an extra to: Date & Cardamom Shake (“Pistachio crumb”, +AED 4).
