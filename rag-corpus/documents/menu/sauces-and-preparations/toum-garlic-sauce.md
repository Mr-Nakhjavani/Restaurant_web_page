# Toum Garlic Sauce (ثومية)

Toum Garlic Sauce is a dip made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Toum Garlic Sauce at a glance

Toum Garlic Sauce (Arabic: ثومية) is a dip made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fluffy whipped garlic sauce made without egg or dairy.

- Type: dip
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Sharp, pungent garlic softened by lemon
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Chilled
- Used in: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Saffron Shish Tawook
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: toum, garlic sauce, thoumieh

## Recipe of Toum Garlic Sauce: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Toum Garlic Sauce yields about 650 ml and is made from:

- Garlic: 120 g
- Sunflower oil: 500 ml
- Lemon: 60 ml juice
- Salt: 6 g

## How Toum Garlic Sauce is made

Garlic and salt are blended to a paste, then oil and lemon juice are emulsified in slowly.

Batch size: about 650 ml. Toum Garlic Sauce is served chilled.

## Toum Garlic Sauce: allergens and dietary information

Toum Garlic Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Toum Garlic Sauce is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Toum Garlic Sauce

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118): Toum Garlic Sauce is served on the side with Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (40 ml)
- Saffron Shish Tawook (AED 68): Toum Garlic Sauce is served on the side with Saffron Shish Tawook (40 ml)

Toum Garlic Sauce can be added as an extra to: Rubyan Mashwi (“Toum garlic sauce instead”, no extra charge), Saffron Shish Tawook (“Extra toum”, +AED 4) and Camel Kofta (“Toum garlic sauce instead (nut-free ingredients)”, no extra charge).
