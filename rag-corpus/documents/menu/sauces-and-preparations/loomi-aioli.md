# Loomi Aioli (أيولي باللومي)

Loomi Aioli is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Loomi Aioli at a glance

Loomi Aioli (Arabic: أيولي باللومي) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. House-made egg-yolk aioli seasoned with ground dried black lime.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Creamy and garlicky with loomi's smoky sourness
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: eggs and mustard
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Chilled
- Used in: Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: aioli, loomi mayo, black lime mayonnaise

## Recipe of Loomi Aioli: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Loomi Aioli yields about 360 ml and is made from:

- Egg yolk: 40 g (2 yolks, pasteurised) (contains eggs)
- Rapeseed (canola) oil: 300 ml
- Loomi (dried black lime): 6 g, ground
- Garlic: 6 g
- Lemon: 15 ml juice
- Dijon mustard: 10 g (contains mustard)
- Salt: 3 g

## How Loomi Aioli is made

Yolks, mustard, garlic and lemon are whisked and oil is emulsified in; ground loomi is folded through.

Batch size: about 360 ml. Loomi Aioli is served chilled.

## Loomi Aioli: allergens and dietary information

Loomi Aioli contains eggs and mustard:

- Eggs from egg yolk
- Mustard from dijon mustard

Loomi Aioli is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).

## Dishes served with Loomi Aioli

- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72): Loomi Aioli is cooked into Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (25 ml)

Loomi Aioli can be added as an extra to: Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (“Extra loomi aioli”, +AED 4).
