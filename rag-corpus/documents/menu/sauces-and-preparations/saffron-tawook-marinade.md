# Saffron Tawook Marinade (تتبيلة الطاووق بالزعفران)

Saffron Tawook Marinade is a marinade made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Saffron Tawook Marinade at a glance

Saffron Tawook Marinade (Arabic: تتبيلة الطاووق بالزعفران) is a marinade made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Yogurt, saffron, lemon and paprika marinade for chicken tawook.

- Type: marinade
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Tangy and fragrant with mild paprika warmth
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Cooked onto the chicken
- Used in: Saffron Shish Tawook
- Extra portion: not sold separately
- Also called: tawook marinade

## Recipe of Saffron Tawook Marinade: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Saffron Tawook Marinade yields enough for 2 kg chicken and is made from:

- Natural yogurt: 250 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Saffron: 0.2 g
- Garlic: 15 g
- Lemon: 30 ml juice
- Paprika: 6 g
- Tomato paste: 15 g
- Olive oil: 30 ml
- Salt: 6 g

## How Saffron Tawook Marinade is made

Chicken is marinated for at least 6 hours.

Batch size: enough for 2 kg chicken. Saffron Tawook Marinade is served cooked onto the chicken.

## Saffron Tawook Marinade: allergens and dietary information

Saffron Tawook Marinade contains milk (dairy):

- Milk (dairy) from natural yogurt

Saffron Tawook Marinade is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).

## Dishes served with Saffron Tawook Marinade

- Saffron Shish Tawook (AED 68): Saffron Tawook Marinade is used as the marinade for Saffron Shish Tawook
