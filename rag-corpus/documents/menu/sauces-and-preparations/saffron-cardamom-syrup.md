# Saffron Cardamom Syrup (شيرة الزعفران والهيل)

Saffron Cardamom Syrup is a syrup made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Saffron Cardamom Syrup at a glance

Saffron Cardamom Syrup (Arabic: شيرة الزعفران والهيل) is a syrup made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Light sugar syrup infused with saffron, cardamom and rosewater.

- Type: syrup
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Floral, sweet and fragrant
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Warm or room temperature
- Used in: Khanfaroosh
- Extra portion: AED 3 where offered as an extra
- Also called: saffron syrup, sugar syrup, sheera

## Recipe of Saffron Cardamom Syrup: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Saffron Cardamom Syrup yields about 600 ml (with 300 ml water) and is made from:

- Sugar: 500 g
- Saffron: 0.3 g
- Green cardamom: 3 g
- Rosewater: 10 ml
- Lemon: 5 ml juice

## How Saffron Cardamom Syrup is made

Sugar and water are simmered with cardamom and saffron, finished with lemon and rosewater.

Batch size: about 600 ml (with 300 ml water). Saffron Cardamom Syrup is served warm or room temperature.

## Saffron Cardamom Syrup: allergens and dietary information

Saffron Cardamom Syrup contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Saffron Cardamom Syrup is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Saffron Cardamom Syrup

Saffron Cardamom Syrup can be added as an extra to: Luqaimat (“Saffron cardamom syrup”, no extra charge).
