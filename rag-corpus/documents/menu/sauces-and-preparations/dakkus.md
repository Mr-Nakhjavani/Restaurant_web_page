# Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce (دقوس)

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is a sauce made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce at a glance

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce (Arabic: دقوس) is a sauce made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste.

- Type: sauce
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Bright, tangy tomato with garlic, lime and a clean chilli heat
- Spice: medium-hot (spice level 2 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Room temperature
- Used in: Sambousa Trio, Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Vegetable Machboos, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)
- Extra portion: AED 4 where offered as an extra
- Also called: dakkus, daqoos, duqqus, tomato chilli sauce, hot sauce

## Recipe of Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce yields about 700 ml (14 portions of 50 ml) and is made from:

- Tomato: 600 g
- Tomato paste: 40 g
- Garlic: 25 g
- Green chilli: 15 g
- Dried red chilli: 6 g
- Lime: 30 ml juice
- Cumin: 3 g
- Sunflower oil: 20 ml
- Salt: 8 g

## How Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is made

Garlic and chillies are sizzled in oil, then tomatoes, paste and cumin are simmered for 25 minutes, blended and finished with lime juice.

Batch size: about 700 ml (14 portions of 50 ml). Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is served room temperature.

## Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce: allergens and dietary information

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is vegan (no animal products).
