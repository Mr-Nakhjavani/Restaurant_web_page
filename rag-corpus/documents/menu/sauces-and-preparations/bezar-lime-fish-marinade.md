# Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade (تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون)

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is a marinade made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade at a glance

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade (Arabic: تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون) is a marinade made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Bright marinade for fish: lime juice, bezar, turmeric and garlic.

- Type: marinade
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Citrusy and warmly spiced
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Cooked onto the fish
- Used in: Saloonat Samak (Monday), Muhammar with Fried Safi and Hammour Mashwi
- Extra portion: not sold separately
- Also called: fish marinade

## Recipe of Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade yields enough for 1.5 kg fish and is made from:

- Lime: 60 ml juice
- Turmeric: 3 g
- Garlic: 15 g
- Sunflower oil: 40 ml
- Salt: 6 g
- Bezar Spice Blend: 12 g (a Bayt Al Ghaf preparation with its own recipe)

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade includes Bezar Spice Blend, so everything in Bezar Spice Blend is also in Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade.

## How Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is made

Everything is whisked together; fish is marinated for 30–60 minutes before cooking.

Batch size: enough for 1.5 kg fish. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is served cooked onto the fish.

## Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade: allergens and dietary information

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade

- Hammour Mashwi (AED 98): Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is used as the marinade for Hammour Mashwi
