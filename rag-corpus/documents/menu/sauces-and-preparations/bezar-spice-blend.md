# Bezar Spice Blend (بزار)

Bezar Spice Blend is a spice blend made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Bezar Spice Blend at a glance

Bezar Spice Blend (Arabic: بزار) is a spice blend made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen and one of its named house preparations. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week.

- Type: spice blend
- House preparation: yes
- Flavour: Warm, earthy and aromatic with gentle heat; cumin and coriander lead, with cinnamon, cardamom and clove in the background
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Used as a seasoning
- Used in: Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Sambousa Trio, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Camel Kofta, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)
- Extra portion: not sold separately
- Also called: bezar, bizar, emirati spice mix, machboos spice

## Recipe of Bezar Spice Blend: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Bezar Spice Blend yields 100 g of ground blend and is made from:

- Coriander seed: 25 g
- Cumin: 20 g
- Fennel seed: 10 g
- Black pepper: 10 g
- Turmeric: 10 g
- Cinnamon: 8 g
- Green cardamom: 7 g
- Dried red chilli: 5 g
- Cloves: 3 g
- Nutmeg: 2 g

## How Bezar Spice Blend is made

Whole spices are dry-toasted separately, cooled, ground together and stored airtight for up to 14 days.

Batch size: 100 g of ground blend. Bezar Spice Blend is served used as a seasoning.

## Bezar Spice Blend: allergens and dietary information

Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Bezar Spice Blend may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Cross-contact notes for Bezar Spice Blend:

- Ground cumin and coriander are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery, so bezar may contain traces of mustard and celery.

Bezar Spice Blend is vegan (no animal products).

## Preparations that include Bezar Spice Blend

Bezar Spice Blend is an ingredient of Hashu Topping, Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade and Laban Marinade. Dishes made with those preparations therefore also contain Bezar Spice Blend.

## Bezar Spice Blend and the other Bayt Al Ghaf house preparations

Bayt Al Ghaf has 4 named house preparations: Bezar Spice Blend, Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a). When a guest asks for “the house sauce”, it is worth checking which one they mean. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens.
