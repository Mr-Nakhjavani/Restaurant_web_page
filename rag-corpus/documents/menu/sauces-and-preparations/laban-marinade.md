# Laban Marinade (تتبيلة اللبن)

Laban Marinade is a marinade made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Laban Marinade at a glance

Laban Marinade (Arabic: تتبيلة اللبن) is a marinade made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Yogurt marinade that tenderises our lamb chops overnight.

- Type: marinade
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Tangy, garlicky and gently spiced
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: milk (dairy)
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish)
- Served: Cooked onto the meat
- Used in: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops)
- Extra portion: not sold separately
- Also called: yogurt marinade

## Recipe of Laban Marinade: every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Laban Marinade yields enough for 2 kg lamb and is made from:

- Natural yogurt: 300 g (contains milk (dairy))
- Garlic: 15 g
- Onion: 50 g, grated
- Lemon: 20 ml juice
- Olive oil: 20 ml
- Salt: 6 g
- Bezar Spice Blend: 8 g (a Bayt Al Ghaf preparation with its own recipe)

Laban Marinade includes Bezar Spice Blend, so everything in Bezar Spice Blend is also in Laban Marinade.

## How Laban Marinade is made

Everything is mixed together; lamb is marinated for 12–18 hours.

Batch size: enough for 2 kg lamb. Laban Marinade is served cooked onto the meat.

## Laban Marinade: allergens and dietary information

Laban Marinade contains milk (dairy):

- Milk (dairy) from natural yogurt

Laban Marinade may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Laban Marinade is vegetarian (contains dairy, eggs or honey, but no meat or fish).

## Dishes served with Laban Marinade

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118): Laban Marinade is used as the marinade for Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops)
