# Dibs (Date Syrup) (دبس)

Dibs (Date Syrup) is a syrup made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Dibs (Date Syrup) at a glance

Dibs (Date Syrup) (Arabic: دبس) is a syrup made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab.

- Type: syrup
- House preparation: no
- Flavour: Deep caramel and date sweetness
- Spice: not spicy (spice level 0 of 3)
- Contains allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: no specific trace warnings
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Warm
- Used in: Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Luqaimat, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day)
- Extra portion: AED 3 where offered as an extra
- Also called: dibs, date syrup, date molasses

## Recipe of Dibs (Date Syrup): every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Dibs (Date Syrup) yields Served from the bottle, 30 ml per portion and is made from:

- Date syrup (dibs): 100%

## How Dibs (Date Syrup) is made

Warmed gently before serving.

Batch size: Served from the bottle, 30 ml per portion. Dibs (Date Syrup) is served warm.

## Dibs (Date Syrup): allergens and dietary information

Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens as ingredients.

Dibs (Date Syrup) is vegan (no animal products).
