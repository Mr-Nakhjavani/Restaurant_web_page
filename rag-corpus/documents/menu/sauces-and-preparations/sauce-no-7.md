# Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (صلصة سبعة)

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is a glaze made at Bayt Al Ghaf. This document gives its full recipe with batch quantities, allergens, diet, method and every dish it is served with.

## Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) at a glance

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (Arabic: صلصة سبعة) is a glaze made in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen and one of its named house preparations. The chef's signature glaze, named for its seven key flavours: tamarind, date, soy, sesame, garlic, ginger and bezar. It contains soy, wheat (gluten, from the soy sauce) and sesame.

- Type: glaze
- House preparation: yes
- Flavour: Sticky, sweet-sour and deeply savoury with a mild warmth
- Spice: mild (spice level 1 of 3)
- Contains allergens: gluten (wheat), soy and sesame
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Diet: vegan (no animal products)
- Served: Brushed hot onto grills; also served warm as a dip
- Used in: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli
- Extra portion: AED 5 where offered as an extra
- Also called: sauce no 7, sauce number seven, saba'a, sabaa sauce, house glaze, seven sauce

## Recipe of Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a): every ingredient with quantities

One batch of Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) yields about 300 ml and is made from:

- Tamarind: 100 g
- Date syrup (dibs): 120 ml
- Soy sauce: 40 ml (contains gluten (wheat) and soy)
- Toasted sesame oil: 15 ml (contains sesame)
- Garlic: 10 g
- Ginger: 10 g
- Loomi (dried black lime): 3 g, ground
- Sugar: 20 g
- Bezar Spice Blend: 5 g (a Bayt Al Ghaf preparation with its own recipe)

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) includes Bezar Spice Blend, so everything in Bezar Spice Blend is also in Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a).

## How Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is made

Simmered for 20 minutes until glossy; the bezar is whisked in off the heat.

Batch size: about 300 ml. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is served brushed hot onto grills; also served warm as a dip.

## Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a): allergens and dietary information

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame:

- Gluten (wheat) from soy sauce
- Soy from soy sauce
- Sesame from toasted sesame oil

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) may contain traces of celery and mustard.

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is vegan (no animal products).

## Dishes served with Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118): Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is brushed as a glaze on Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (20 ml)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72): Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is brushed as a glaze on Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (15 ml)

## Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) and the other Bayt Al Ghaf house preparations

Bayt Al Ghaf has 4 named house preparations: Bezar Spice Blend, Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a). When a guest asks for “the house sauce”, it is worth checking which one they mean. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame.
