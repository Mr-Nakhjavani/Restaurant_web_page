# Bayt Al Ghaf sauces, marinades and spice blends

All 19 sauces, dips, marinades, spice blends, toppings and syrups made at Bayt Al Ghaf, with their allergens and the dishes they are used in.

## Bayt Al Ghaf house preparations

Bayt Al Ghaf has 4 named house preparations: Bezar Spice Blend (contains none of the 14 major allergens), Al Ghaf House Red (contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts), Al Ghaf House Green (contains none of the 14 major allergens) and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame). Guests sometimes say “the house sauce” for Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green or Sauce No. 7, which are three different sauces.

## Bezar Spice Blend in short

Bezar Spice Blend (بزار) is a spice blend. The Emirati house spice blend that seasons our machboos, saloona and grills. Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house every week. Bezar Spice Blend contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Bezar Spice Blend is served with Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Sambousa Trio, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Lamb Thareed, Saloonat Dajaj, Saloonat Samak (Monday), Margooga Laham (Tuesday), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Camel Kofta, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, Vegetable Machboos, Bamia Saloona, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Bezar Spice Blend is part of Hashu Topping, Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a), Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade and Laban Marinade.

## Hashu Topping in short

Hashu Topping (حشو) is a topping. Slow-caramelised onions with golden raisins, ghee and a little bezar — the sweet-savoury crown of a traditional machboos. Hashu Topping contains milk (dairy) and sulphites and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Hashu Topping is served with Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Hashu Topping can be added as an extra to Vegetable Machboos.

## Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce in short

Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce (دقوس) is a sauce. The Gulf's classic tomato and chilli sauce, served on the side of every machboos so guests can add heat to taste. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce is served with Sambousa Trio, Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Hammour Mashwi, Vegetable Machboos, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce can be added as an extra to Sambousa Trio, Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Saffron Shish Tawook and Vegetable Machboos.

## Mehyawa in short

Mehyawa (مهياوة) is a sauce. A pungent, savoury fermented fish sauce from the Gulf coast, traditionally spread on regag with egg and cheese. Mehyawa contains fish and mustard and may contain traces of celery. Mehyawa is served with Mehyawa Regag Bites. Mehyawa can be added as an extra to Regag with Egg & Cheese.

## Saffron Yogurt Sauce in short

Saffron Yogurt Sauce (صلصة الزبادي بالزعفران) is a sauce. Cool, garlicky yogurt sauce scented with saffron and mint. Saffron Yogurt Sauce contains milk (dairy). Saffron Yogurt Sauce can be added as an extra to Lamb Machboos and Chicken Machboos.

## Toum Garlic Sauce in short

Toum Garlic Sauce (ثومية) is a dip. Fluffy whipped garlic sauce made without egg or dairy. Toum Garlic Sauce contains none of the 14 major allergens. Toum Garlic Sauce is served with Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Saffron Shish Tawook. Toum Garlic Sauce can be added as an extra to Rubyan Mashwi, Saffron Shish Tawook and Camel Kofta.

## Tahini Lemon Sauce in short

Tahini Lemon Sauce (صلصة الطحينة) is a sauce. Silky sesame sauce with lemon and garlic. Tahini Lemon Sauce contains sesame and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Tahini Lemon Sauce is not currently served with any dish.

## Loomi Aioli in short

Loomi Aioli (أيولي باللومي) is a sauce. House-made egg-yolk aioli seasoned with ground dried black lime. Loomi Aioli contains eggs and mustard. Loomi Aioli is served with Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli. Loomi Aioli can be added as an extra to Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

## Dibs (Date Syrup) in short

Dibs (Date Syrup) (دبس) is a syrup. Pure date syrup from Emirati dates, served warm over luqaimat and chebab. Dibs (Date Syrup) contains none of the 14 major allergens. Dibs (Date Syrup) is served with Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Luqaimat, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Dibs (Date Syrup) can be added as an extra to Regag with Egg & Cheese.

## Saffron Cardamom Syrup in short

Saffron Cardamom Syrup (شيرة الزعفران والهيل) is a syrup. Light sugar syrup infused with saffron, cardamom and rosewater. Saffron Cardamom Syrup contains none of the 14 major allergens. Saffron Cardamom Syrup is served with Khanfaroosh. Saffron Cardamom Syrup can be added as an extra to Luqaimat.

## Al Ghaf House Red in short

Al Ghaf House Red (صلصة الغاف الحمراء) is a sauce. One of our two house sauces: a thick roasted-pepper and walnut sauce sharpened with pomegranate molasses. It contains walnuts and wheat breadcrumbs. Al Ghaf House Red contains gluten (wheat) and tree nuts and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Al Ghaf House Red is served with Camel Kofta.

## Al Ghaf House Green in short

Al Ghaf House Green (صلصة الغاف الخضراء) is a sauce. Our second house sauce: a vivid herb sauce of coriander, parsley, mint and green chilli with lime. It has no nut, dairy, gluten or sesame ingredients, but it is made on the same station as the walnut-based House Red. Al Ghaf House Green contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of gluten (wheat), tree nuts, celery and mustard. Al Ghaf House Green is served with Rubyan Mashwi.

## Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) in short

Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) (صلصة سبعة) is a glaze. The chef's signature glaze, named for its seven key flavours: tamarind, date, soy, sesame, garlic, ginger and bezar. It contains soy, wheat (gluten, from the soy sauce) and sesame. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) contains gluten (wheat), soy and sesame and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) is served with Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

## Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade in short

Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade (تتبيلة السمك بالبزار والليمون) is a marinade. Bright marinade for fish: lime juice, bezar, turmeric and garlic. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade contains none of the 14 major allergens and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade is served with Saloonat Samak (Monday), Muhammar with Fried Safi and Hammour Mashwi.

## Laban Marinade in short

Laban Marinade (تتبيلة اللبن) is a marinade. Yogurt marinade that tenderises our lamb chops overnight. Laban Marinade contains milk (dairy) and may contain traces of celery and mustard. Laban Marinade is served with Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops).

## Saffron Tawook Marinade in short

Saffron Tawook Marinade (تتبيلة الطاووق بالزعفران) is a marinade. Yogurt, saffron, lemon and paprika marinade for chicken tawook. Saffron Tawook Marinade contains milk (dairy). Saffron Tawook Marinade is served with Saffron Shish Tawook.

## Garlic Lime Butter in short

Garlic Lime Butter (زبدة الثوم والليمون) is a sauce. Melted butter with garlic, lime, parsley and a pinch of chilli, spooned over grilled prawns. Garlic Lime Butter contains milk (dairy). Garlic Lime Butter is served with Rubyan Mashwi.

## Pistachio Cardamom Crumb in short

Pistachio Cardamom Crumb (فتات الفستق بالهيل) is a topping. Crushed pistachios with sugar and cardamom, scattered over desserts. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb contains tree nuts. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb is served with Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream. Pistachio Cardamom Crumb can be added as an extra to Date & Cardamom Shake.

## Loomi & Sumac Dressing in short

Loomi & Sumac Dressing (صلصة اللومي والسماق) is a dressing. Olive-oil dressing with lime, ground loomi, sumac and pomegranate molasses. Loomi & Sumac Dressing contains none of the 14 major allergens. Loomi & Sumac Dressing is served with Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day). Loomi & Sumac Dressing can be added as an extra to Hammour Mashwi.
