Sambousa pastry sheets (Arabic: رقائق السمبوسة) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Thin wheat-flour pastry sheets (wheat flour, water, sunflower oil, salt).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
