# Saffron (زعفران)

Saffron as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Saffron: description, allergens and diet

Saffron (Arabic: زعفران) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Iranian saffron threads (sargol grade).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 12 dishes and 3 preparations

## Dishes that use Saffron

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 0.1 g of saffron.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 0.05 g of saffron.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 0.15 g of saffron.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) uses 0.1 g of saffron.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) uses 0.05 g of saffron.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses 0.05 g of saffron.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) uses 0.05 g of saffron.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) uses 0.1 g of saffron.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) uses 0.02 g of saffron.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 0.1 g of saffron.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 0.2 g of saffron.

## Sauces and preparations that use Saffron

- Saffron Yogurt Sauce uses 0.3 g, bloomed in warm water of saffron per batch.
- Saffron Cardamom Syrup uses 0.3 g of saffron per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 0.2 g of saffron per batch.

## Dishes that contain Saffron through a preparation

These dishes contain saffron because one of their preparations does:

- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade
