# Tomato paste (معجون الطماط)

Tomato paste as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Tomato paste: description, allergens and diet

Tomato paste (Arabic: معجون الطماط) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Double-concentrated tomato paste (tomatoes, salt).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 18 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Tomato paste

- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 10 g of tomato paste.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 10 g of tomato paste.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) uses 10 g of tomato paste.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 10 g of tomato paste.

## Sauces and preparations that use Tomato paste

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 40 g of tomato paste per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 15 g of tomato paste per batch.
