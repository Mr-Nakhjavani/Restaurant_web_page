# Rapeseed (canola) oil (زيت الكانولا)

Rapeseed (canola) oil as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Rapeseed (canola) oil: description, allergens and diet

Rapeseed (canola) oil (Arabic: زيت الكانولا) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Cold-pressed rapeseed oil.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Rapeseed (canola) oil

Rapeseed (canola) oil is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Rapeseed (canola) oil

- Loomi Aioli uses 300 ml of rapeseed (canola) oil per batch.

## Dishes that contain Rapeseed (canola) oil through a preparation

These dishes contain rapeseed (canola) oil because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Loomi Aioli
