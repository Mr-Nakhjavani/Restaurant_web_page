# Date syrup (dibs) (دبس التمر)

Date syrup (dibs) as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Date syrup (dibs): description, allergens and diet

Date syrup (dibs) (Arabic: دبس التمر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Pure date syrup (100% dates).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: dibs, date molasses
- Used in 12 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Date syrup (dibs)

- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) uses 30 ml of date syrup (dibs).
- Date & Cardamom Shake (AED 28) uses 15 ml of date syrup (dibs).

## Sauces and preparations that use Date syrup (dibs)

- Dibs (Date Syrup) uses 100% of date syrup (dibs) per batch.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 120 ml of date syrup (dibs) per batch.
