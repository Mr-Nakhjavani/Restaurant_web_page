# Chickpeas (حمص حب)

Chickpeas as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Chickpeas: description, allergens and diet

Chickpeas (Arabic: حمص حب) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Dried chickpeas soaked overnight and boiled.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: garbanzo, dango, nakhi
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Chickpeas

- Nakhi (AED 22) uses 180 g (cooked weight) of chickpeas.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 150 g of chickpeas.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 60 g of chickpeas.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 80 g of chickpeas.

## Leaving out or adding Chickpeas

- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)” adds chickpeas (−AED 5 (cheaper)).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Vegetable machboos” adds chickpeas (no extra charge).
