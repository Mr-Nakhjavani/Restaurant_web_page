# Garlic (ثوم)

Garlic as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Garlic: description, allergens and diet

Garlic (Arabic: ثوم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh garlic.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 25 dishes and 12 preparations

## Dishes that use Garlic

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 5 g of garlic.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 3 g of garlic.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 4 g of garlic.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 4 g of garlic.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 5 g of garlic.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 6 g of garlic.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 6 g of garlic.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 6 g of garlic.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 6 g of garlic.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 10 g of garlic.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 5 g of garlic.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 20 g of garlic.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 25 g of garlic.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 80 g of garlic.

## Sauces and preparations that use Garlic

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 25 g of garlic per batch.
- Saffron Yogurt Sauce uses 8 g of garlic per batch.
- Toum Garlic Sauce uses 120 g of garlic per batch.
- Tahini Lemon Sauce uses 6 g of garlic per batch.
- Loomi Aioli uses 6 g of garlic per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Red uses 10 g of garlic per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Green uses 10 g of garlic per batch.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 10 g of garlic per batch.
- Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade uses 15 g of garlic per batch.
- Laban Marinade uses 15 g of garlic per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 15 g of garlic per batch.
- Garlic Lime Butter uses 20 g of garlic per batch.

## Dishes that contain Garlic through a preparation

These dishes contain garlic because one of their preparations does:

- Sambousa Trio, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Muhammar with Fried Safi, through Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade
- Hammour Mashwi, through Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade
- Rubyan Mashwi, through Garlic Lime Butter
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Laban Marinade
- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade
- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce

## Leaving out or adding Garlic

- Rubyan Mashwi: choosing “No butter (grilled with olive oil)” adds garlic (no extra charge).
