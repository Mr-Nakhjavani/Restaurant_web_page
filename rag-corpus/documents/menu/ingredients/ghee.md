# Ghee (clarified butter) (سمن)

Ghee (clarified butter) as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Ghee (clarified butter): description, allergens and diet

Ghee (clarified butter) (Arabic: سمن) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Cow's-milk ghee; a dairy product.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Also called: samn
- Used in 18 dishes and 1 preparation

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 15 g of ghee (clarified butter).
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 10 g (griddle) of ghee (clarified butter).
- Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) uses 5 g of ghee (clarified butter).
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 25 g of ghee (clarified butter).

## Leaving out or adding Ghee (clarified butter)

- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)” removes ghee (clarified butter).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Vegetable machboos” removes ghee (clarified butter).
