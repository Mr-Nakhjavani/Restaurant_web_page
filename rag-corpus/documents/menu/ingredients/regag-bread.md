# Regag bread (house-made) (خبز رقاق)

Regag bread (house-made) as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Regag bread (house-made): description, allergens and diet

Regag bread (house-made) (Arabic: خبز رقاق) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Paper-thin crisp Emirati bread baked to order on a hot iron plate from wheat flour, water and salt.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
- Also called: rgag, raqaq
- Used in 7 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Regag bread (house-made)

- Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) uses 2 sheets (about 60 g) of regag bread (house-made).
- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 1 sheet (about 30 g), on the side of regag bread (house-made); it can be left out on request.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 2 sheets of regag bread (house-made).
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 1 sheet, on the side of regag bread (house-made); it can be left out on request.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26) uses 2 sheets (about 50 g) of regag bread (house-made).
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 80 g of regag bread (house-made).
- Camel Kofta (AED 76) uses 1 sheet of regag bread (house-made); it can be left out on request.
