# Ginger (زنجبيل)

Ginger as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Ginger: description, allergens and diet

Ginger (Arabic: زنجبيل) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh ginger root.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 8 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Ginger

- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 3 g of ginger.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 4 g of ginger.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 4 g of ginger.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 4 g of ginger.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 12 g of ginger.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 15 g of ginger.

## Sauces and preparations that use Ginger

- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 10 g of ginger per batch.

## Dishes that contain Ginger through a preparation

These dishes contain ginger because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

## Questions guests ask about Ginger

**Q: Do you use ginger?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses ginger in Saloonat Dajaj, Lamb Machboos, Vegetable Machboos, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a).

**Q: Which dishes contain ginger?**

A: Saloonat Dajaj, Lamb Machboos, Vegetable Machboos, Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Is ginger an allergen?**

A: Ginger is not one of the 14 major allergens.
