## Brioche bun: description, allergens and diet

Brioche bun (Arabic: خبز البريوش) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Bakery brioche bun made with wheat flour, eggs, butter and milk, topped with sesame seeds.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: gluten (wheat), eggs, milk (dairy) and sesame
- May contain traces of: soy
- Used in 1 dish and 0 preparations

Supplied by a partner bakery; the supplier label states it may contain soy.
