# Loomi (dried black lime) (لومي)

Loomi (dried black lime) as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Loomi (dried black lime): description, allergens and diet

Loomi (dried black lime) (Arabic: لومي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Whole limes boiled in brine and sun-dried until black; used pierced whole or ground for a sour, smoky flavour.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: dried lemon, dried lime, black lime, noomi basra
- Used in 20 dishes and 3 preparations

## Sauces and preparations that use Loomi (dried black lime)

- Loomi Aioli uses 6 g, ground of loomi (dried black lime) per batch.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 3 g, ground of loomi (dried black lime) per batch.
