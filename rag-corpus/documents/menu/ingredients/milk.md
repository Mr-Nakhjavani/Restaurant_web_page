# Full-cream milk (حليب)

Full-cream milk as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Full-cream milk: description, allergens and diet

Full-cream milk (Arabic: حليب) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh full-cream cow's milk.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Full-cream milk

- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 80 ml of full-cream milk.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 50 ml (chebab batter) of full-cream milk.
- Date & Cardamom Shake (AED 28) uses 250 ml of full-cream milk.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 80 ml of full-cream milk.

## Leaving out or adding Full-cream milk

- Date & Cardamom Shake: choosing “Camel milk” removes full-cream milk.
