# Turmeric (كركم)

Turmeric as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Turmeric: description, allergens and diet

Turmeric (Arabic: كركم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Ground turmeric.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 27 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Turmeric

- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 0.5 g of turmeric.
- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 0.5 g of turmeric.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 1 g of turmeric.

## Sauces and preparations that use Turmeric

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 10 g of turmeric per batch.
- Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade uses 3 g of turmeric per batch.
