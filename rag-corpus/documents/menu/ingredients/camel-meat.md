## Camel meat: description, allergens and diet

Camel meat (Arabic: لحم إبل) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Halal young camel (hashi) shoulder meat from UAE farms, slow-braised until tender.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: camel, hashi
- Used in 2 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Camel meat

- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 280 g (raw weight) of camel meat.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 560 g (raw weight) of camel meat.
