# Butter (زبدة)

Butter as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Butter: description, allergens and diet

Butter (Arabic: زبدة) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Unsalted cow's-milk butter.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Butter

Butter is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Butter

- Garlic Lime Butter uses 100 g of butter per batch.

## Dishes that contain Butter through a preparation

These dishes contain butter because one of their preparations does:

- Rubyan Mashwi, through Garlic Lime Butter

## Questions guests ask about Butter

**Q: Do you use butter?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses butter in Garlic Lime Butter.

**Q: Which dishes contain butter?**

A: Rubyan Mashwi.

**Q: Is butter an allergen?**

A: Butter contains milk (dairy).
