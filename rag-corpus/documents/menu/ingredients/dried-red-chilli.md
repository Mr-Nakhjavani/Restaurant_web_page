# Dried red chilli (فلفل أحمر مجفف)

Dried red chilli as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Dried red chilli: description, allergens and diet

Dried red chilli (Arabic: فلفل أحمر مجفف) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Whole dried red chillies, ground in-house.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 28 dishes and 4 preparations

## Dishes that use Dried red chilli

- Nakhi (AED 22) uses 1 g of dried red chilli; it can be left out on request.

## Sauces and preparations that use Dried red chilli

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 5 g of dried red chilli per batch.
- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 6 g of dried red chilli per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Red uses 4 g of dried red chilli per batch.
- Garlic Lime Butter uses 2 g of dried red chilli per batch.
