# Green chilli (فلفل أخضر حار)

Green chilli as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Green chilli: description, allergens and diet

Green chilli (Arabic: فلفل أخضر حار) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh green chillies.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 17 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Green chilli

- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 5 g of green chilli; it can be left out on request.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 5 g of green chilli; it can be left out on request.
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 3 g of green chilli.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 6 g of green chilli.

## Sauces and preparations that use Green chilli

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 15 g of green chilli per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Green uses 25 g of green chilli per batch.

## Dishes that contain Green chilli through a preparation

These dishes contain green chilli because one of their preparations does:

- Lamb Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Chicken Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Muhammar with Fried Safi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Camel Machboos (Thursday), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Hammour Mashwi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Rubyan Mashwi, through Al Ghaf House Green
- Vegetable Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce

## Leaving out or adding Green chilli

- Bayd w Tamat: green chilli can be left out on request.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: green chilli can be left out on request.
