## Fennel seed: description, allergens and diet

Fennel seed (Arabic: شمر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fennel seed.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 27 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Fennel seed

Fennel seed is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Fennel seed

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 10 g of fennel seed per batch.
- Mehyawa uses 4 g of fennel seed per batch.
