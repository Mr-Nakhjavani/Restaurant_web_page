# Mint (نعناع)

Mint as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Mint: description, allergens and diet

Mint (Arabic: نعناع) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh mint.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 6 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Mint

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) uses 3 g of mint.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) uses 2 g of mint.
- Chilled Laban with Mint (AED 14) uses 2 g of mint.
- Limon Na'na (AED 22) uses 6 g of mint.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 6 g of mint.

## Sauces and preparations that use Mint

- Saffron Yogurt Sauce uses 5 g of mint per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Green uses 30 g of mint per batch.

## Dishes that contain Mint through a preparation

These dishes contain mint because one of their preparations does:

- Rubyan Mashwi, through Al Ghaf House Green

## Leaving out or adding Mint

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: choosing “2 sulaimani (no milk)” adds mint (no extra charge).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Sulaimani” adds mint (no extra charge).

## Questions guests ask about Mint

**Q: Do you use mint?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses mint in Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na, Majlis Sharing Platter, Saffron Yogurt Sauce and Al Ghaf House Green.

**Q: Which dishes contain mint?**

A: Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Sulaimani, Chilled Laban with Mint, Limon Na'na, Majlis Sharing Platter and Rubyan Mashwi.

**Q: Is mint an allergen?**

A: Mint is not one of the 14 major allergens.
