# Processed cheese (جبن مطبوخ)

Processed cheese as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Processed cheese: description, allergens and diet

Processed cheese (Arabic: جبن مطبوخ) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Processed cow's-milk cheese slices, the classic regag filling.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Processed cheese

- Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) uses 30 g of processed cheese; it can be left out on request.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 30 g of processed cheese.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26) uses 20 g of processed cheese.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72) uses 1 slice (20 g) of processed cheese; it can be left out on request.

## Leaving out or adding Processed cheese

- Regag with Egg & Cheese: processed cheese can be left out on request.
