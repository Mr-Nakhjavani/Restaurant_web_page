# Toasted sesame oil (زيت السمسم)

Toasted sesame oil as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Toasted sesame oil: description, allergens and diet

Toasted sesame oil (Arabic: زيت السمسم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Toasted sesame oil.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: sesame
- Used in 2 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Toasted sesame oil

Toasted sesame oil is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Toasted sesame oil

- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 15 ml of toasted sesame oil per batch.

## Dishes that contain Toasted sesame oil through a preparation

These dishes contain toasted sesame oil because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

## Questions guests ask about Toasted sesame oil

**Q: Do you use toasted sesame oil?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses toasted sesame oil in Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a).

**Q: Which dishes contain toasted sesame oil?**

A: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Is toasted sesame oil an allergen?**

A: Toasted sesame oil contains sesame.
