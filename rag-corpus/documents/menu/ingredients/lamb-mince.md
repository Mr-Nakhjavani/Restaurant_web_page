# Minced lamb (لحم غنم مفروم)

Minced lamb as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Minced lamb: description, allergens and diet

Minced lamb (Arabic: لحم غنم مفروم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Halal lamb shoulder minced in-house.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Minced lamb

- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 50 g (lamb filling) of minced lamb.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 70 g of minced lamb; it can be left out on request.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 70 g of minced lamb.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 25 g of minced lamb.

## Leaving out or adding Minced lamb

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: minced lamb can be left out on request.
- Sambousa Trio: choosing “Vegetarian (3 cheese, 3 vegetable)” removes minced lamb.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: choosing “No lamb (vegetarian hummus)” removes minced lamb.
