# Golden raisins (زبيب)

Golden raisins as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Golden raisins: description, allergens and diet

Golden raisins (Arabic: زبيب) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Golden raisins preserved with sulphur dioxide (contain sulphites).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: sulphites
- Used in 9 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Golden raisins

Golden raisins are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Golden raisins

- Hashu Topping uses 80 g of golden raisins per batch.

## Dishes that contain Golden raisins through a preparation

These dishes contain golden raisins because one of their preparations does:

- Lamb Machboos, through Hashu Topping
- Chicken Machboos, through Hashu Topping
- Camel Machboos (Thursday), through Hashu Topping
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Hashu Topping
- Friday Family Machboos Platter, through Hashu Topping
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Hashu Topping
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Hashu Topping
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person), through Hashu Topping
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Hashu Topping

## Leaving out or adding Golden raisins

- Lamb Machboos: choosing “Hashu without raisins” removes golden raisins.
- Chicken Machboos: choosing “Hashu without raisins” removes golden raisins.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday): choosing “Hashu without raisins” removes golden raisins.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter: choosing “Hashu without raisins” removes golden raisins.

## Questions guests ask about Golden raisins

**Q: Do you use golden raisins?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses golden raisins in Hashu Topping.

**Q: Which dishes contain golden raisins?**

A: Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Is golden raisins an allergen?**

A: Golden raisins contain sulphites.
