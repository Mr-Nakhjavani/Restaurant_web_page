# Sunflower oil (زيت دوار الشمس)

Sunflower oil as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Sunflower oil: description, allergens and diet

Sunflower oil (Arabic: زيت دوار الشمس) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Refined sunflower oil used for cooking and in the fryers.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 23 dishes and 3 preparations

## Dishes that use Sunflower oil

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 5 ml (omelette pan) of sunflower oil.
- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 15 ml of sunflower oil.
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 15 ml of sunflower oil.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 15 ml of sunflower oil.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 20 ml of sunflower oil.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 15 ml of sunflower oil.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses for frying and cooking of sunflower oil.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses for frying of sunflower oil.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses for frying of sunflower oil.

## Sauces and preparations that use Sunflower oil

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 20 ml of sunflower oil per batch.
- Toum Garlic Sauce uses 500 ml of sunflower oil per batch.
- Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade uses 40 ml of sunflower oil per batch.

## Dishes that contain Sunflower oil through a preparation

These dishes contain sunflower oil because one of their preparations does:

- Lamb Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Chicken Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Camel Machboos (Thursday), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Hammour Mashwi, through Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Toum Garlic Sauce
- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Toum Garlic Sauce
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
