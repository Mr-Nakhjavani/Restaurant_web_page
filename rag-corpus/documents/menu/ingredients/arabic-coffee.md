## Arabic coffee beans (light roast): description, allergens and diet

Arabic coffee beans (light roast) (Arabic: بن عربي) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Lightly roasted Arabica beans, ground for gahwa each morning.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
