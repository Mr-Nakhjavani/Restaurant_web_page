# Parsley (بقدونس)

Parsley as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Parsley: description, allergens and diet

Parsley (Arabic: بقدونس) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh flat-leaf parsley.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 3 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Parsley

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 2 g of parsley.
- Camel Kofta (AED 76) uses 5 g of parsley.

## Sauces and preparations that use Parsley

- Al Ghaf House Green uses 60 g of parsley per batch.
- Garlic Lime Butter uses 5 g of parsley per batch.

## Dishes that contain Parsley through a preparation

These dishes contain parsley because one of their preparations does:

- Rubyan Mashwi, through Garlic Lime Butter
