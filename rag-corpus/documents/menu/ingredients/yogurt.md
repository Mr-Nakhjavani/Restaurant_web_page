# Natural yogurt (زبادي)

Natural yogurt as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Natural yogurt: description, allergens and diet

Natural yogurt (Arabic: زبادي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Plain full-fat cow's-milk yogurt.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 2 dishes and 3 preparations

## Dishes that use Natural yogurt

Natural yogurt is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Natural yogurt

- Saffron Yogurt Sauce uses 500 g of natural yogurt per batch.
- Laban Marinade uses 300 g of natural yogurt per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 250 g of natural yogurt per batch.

## Dishes that contain Natural yogurt through a preparation

These dishes contain natural yogurt because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Laban Marinade
- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade

## Questions guests ask about Natural yogurt

**Q: Do you use natural yogurt?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses natural yogurt in Saffron Yogurt Sauce, Laban Marinade and Saffron Tawook Marinade.

**Q: Which dishes contain natural yogurt?**

A: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Saffron Shish Tawook.

**Q: Is natural yogurt an allergen?**

A: Natural yogurt contains milk (dairy).
