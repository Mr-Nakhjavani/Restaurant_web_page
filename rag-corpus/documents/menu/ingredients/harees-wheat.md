# Hulled wheat (harees wheat) (قمح الهريس)

## Hulled wheat (harees wheat): description, allergens and diet

Hulled wheat (harees wheat) (Arabic: قمح الهريس) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Whole hulled wheat grains soaked overnight and slow-cooked for harees.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
- Also called: jareesh, whole wheat grain
- Used in 2 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Hulled wheat (harees wheat)

- Harees (AED 48) uses 120 g (dry weight) of hulled wheat (harees wheat).
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 240 g (dry weight) of hulled wheat (harees wheat).

## Leaving out or adding Hulled wheat (harees wheat)

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Harees” adds hulled wheat (harees wheat) (no extra charge).
