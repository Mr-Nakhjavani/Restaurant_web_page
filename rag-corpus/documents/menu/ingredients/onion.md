# Onion (بصل)

Onion as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Onion: description, allergens and diet

Onion (Arabic: بصل) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Brown and red onions.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 25 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Onion

- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 50 g of onion.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 50 g of onion.
- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 40 g of onion.
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 30 g of onion.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) uses 15 g (red onion) of onion; it can be left out on request.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 40 g of onion.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 40 g of onion.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 50 g of onion.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) uses 40 g of onion.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (AED 54) uses 40 g of onion.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 60 g of onion.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 60 g of onion.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 60 g of onion.
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118) uses 1/2 onion, grilled of onion.
- Camel Kofta (AED 76) uses 40 g of onion.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72) uses 30 g, caramelised of onion.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 60 g of onion.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 40 g of onion.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 40 g of onion.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 300 g of onion.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 300 g of onion.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 1 kg of onion.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses 60 g of onion.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 80 g of onion.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 150 g of onion.

## Sauces and preparations that use Onion

- Hashu Topping uses 500 g, thinly sliced of onion per batch.
- Laban Marinade uses 50 g, grated of onion per batch.

## Leaving out or adding Onion

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): onion can be left out on request.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad): choosing “No onion” removes onion.
