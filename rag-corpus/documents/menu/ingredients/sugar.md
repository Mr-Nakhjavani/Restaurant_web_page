# Sugar (سكر)

Sugar as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Sugar: description, allergens and diet

Sugar (Arabic: سكر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. White cane sugar.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 22 dishes and 4 preparations

## Dishes that use Sugar

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 25 g of sugar.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 15 g of sugar.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 45 g (balaleet, chebab and karak) of sugar.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) uses 10 g of sugar.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) uses 10 g of sugar.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses 30 g of sugar.
- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36) uses 25 g of sugar.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) uses 25 g of sugar.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) uses 15 g of sugar; it can be left out on request.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) uses 10 g of sugar; it can be left out on request.
- Limon Na'na (AED 22) uses 25 g of sugar; it can be left out on request.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 20 g of sugar.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 20 g of sugar.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 25 g of sugar.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 40 g of sugar.

## Sauces and preparations that use Sugar

- Hashu Topping uses 10 g of sugar per batch.
- Saffron Cardamom Syrup uses 500 g of sugar per batch.
- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 20 g of sugar per batch.
- Pistachio Cardamom Crumb uses 20 g of sugar per batch.

## Dishes that contain Sugar through a preparation

These dishes contain sugar because one of their preparations does:

- Lamb Machboos, through Hashu Topping
- Chicken Machboos, through Hashu Topping
- Camel Machboos (Thursday), through Hashu Topping
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Hashu Topping
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Hashu Topping

## Leaving out or adding Sugar

- Karak Chai: sugar can be left out on request.
- Sulaimani: sugar can be left out on request.
- Limon Na'na: sugar can be left out on request.
- Karak Chai: choosing “No sugar” removes sugar.
- Sulaimani: choosing “No sugar” removes sugar.
- Limon Na'na: choosing “No sugar” removes sugar.
