# Sumac (سماق)

Sumac as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Sumac: description, allergens and diet

Sumac (Arabic: سماق) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Ground sumac berries.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 8 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Sumac

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 0.5 g of sumac.
- Camel Kofta (AED 76) uses 1 g of sumac.

## Sauces and preparations that use Sumac

- Loomi & Sumac Dressing uses 4 g of sumac per batch.

## Dishes that contain Sumac through a preparation

These dishes contain sumac because one of their preparations does:

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Friday Family Machboos Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing

## Questions guests ask about Sumac
