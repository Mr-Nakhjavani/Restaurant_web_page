# Evaporated milk (حليب مبخر)

Evaporated milk as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Evaporated milk: description, allergens and diet

Evaporated milk (Arabic: حليب مبخر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Unsweetened evaporated cow's milk, the traditional base for karak.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 3 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Evaporated milk

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 120 ml (karak) of evaporated milk.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) uses 60 ml of evaporated milk.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 60 ml of evaporated milk.

## Leaving out or adding Evaporated milk

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: choosing “2 sulaimani (no milk)” removes evaporated milk.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: choosing “Dallah of gahwa instead” removes evaporated milk.
- Karak Chai: choosing “Camel milk” removes evaporated milk.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Sulaimani” removes evaporated milk.
