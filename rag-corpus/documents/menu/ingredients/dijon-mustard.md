# Dijon mustard (خردل ديجون)

Dijon mustard as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Dijon mustard: description, allergens and diet

Dijon mustard (Arabic: خردل ديجون) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Prepared mustard (mustard seed, vinegar, salt).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: mustard
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Dijon mustard

Dijon mustard is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Dijon mustard

- Loomi Aioli uses 10 g of dijon mustard per batch.

## Dishes that contain Dijon mustard through a preparation

These dishes contain dijon mustard because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Loomi Aioli

## Questions guests ask about Dijon mustard
