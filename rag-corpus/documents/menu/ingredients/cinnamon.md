# Cinnamon (دارسين)

Cinnamon as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Cinnamon: description, allergens and diet

Cinnamon (Arabic: دارسين) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Cassia cinnamon sticks and ground cinnamon.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 27 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Cinnamon

- Harees (AED 48) uses 0.5 g of cinnamon.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 1 stick of cinnamon.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 1 stick of cinnamon.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 1 stick of cinnamon.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 1 stick of cinnamon.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 2 sticks of cinnamon.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 3 sticks of cinnamon.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 6 sticks of cinnamon.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 2 sticks of cinnamon.

## Sauces and preparations that use Cinnamon

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 8 g of cinnamon per batch.

## Questions guests ask about Cinnamon

**Q: Do you use cinnamon?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses cinnamon in Harees, Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Vegetable Machboos, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) and Bezar Spice Blend.

**Q: Which dishes contain cinnamon?**
