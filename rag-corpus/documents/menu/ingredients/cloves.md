# Cloves (قرنفل)

Cloves as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Cloves: description, allergens and diet

Cloves (Arabic: قرنفل) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Whole cloves.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 27 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Cloves

- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 2 of cloves.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 2 of cloves.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 2 of cloves.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 2 of cloves.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) uses 0.5 g of cloves.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 5 of cloves.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 6 of cloves.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 12 of cloves.

## Sauces and preparations that use Cloves

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 3 g of cloves per batch.
