# Tahini (طحينة)

Tahini as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Tahini: description, allergens and diet

Tahini (Arabic: طحينة) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Ground sesame paste.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: sesame
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Tahini

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 30 g of tahini.

## Sauces and preparations that use Tahini

- Tahini Lemon Sauce uses 250 g of tahini per batch.

## Questions guests ask about Tahini

**Q: Do you use tahini?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses tahini in Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts and Tahini Lemon Sauce.

**Q: Which dishes contain tahini?**

A: Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts.

**Q: Is tahini an allergen?**

A: Tahini contains sesame.
