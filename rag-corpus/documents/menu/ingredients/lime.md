# Lime (ليمون أخضر)

Lime as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Lime: description, allergens and diet

Lime (Arabic: ليمون أخضر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh limes and lime juice.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 19 dishes and 5 preparations

## Dishes that use Lime

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 1 wedge of lime.
- Nakhi (AED 22) uses 1/4 lime of lime.
- Hammour Mashwi (AED 98) uses 1/2 lime of lime.
- Rubyan Mashwi (AED 105) uses 1/2 lime of lime.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) uses 1 wedge of lime.

## Sauces and preparations that use Lime

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 30 ml juice of lime per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Green uses 50 ml juice of lime per batch.
- Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade uses 60 ml juice of lime per batch.
- Garlic Lime Butter uses 20 ml juice of lime per batch.
- Loomi & Sumac Dressing uses 50 ml juice of lime per batch.

## Dishes that contain Lime through a preparation

These dishes contain lime because one of their preparations does:

- Sambousa Trio, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Saloonat Samak (Monday), through Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade
- Lamb Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Chicken Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Muhammar with Fried Safi, through Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade
- Camel Machboos (Thursday), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Vegetable Machboos, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Friday Family Machboos Platter, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce

## Leaving out or adding Lime

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: choosing “2 sulaimani (no milk)” adds lime (no extra charge).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Sulaimani” adds lime (no extra charge).

## Questions guests ask about Lime

**Q: Do you use lime?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses lime in Shorbat Adas, Nakhi, Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Sulaimani, Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce, Al Ghaf House Green, Bezar & Lime Fish Marinade, Garlic Lime Butter and Loomi & Sumac Dressing.

**Q: Which dishes contain lime?**

A: Shorbat Adas, Nakhi, Hammour Mashwi, Rubyan Mashwi, Sulaimani, Sambousa Trio, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Saloonat Samak (Monday), Lamb Machboos, Chicken Machboos, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Camel Machboos (Thursday), Vegetable Machboos, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box, Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Is lime an allergen?**

A: Lime is not one of the 14 major allergens.
