## Lamb stock (house-made): description, allergens and diet

Lamb stock (house-made) (Arabic: مرق اللحم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Stock simmered from lamb bones with onion, loomi, cinnamon, cardamom and salt.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 7 dishes and 0 preparations
