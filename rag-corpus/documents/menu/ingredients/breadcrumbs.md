# Breadcrumbs (فتات الخبز)

Breadcrumbs as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Breadcrumbs: description, allergens and diet

Breadcrumbs (Arabic: فتات الخبز) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Dried white wheat breadcrumbs (wheat flour, yeast, salt).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Breadcrumbs

Breadcrumbs are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Breadcrumbs

- Al Ghaf House Red uses 60 g of breadcrumbs per batch.

## Dishes that contain Breadcrumbs through a preparation

These dishes contain breadcrumbs because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red

## Questions guests ask about Breadcrumbs

**Q: Do you use breadcrumbs?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses breadcrumbs in Al Ghaf House Red.

**Q: Which dishes contain breadcrumbs?**

A: Camel Kofta.

**Q: Is breadcrumbs an allergen?**

A: Breadcrumbs contain gluten (wheat).
