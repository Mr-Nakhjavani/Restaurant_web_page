# Tamarind (تمر هندي)

Tamarind as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Tamarind: description, allergens and diet

Tamarind (Arabic: تمر هندي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Seedless tamarind pulp.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 3 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Tamarind

- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 10 g of tamarind.

## Sauces and preparations that use Tamarind

- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 100 g of tamarind per batch.

## Dishes that contain Tamarind through a preparation

These dishes contain tamarind because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

## Questions guests ask about Tamarind

**Q: Do you use tamarind?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses tamarind in Saloonat Samak (Monday) and Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a).

**Q: Which dishes contain tamarind?**

A: Saloonat Samak (Monday), Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Is tamarind an allergen?**

A: Tamarind is not one of the 14 major allergens.
