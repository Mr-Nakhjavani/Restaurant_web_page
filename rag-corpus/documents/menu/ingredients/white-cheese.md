# White brined cheese (akkawi) (جبنة عكاوي)

## White brined cheese (akkawi): description, allergens and diet

White brined cheese (akkawi) (Arabic: جبنة عكاوي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Mild white brined cow's-milk cheese, soaked to reduce salt.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use White brined cheese (akkawi)

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 80 g of white brined cheese (akkawi).
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 40 g (cheese filling) of white brined cheese (akkawi).
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 50 g of white brined cheese (akkawi).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 20 g of white brined cheese (akkawi).

## Leaving out or adding White brined cheese (akkawi)
