# Pine nuts (صنوبر)

Pine nuts as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Pine nuts: description, allergens and diet

Pine nuts (Arabic: صنوبر) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Toasted pine nuts. Botanically seeds, but we label them as tree nuts.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: tree nuts
- Used in 2 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Pine nuts

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 8 g of pine nuts; it can be left out on request.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 60 g of pine nuts; it can be left out on request.

## Leaving out or adding Pine nuts

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: pine nuts can be left out on request.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi: pine nuts can be left out on request.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts: choosing “No pine nuts” removes pine nuts.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi: choosing “No nut garnish” removes pine nuts.
