## Milk powder: description, allergens and diet

Milk powder (Arabic: حليب مجفف) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Full-cream cow's milk powder.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 6 dishes and 0 preparations
