# Sesame seeds (سمسم)

Sesame seeds as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Sesame seeds: description, allergens and diet

Sesame seeds (Arabic: سمسم) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Toasted white sesame seeds.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: sesame
- Used in 6 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Sesame seeds

- Luqaimat (AED 32) uses 3 g of sesame seeds; it can be left out on request.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 6 g of sesame seeds.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 6 g of sesame seeds.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses 1 g of sesame seeds.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 2 g of sesame seeds.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 6 g of sesame seeds.

## Leaving out or adding Sesame seeds

- Luqaimat: sesame seeds can be left out on request.
- Luqaimat: choosing “No sesame seeds” removes sesame seeds.
