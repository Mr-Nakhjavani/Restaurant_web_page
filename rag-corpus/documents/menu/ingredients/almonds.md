## Almonds: description, allergens and diet

Almonds (Arabic: لوز) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Blanched almonds, fried until golden.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: tree nuts
- Used in 1 dish and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Almonds

- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 120 g of almonds; it can be left out on request.

## Leaving out or adding Almonds

- Whole Lamb Ouzi: almonds can be left out on request.
