# Lamb shoulder (كتف غنم)

Lamb shoulder as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Lamb shoulder: description, allergens and diet

Lamb shoulder (Arabic: كتف غنم) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Halal lamb shoulder, bone-in unless stated, from Australian and local farms.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 8 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Lamb shoulder

- Harees (AED 48) uses 100 g boneless of lamb shoulder.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 200 g bone-in of lamb shoulder.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) uses 180 g of lamb shoulder.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 280 g bone-in (raw weight) of lamb shoulder.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 1.1 kg bone-in (raw weight) of lamb shoulder.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 1.2 kg bone-in (raw weight) of lamb shoulder.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 250 g bone-in (main) of lamb shoulder.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 200 g (harees) of lamb shoulder.

## Leaving out or adding Lamb shoulder

- Lamb Thareed: choosing “Chicken instead of lamb” removes lamb shoulder.
- Majlis Sharing Platter: choosing “Chicken machboos instead of lamb” removes lamb shoulder.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Chicken machboos” removes lamb shoulder.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Vegetable machboos” removes lamb shoulder.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Lamb machboos” adds lamb shoulder (+AED 10).
