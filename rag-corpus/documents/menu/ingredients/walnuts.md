# Walnuts (جوز)

Walnuts as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Walnuts: description, allergens and diet

Walnuts (Arabic: جوز) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Walnut halves, toasted.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: tree nuts
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Walnuts

Walnuts are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Walnuts

- Al Ghaf House Red uses 120 g of walnuts per batch.

## Dishes that contain Walnuts through a preparation

These dishes contain walnuts because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red

## Questions guests ask about Walnuts

**Q: Do you use walnuts?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses walnuts in Al Ghaf House Red.

**Q: Which dishes contain walnuts?**

A: Camel Kofta.

**Q: Is walnuts an allergen?**

A: Walnuts contain tree nuts.
