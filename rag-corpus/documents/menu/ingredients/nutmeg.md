# Nutmeg (جوزة الطيب)

Nutmeg as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Nutmeg: description, allergens and diet

Nutmeg (Arabic: جوزة الطيب) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Ground nutmeg (a seed spice, not a tree nut).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 26 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Nutmeg

Nutmeg is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Nutmeg

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 2 g of nutmeg per batch.
