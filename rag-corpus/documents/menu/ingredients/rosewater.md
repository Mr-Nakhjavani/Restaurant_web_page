# Rosewater (ماء الورد)

Rosewater as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Rosewater: description, allergens and diet

Rosewater (Arabic: ماء الورد) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Distilled rosewater.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 6 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Rosewater

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 5 ml of rosewater.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 5 ml of rosewater.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi (AED 69) uses 3 ml of rosewater.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses 5 ml of rosewater.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) uses 3 ml of rosewater.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) uses 2 ml of rosewater.

## Sauces and preparations that use Rosewater

- Saffron Cardamom Syrup uses 10 ml of rosewater per batch.

## Questions guests ask about Rosewater

**Q: Do you use rosewater?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses rosewater in Balaleet, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Khanfaroosh, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida), Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) and Saffron Cardamom Syrup.

**Q: Which dishes contain rosewater?**

A: Balaleet, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Muhammar with Fried Safi, Khanfaroosh, Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) and Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table).

**Q: Is rosewater an allergen?**

A: Rosewater is not one of the 14 major allergens.
