# Black pepper (فلفل أسود)

Black pepper as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Black pepper: description, allergens and diet

Black pepper (Arabic: فلفل أسود) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Black peppercorns, ground.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 27 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Black pepper

Black pepper is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Black pepper

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 10 g of black pepper per batch.
- Loomi & Sumac Dressing uses 1 g of black pepper per batch.
