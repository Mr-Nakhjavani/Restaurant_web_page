# Coriander leaves (كزبرة خضراء)

Coriander leaves as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Coriander leaves: description, allergens and diet

Coriander leaves (Arabic: كزبرة خضراء) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh coriander.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: cilantro
- Used in 7 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Coriander leaves

- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 3 g of coriander leaves.
- Nakhi (AED 22) uses 3 g of coriander leaves.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 3 g of coriander leaves.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 2 g of coriander leaves.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 5 g of coriander leaves.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 4 g of coriander leaves.

## Sauces and preparations that use Coriander leaves

- Al Ghaf House Green uses 120 g of coriander leaves per batch.
