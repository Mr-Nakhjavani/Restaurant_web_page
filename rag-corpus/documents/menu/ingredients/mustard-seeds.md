# Mustard seeds (بذور الخردل)

Mustard seeds as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Mustard seeds: description, allergens and diet

Mustard seeds (Arabic: بذور الخردل) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Brown mustard seeds.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: mustard
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Mustard seeds

Mustard seeds are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Mustard seeds

- Mehyawa uses 10 g of mustard seeds per batch.

## Dishes that contain Mustard seeds through a preparation

These dishes contain mustard seeds because one of their preparations does:

- Mehyawa Regag Bites, through Mehyawa

## Questions guests ask about Mustard seeds

**Q: Do you use mustard seeds?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses mustard seeds in Mehyawa.

**Q: Which dishes contain mustard seeds?**

A: Mehyawa Regag Bites.

**Q: Is mustard seeds an allergen?**

A: Mustard seeds contain mustard.
