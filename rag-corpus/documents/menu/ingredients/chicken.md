# Chicken (bone-in) (دجاج)

Chicken (bone-in) as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Chicken (bone-in): description, allergens and diet

Chicken (bone-in) (Arabic: دجاج) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Halal fresh UAE-raised chicken, cut into bone-in quarters.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 6 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Chicken (bone-in)

- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 250 g bone-in of chicken (bone-in).
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (AED 54) uses 180 g (shredded off the bone) of chicken (bone-in).
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 350 g bone-in (half chicken, raw weight) of chicken (bone-in).
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 500 g bone-in (saloona) of chicken (bone-in).
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 1 whole chicken (about 1.2 kg) of chicken (bone-in).
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses 300 g bone-in (raw weight) of chicken (bone-in).

## Leaving out or adding Chicken (bone-in)

- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Lamb machboos” removes chicken (bone-in).
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)” removes chicken (bone-in).
- Lamb Thareed: choosing “Chicken instead of lamb” adds chicken (bone-in) (−AED 6 (cheaper)).
- Majlis Sharing Platter: choosing “Chicken machboos instead of lamb” adds chicken (bone-in) (−AED 40 (cheaper)).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Chicken machboos” adds chicken (bone-in) (no extra charge).
