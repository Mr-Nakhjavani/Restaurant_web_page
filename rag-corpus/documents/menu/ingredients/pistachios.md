# Pistachios (فستق حلبي)

Pistachios as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Pistachios: description, allergens and diet

Pistachios (Arabic: فستق حلبي) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Shelled pistachios.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: tree nuts
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Pistachios

Pistachios are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Pistachios

- Pistachio Cardamom Crumb uses 100 g of pistachios per batch.

## Dishes that contain Pistachios through a preparation

These dishes contain pistachios because one of their preparations does:

- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, through Pistachio Cardamom Crumb

## Questions guests ask about Pistachios

**Q: Do you use pistachios?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses pistachios in Pistachio Cardamom Crumb.

**Q: Which dishes contain pistachios?**

A: Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream.

**Q: Is pistachios an allergen?**

A: Pistachios contain tree nuts.
