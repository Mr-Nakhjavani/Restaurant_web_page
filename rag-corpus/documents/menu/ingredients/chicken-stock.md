# Chicken stock (house-made) (مرق الدجاج)

## Chicken stock (house-made): description, allergens and diet

Chicken stock (house-made) (Arabic: مرق الدجاج) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Stock simmered from chicken bones with onion, loomi, bay leaf and salt.

- Diet class: meat
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 6 dishes and 0 preparations

- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 350 ml of chicken stock (house-made).
