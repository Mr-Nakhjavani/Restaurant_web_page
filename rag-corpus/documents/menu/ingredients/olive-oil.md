# Olive oil (زيت زيتون)

Olive oil as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Olive oil: description, allergens and diet

Olive oil (Arabic: زيت زيتون) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Extra-virgin olive oil.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 13 dishes and 5 preparations

## Dishes that use Olive oil

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 10 ml of olive oil.
- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 15 ml of olive oil.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 15 ml of olive oil.

## Sauces and preparations that use Olive oil

- Al Ghaf House Red uses 60 ml of olive oil per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Green uses 150 ml of olive oil per batch.
- Laban Marinade uses 20 ml of olive oil per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 30 ml of olive oil per batch.
- Loomi & Sumac Dressing uses 120 ml of olive oil per batch.

## Dishes that contain Olive oil through a preparation

These dishes contain olive oil because one of their preparations does:

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Rubyan Mashwi, through Al Ghaf House Green
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Laban Marinade
- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade
- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Friday Family Machboos Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing

## Leaving out or adding Olive oil

- Rubyan Mashwi: choosing “No butter (grilled with olive oil)” adds olive oil (no extra charge).

## Questions guests ask about Olive oil

**Q: Do you use olive oil?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses olive oil in Shorbat Adas, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Bamia Saloona, Al Ghaf House Red, Al Ghaf House Green, Laban Marinade, Saffron Tawook Marinade and Loomi & Sumac Dressing.

**Q: Which dishes contain olive oil?**

A: Shorbat Adas, Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Bamia Saloona, Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Rubyan Mashwi, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Saffron Shish Tawook, Camel Kofta, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Is olive oil an allergen?**

A: Olive oil is not one of the 14 major allergens.
