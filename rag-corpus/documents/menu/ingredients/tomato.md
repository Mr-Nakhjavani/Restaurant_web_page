# Tomato (طماط)

Tomato as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Tomato: description, allergens and diet

Tomato (Arabic: طماط) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh tomatoes.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 25 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Tomato

- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 150 g of tomato.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 150 g of tomato.
- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) uses 80 g of tomato.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 60 g of tomato.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 80 g of tomato.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday) (AED 64) uses 100 g of tomato.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) uses 80 g of tomato.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (AED 54) uses 50 g of tomato.
- Lamb Machboos (AED 72) uses 70 g of tomato.
- Chicken Machboos (AED 58) uses 70 g of tomato.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday) (AED 92) uses 70 g of tomato.
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) (AED 118) uses 1 tomato, charred of tomato.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72) uses 2 slices of tomato.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 70 g of tomato.
- Bamia Saloona (AED 44) uses 120 g of tomato.
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 60 g of tomato.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 450 g of tomato.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 400 g of tomato.
- Whole Lamb Ouzi (AED 1,450) uses 800 g of tomato.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses 100 g of tomato.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 60 g of tomato.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 300 g of tomato.

## Sauces and preparations that use Tomato

- Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce uses 600 g of tomato per batch.

## Dishes that contain Tomato through a preparation

These dishes contain tomato because one of their preparations does:

- Sambousa Trio, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Muhammar with Fried Safi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce
- Hammour Mashwi, through Dakkus Tomato-Chilli Sauce

## Leaving out or adding Tomato

- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Harees” removes tomato.
- Hammour Mashwi: choosing “House salad instead of rice” adds tomato (+AED 4).
