# Carrot (جزر)

Carrot as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Carrot: description, allergens and diet

Carrot (Arabic: جزر) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh carrots.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 6 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Carrot

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 30 g of carrot.
- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 20 g of carrot.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 50 g of carrot.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 50 g of carrot.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 50 g of carrot.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 150 g of carrot.

## Leaving out or adding Carrot

- Sambousa Trio: choosing “All lamb (6 pieces)” removes carrot.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)” adds carrot (−AED 5 (cheaper)).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Vegetable machboos” adds carrot (no extra charge).
