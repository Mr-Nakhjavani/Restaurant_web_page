# Coriander seed (كزبرة ناشفة)

Coriander seed as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Coriander seed: description, allergens and diet

Coriander seed (Arabic: كزبرة ناشفة) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Coriander seed, toasted and ground.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- May contain traces of: celery and mustard
- Used in 27 dishes and 2 preparations

Ground spices are packed by a supplier that also handles mustard and celery.

## Dishes that use Coriander seed

Coriander seed is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Coriander seed

- Bezar Spice Blend uses 25 g of coriander seed per batch.
- Mehyawa uses 4 g of coriander seed per batch.
