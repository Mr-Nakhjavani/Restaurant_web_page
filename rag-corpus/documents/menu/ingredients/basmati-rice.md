# Basmati rice (أرز بسمتي)

Basmati rice as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Basmati rice: description, allergens and diet

Basmati rice (Arabic: أرز بسمتي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Aged long-grain Indian basmati rice, washed and soaked before cooking.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 19 dishes and 0 preparations

## Leaving out or adding Basmati rice

- Hammour Mashwi: basmati rice can be left out on request.
- Saloonat Dajaj: choosing “Regag bread instead of rice” removes basmati rice.
- Hammour Mashwi: choosing “House salad instead of rice” removes basmati rice.
- Saffron Shish Tawook: choosing “Regag bread” removes basmati rice.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Harees” removes basmati rice.
