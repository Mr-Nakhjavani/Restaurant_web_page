# Khalas dates (تمر خلاص)

Khalas dates as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Khalas dates: description, allergens and diet

Khalas dates (Arabic: تمر خلاص) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Whole Khalas dates from Al Ain and Liwa farms (pits removed where stated).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: tamr, dates
- Used in 5 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Khalas dates

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 8 dates of khalas dates.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table) (AED 30) uses 6 dates of khalas dates.
- Date & Cardamom Shake (AED 28) uses 60 g (pitted) of khalas dates.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 3 dates of khalas dates.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 6 dates of khalas dates.
