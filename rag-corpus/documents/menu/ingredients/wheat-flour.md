# Wheat flour (طحين القمح)

Wheat flour as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Wheat flour: description, allergens and diet

Wheat flour (Arabic: طحين القمح) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. White wheat flour used for regag, chebab, luqaimat, sambousa pastry and dough sheets.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
- Used in 10 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Wheat flour

- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 120 g of wheat flour.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 80 g (chebab) of wheat flour.
- Luqaimat (AED 32) uses 90 g of wheat flour.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses 30 g of wheat flour.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (AED 34) uses 40 g, toasted of wheat flour.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 180 g (luqaimat) of wheat flour.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 180 g (luqaimat) of wheat flour.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box (AED 45) uses 27 g (luqaimat) of wheat flour.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 45 g (luqaimat) of wheat flour.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 340 g (chebab and luqaimat) of wheat flour.
