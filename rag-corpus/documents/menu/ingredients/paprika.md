# Paprika (بابريكا)

Paprika as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Paprika: description, allergens and diet

Paprika (Arabic: بابريكا) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Sweet paprika.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Paprika

Paprika is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Paprika

- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 6 g of paprika per batch.

## Dishes that contain Paprika through a preparation

These dishes contain paprika because one of their preparations does:

- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade

## Questions guests ask about Paprika

**Q: Do you use paprika?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses paprika in Saffron Tawook Marinade.

**Q: Which dishes contain paprika?**

A: Saffron Shish Tawook.

**Q: Is paprika an allergen?**

A: Paprika is not one of the 14 major allergens.
