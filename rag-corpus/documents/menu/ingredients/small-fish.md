## Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy): description, allergens and diet

Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) (Arabic: سمك صغير مملح (عوال)) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Small Gulf sardines and anchovies salted and fermented for mehyawa.

- Diet class: pescatarian
- Allergens: fish
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy)

Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy)

- Mehyawa uses 200 g of small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) per batch.

## Dishes that contain Small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) through a preparation

These dishes contain small salted fish (sardine/anchovy) because one of their preparations does:
