# Vegetable stock (house-made) (مرق الخضار)

## Vegetable stock (house-made): description, allergens and diet

Vegetable stock (house-made) (Arabic: مرق الخضار) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Stock simmered from onion, carrot, celery, tomato, loomi and bay leaf. Contains celery.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: celery
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Vegetable stock (house-made)

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 300 ml of vegetable stock (house-made).
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 350 ml of vegetable stock (house-made).
- Pumpkin & Chickpea Saloona (AED 44) uses 200 ml of vegetable stock (house-made).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 220 ml of vegetable stock (house-made).
