## Camel milk: description, allergens and diet

Camel milk (Arabic: حليب النوق) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Pasteurised UAE camel milk. Camel milk is a milk (dairy) allergen.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 1 dish and 0 preparations

- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36) uses 150 ml of camel milk.
