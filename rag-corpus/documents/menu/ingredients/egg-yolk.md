# Egg yolk (صفار البيض)

Egg yolk as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Egg yolk: description, allergens and diet

Egg yolk (Arabic: صفار البيض) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Free-range egg yolks (pasteurised for aioli and ice cream).

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: eggs
- Used in 2 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Egg yolk

- Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream (AED 36) uses 20 g of egg yolk.

## Sauces and preparations that use Egg yolk

- Loomi Aioli uses 40 g (2 yolks, pasteurised) of egg yolk per batch.

## Dishes that contain Egg yolk through a preparation

These dishes contain egg yolk because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Loomi Aioli

## Questions guests ask about Egg yolk

**Q: Do you use egg yolk?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses egg yolk in Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream and Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Which dishes contain egg yolk?**

A: Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Is egg yolk an allergen?**

A: Egg yolk contains eggs.
