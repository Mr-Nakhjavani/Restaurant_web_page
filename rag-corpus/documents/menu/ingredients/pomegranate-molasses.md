# Pomegranate molasses (دبس الرمان)

Pomegranate molasses as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Pomegranate molasses: description, allergens and diet

Pomegranate molasses (Arabic: دبس الرمان) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Reduced pomegranate juice (pomegranate, sugar, lemon juice).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 7 dishes and 2 preparations

## Dishes that use Pomegranate molasses

Pomegranate molasses are not added directly to any dish; they are used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Pomegranate molasses

- Al Ghaf House Red uses 40 ml of pomegranate molasses per batch.
- Loomi & Sumac Dressing uses 20 ml of pomegranate molasses per batch.

## Dishes that contain Pomegranate molasses through a preparation

These dishes contain pomegranate molasses because one of their preparations does:

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red
- Majlis Sharing Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Friday Family Machboos Platter, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Whole Lamb Ouzi, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Office Machboos Lunch Box, through Loomi & Sumac Dressing
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day), through Loomi & Sumac Dressing

## Questions guests ask about Pomegranate molasses

**Q: Do you use pomegranate molasses?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses pomegranate molasses in Al Ghaf House Red and Loomi & Sumac Dressing.

**Q: Which dishes contain pomegranate molasses?**

A: Salatat Al Bait (House Salad), Camel Kofta, Majlis Sharing Platter, Friday Family Machboos Platter, Whole Lamb Ouzi, Office Machboos Lunch Box and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Is pomegranate molasses an allergen?**

A: Pomegranate molasses are not one of the 14 major allergens.
