# Green cardamom (هيل)

Green cardamom as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Green cardamom: description, allergens and diet

Green cardamom (Arabic: هيل) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Green cardamom pods, ground fresh daily.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Also called: hail, elaichi
- Used in 35 dishes and 4 preparations
