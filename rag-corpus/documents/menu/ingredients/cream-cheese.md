## Cream cheese: description, allergens and diet

Cream cheese (Arabic: جبن كريمي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Soft cow's-milk cream cheese.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: milk (dairy)
- Used in 1 dish and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Cream cheese

- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 40 g of cream cheese; it can be left out on request.

## Leaving out or adding Cream cheese

- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: cream cheese can be left out on request.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese: choosing “Dibs only (no cream cheese)” removes cream cheese.
