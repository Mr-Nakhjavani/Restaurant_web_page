# Black tea (شاي أسود)

Black tea as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Black tea: description, allergens and diet

Black tea (Arabic: شاي أسود) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Strong Assam-style loose black tea.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Black tea

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 10 g (karak) of black tea.
- Karak Chai (AED 12) uses 6 g of black tea.
- Sulaimani (AED 10) uses 3 g of black tea.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 6 g of black tea.

## Leaving out or adding Black tea

- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: choosing “Dallah of gahwa instead” removes black tea.
