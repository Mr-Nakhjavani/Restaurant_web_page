# Lemon (ليمون)

Lemon as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Lemon: description, allergens and diet

Lemon (Arabic: ليمون) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh lemons and lemon juice.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 7 dishes and 8 preparations

## Dishes that use Lemon

- Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts (AED 38) uses 15 ml juice of lemon.
- Limon Na'na (AED 22) uses 60 ml juice of lemon.

## Sauces and preparations that use Lemon

- Saffron Yogurt Sauce uses 20 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Toum Garlic Sauce uses 60 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Tahini Lemon Sauce uses 80 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Loomi Aioli uses 15 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Saffron Cardamom Syrup uses 5 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Al Ghaf House Red uses 15 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Laban Marinade uses 20 ml juice of lemon per batch.
- Saffron Tawook Marinade uses 30 ml juice of lemon per batch.

## Dishes that contain Lemon through a preparation

These dishes contain lemon because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Laban Marinade
- Saffron Shish Tawook, through Saffron Tawook Marinade
- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Loomi Aioli
- Khanfaroosh, through Saffron Cardamom Syrup

## Questions guests ask about Lemon

**Q: Do you use lemon?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses lemon in Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Limon Na'na, Saffron Yogurt Sauce, Toum Garlic Sauce, Tahini Lemon Sauce, Loomi Aioli, Saffron Cardamom Syrup, Al Ghaf House Red, Laban Marinade and Saffron Tawook Marinade.

**Q: Which dishes contain lemon?**

A: Hummus with Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts, Limon Na'na, Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), Saffron Shish Tawook, Camel Kofta, Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli and Khanfaroosh.

**Q: Is lemon an allergen?**

A: Lemon is not one of the 14 major allergens.
