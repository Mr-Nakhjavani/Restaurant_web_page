# Sourdough croutons (خبز محمص)

Sourdough croutons as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Sourdough croutons: description, allergens and diet

Sourdough croutons (Arabic: خبز محمص) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Cubes of wheat sourdough toasted with olive oil and salt.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat)
- Used in 2 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Sourdough croutons

- Shorbat Adas (AED 26) uses 15 g of sourdough croutons; it can be left out on request.
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (AED 125) uses 10 g of sourdough croutons.

## Leaving out or adding Sourdough croutons

- Shorbat Adas: sourdough croutons can be left out on request.
- Shorbat Adas: choosing “No croutons” removes sourdough croutons.
