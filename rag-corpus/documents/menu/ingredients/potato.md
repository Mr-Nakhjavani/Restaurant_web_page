# Potato (بطاط)

Potato as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Potato: description, allergens and diet

Potato (Arabic: بطاط) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh potatoes.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 7 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Potato

- Sambousa Trio (AED 34) uses 40 g (vegetable filling) of potato.
- Lamb Thareed (AED 62) uses 80 g of potato.
- Saloonat Dajaj (AED 52) uses 80 g of potato.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (AED 58) uses 60 g of potato.
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli (AED 72) uses 150 g wedges of potato.
- Vegetable Machboos (AED 48) uses 60 g of potato.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 240 g of potato.

## Leaving out or adding Potato

- Sambousa Trio: choosing “All lamb (6 pieces)” removes potato.
- Office Machboos Lunch Box: choosing “Vegetable machboos (no ghee, no hashu)” adds potato (−AED 5 (cheaper)).
- Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person): choosing “Vegetable machboos” adds potato (no extra charge).
