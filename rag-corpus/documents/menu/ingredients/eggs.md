# Eggs (بيض)

Eggs as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Eggs: description, allergens and diet

Eggs (Arabic: بيض) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Free-range hen's eggs.

- Diet class: vegetarian
- Allergens: eggs
- Used in 8 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Eggs

- Balaleet (AED 34) uses 2 eggs (omelette) of eggs; it can be left out on request.
- Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese (AED 36) uses 1 egg of eggs.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese (AED 28) uses 1 egg of eggs; it can be left out on request.
- Bayd w Tamat (AED 30) uses 3 eggs of eggs.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (AED 149) uses 6 eggs across the dishes of eggs.
- Mehyawa Regag Bites (AED 26) uses 1 egg of eggs.
- Khanfaroosh (AED 30) uses 1 egg of eggs.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 1 egg (chebab) of eggs.

## Leaving out or adding Eggs

- Balaleet: eggs can be left out on request.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese: eggs can be left out on request.
- Balaleet: choosing “No omelette” removes eggs.
- Regag with Egg & Cheese: choosing “No egg” removes eggs.

## Questions guests ask about Eggs

**Q: Do you use eggs?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses eggs in Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Khanfaroosh and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Which dishes contain eggs?**

A: Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Khanfaroosh and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day).

**Q: Is eggs an allergen?**

A: Eggs contain eggs.
