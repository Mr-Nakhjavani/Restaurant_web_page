# Soy sauce (صلصة الصويا)

Soy sauce as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Soy sauce: description, allergens and diet

Soy sauce (Arabic: صلصة الصويا) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Naturally brewed soy sauce (soybeans, wheat, salt, water).

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: gluten (wheat) and soy
- Used in 2 dishes and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Soy sauce

Soy sauce is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Soy sauce

- Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a) uses 40 ml of soy sauce per batch.

## Dishes that contain Soy sauce through a preparation

These dishes contain soy sauce because one of their preparations does:

- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops), through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)
- Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli, through Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a)

## Questions guests ask about Soy sauce

**Q: Do you use soy sauce?**

A: Yes. Bayt Al Ghaf uses soy sauce in Sauce No. 7 (Saba'a).

**Q: Which dishes contain soy sauce?**

A: Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops) and Camel Burger with Loomi Aioli.

**Q: Is soy sauce an allergen?**

A: Soy sauce contains gluten (wheat) and soy.
