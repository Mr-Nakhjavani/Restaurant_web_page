# Pomegranate seeds (حب الرمان)

Pomegranate seeds as used at Bayt Al Ghaf: what this ingredient is, its allergens and diet class, and every dish and preparation that uses it, with quantities.

## Pomegranate seeds: description, allergens and diet

Pomegranate seeds (Arabic: حب الرمان) are used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Fresh pomegranate arils.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 4 dishes and 0 preparations

## Dishes that use Pomegranate seeds

- Salatat Al Bait (House Salad) (AED 30) uses 20 g of pomegranate seeds.
- Majlis Sharing Platter (AED 329) uses 40 g of pomegranate seeds.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter (AED 349) uses 40 g of pomegranate seeds.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (AED 355) uses 40 g of pomegranate seeds.

## Leaving out or adding Pomegranate seeds

- Hammour Mashwi: choosing “House salad instead of rice” adds pomegranate seeds (+AED 4).
