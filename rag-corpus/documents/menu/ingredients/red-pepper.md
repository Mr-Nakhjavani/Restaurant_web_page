## Roasted red pepper: description, allergens and diet

Roasted red pepper (Arabic: فلفل أحمر مشوي) is used in the Bayt Al Ghaf kitchen. Red bell peppers roasted over flame and peeled.

- Diet class: vegan
- Allergens: none of the 14 major allergens
- Used in 1 dish and 1 preparation

## Dishes that use Roasted red pepper

Roasted red pepper is not added directly to any dish; it is used inside preparations (see below).

## Sauces and preparations that use Roasted red pepper

- Al Ghaf House Red uses 400 g of roasted red pepper per batch.

## Dishes that contain Roasted red pepper through a preparation

These dishes contain roasted red pepper because one of their preparations does:

- Camel Kofta, through Al Ghaf House Red
