# Changing or cancelling a Bayt Al Ghaf order

Until when a Bayt Al Ghaf order can be changed or cancelled, what can change, and what happens to large changes and late orders.

## Changing a Bayt Al Ghaf order

Items, time, delivery address and instructions can be changed until 30 minutes before the pickup or delivery time, or until the kitchen starts preparing the order, whichever is earlier. Every change is checked again for availability, kitchen capacity and price.

## Cancelling a Bayt Al Ghaf order

Cancelling is free until the kitchen starts preparing the order. After that, the team decides case by case.

## Turning a Bayt Al Ghaf order into a large order

Changing an order into a large one (more than 20 kitchen portions — for example from 6 to 50 meals) makes it a catering order, which needs catering notice. It cannot keep the original time.

## When Bayt Al Ghaf is running late

If Bayt Al Ghaf is running late, the team tells the customer the new time. Customers may cancel without charge if the order is more than 20 minutes late.

## Finding or changing your Bayt Al Ghaf order

To check, change or cancel an order, give the order number and the phone number used to order. After 5 wrong attempts, the order is locked for 15 minutes to protect the customer.
