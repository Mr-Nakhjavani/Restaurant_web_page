# Collecting a pickup order from Bayt Al Ghaf

Where and how to collect a Bayt Al Ghaf takeaway order, how long orders are held, who can collect, and how to pay.

## How to collect a Bayt Al Ghaf pickup order

- Come to the takeaway counter at Heritage Quarter Gate 3 on Al Seef Street. There is a 10-minute drop-off bay outside the counter.
- Give the order number (for example ORD-604215) and the name on the order.
- Pay by cash (AED), card, Apple Pay or Google Pay at the counter.

## Someone else collecting a Bayt Al Ghaf order

Someone else may collect the order if their name was given with the order, or if they quote the order number and the phone number used to order.

## How long Bayt Al Ghaf holds a pickup order

Pickup orders are held for 60 minutes after the pickup time. Hot food is kept warm for about 30 minutes. After 60 minutes an uncollected order is marked “not collected” and is not kept, for food-safety reasons. Customers running late should call +971 4 555 0142 so the order can be re-timed if the kitchen has not started it.
