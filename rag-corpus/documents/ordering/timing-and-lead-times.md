# Bayt Al Ghaf order timing and lead times

How early Bayt Al Ghaf can have an order ready: lead-time rules, 15-minute slots, kitchen capacity, ready times on each day, delivery timing and the preparation time of every dish.

## Bayt Al Ghaf lead-time rule

The earliest an order can be ready is the longest preparation time in the order plus 10 minutes for packing, and never less than 20 minutes after ordering. For example, an order with Hammour Mashwi (20 minutes) needs at least 30 minutes; an order of only Shorbat Adas (8 minutes) needs at least 20 minutes.

## Bayt Al Ghaf pickup slots

Pickup and delivery times at Bayt Al Ghaf are booked in 15-minute slots (for example 13:00, 13:15, 13:30). The kitchen accepts up to 30 kitchen portions and 8 orders per slot. When a slot is full, the team offers the nearest free times instead; Bayt Al Ghaf never accepts a time the kitchen cannot meet.

## Bayt Al Ghaf delivery timing

A delivery time is the time the food arrives. The kitchen starts the order early enough to cover the drive: 15 minutes to Zone A — Old Dubai, 25 minutes to Zone B — Creek & Deira and 35 minutes to Zone C — Downtown & Garhoud. The earliest delivery is therefore the lead time plus the drive time; for a dish that needs 25 minutes, delivery to Zone B is at the earliest 50 minutes after ordering. Arrival is estimated within a 15-minute window.

## Busy times at Bayt Al Ghaf

Friday lunch, Friday evenings, Saturday lunch and Ramadan iftar are the busiest times. For those, ordering or booking a day ahead is recommended.
