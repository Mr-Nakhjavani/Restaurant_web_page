# How ordering works at Bayt Al Ghaf

Ordering from Bayt Al Ghaf: pickup, delivery and dine-in pre-orders, what details are needed, how far ahead, ASAP orders, order numbers and when an order counts as confirmed.

## Ways to order from Bayt Al Ghaf

- Pickup (takeaway): order ahead and collect at the takeaway counter at Heritage Quarter Gate 3, Al Seef Street, at the confirmed time.
- Delivery: Bayt Al Ghaf's own drivers deliver within central Dubai, with arrival between 12:00 (noon) to 22:30 (10:30 pm).
- Dine-in pre-order: order before arriving so the food is ready when you sit down. It needs a confirmed table reservation made with the same phone number.
- Guests can always order at the table without a pre-order, and can order takeaway without a reservation.
- Orders can be placed by phone on +971 4 555 0142 or on the Bayt Al Ghaf website.

## Details Bayt Al Ghaf needs for an order

- The customer's name and a valid phone number (UAE local format such as 050 123 4567, or international with the country code). Delivery orders need a UAE mobile number so the driver can call.
- The dishes, quantities and any options (for example spice level or “no raisins”).
- A pickup time, a delivery time, or ASAP (as soon as possible).
- For delivery only: the area, the building or villa, the street, and the apartment or floor if there is one. A landmark, Makani number and instructions help but are optional. No address is needed for pickup.
- Optional: an e-mail address, the name of someone else who will collect, and special instructions (up to 300 characters).

## How far ahead you can order from Bayt Al Ghaf

Regular orders can be scheduled for any 15-minute slot from the earliest possible time up to 7 days ahead. Orders further ahead cannot be accepted yet — order nearer the date, or use catering for large orders (up to 90 days ahead).

## ASAP orders at Bayt Al Ghaf

An ASAP order is scheduled for the earliest 15-minute slot at which every dish in the order can be ready and is available. If one dish is served later (for example a lunch dish ordered at breakfast time), the whole order is timed for when that dish is available.

## When a Bayt Al Ghaf order is confirmed

An order is confirmed only when Bayt Al Ghaf has accepted it, and the customer receives an order number such as ORD-482913 (catering orders start with CAT-).

Most orders are confirmed straight away. These are received as “awaiting confirmation” and confirmed by the team by phone: orders above AED 1,500; orders from phone numbers with 2 or more uncollected orders in the last 90 days; and every catering order.

## Bayt Al Ghaf order sizes

- Up to 10 of any one dish per regular order (some dishes have a lower limit).
- Up to 20 kitchen portions per regular order (drinks do not count). Larger orders are catering orders, which need notice.
- Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread: up to 6 per order.
- Harees: up to 6 per order.
- Saloonat Samak (Monday): up to 8 per order.
- Margooga Laham (Tuesday): up to 8 per order.
- Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday): up to 8 per order.
- Muhammar with Fried Safi: up to 8 per order.
- Camel Machboos (Thursday): up to 8 per order.
- Hammour Mashwi: up to 8 per order.
- Rubyan Mashwi: up to 8 per order.
- Rish Ghanam (Lamb Chops): up to 8 per order.
- Camel Kofta: up to 8 per order.
- Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida): up to 8 per order.
- Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table): up to 6 per order.
- Majlis Sharing Platter: up to 4 per order.
- Friday Family Machboos Platter: up to 3 per order.
- Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day): up to 4 per order.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes with a daily limit

These dishes are made in limited numbers each day and can sell out: Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread (25 per day), Harees (30 per day), Saloonat Samak (Monday) (20 per day), Margooga Laham (Tuesday) (20 per day), Madrooba Dajaj (Wednesday) (20 per day), Muhammar with Fried Safi (25 per day), Camel Machboos (Thursday) (20 per day), Hammour Mashwi (25 per day), Aseedat Bobar (Winter Pumpkin Asida) (30 per day), Friday Family Machboos Platter (12 per day), Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person) (120 per day) and Eid Al Etihad Feast (National Day) (20 per day).

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that cannot be delivered

These dishes are available for dine-in or pickup but are not delivered: Balaleet, Chebab with Date Syrup & Cream Cheese, Regag with Egg & Cheese, Bayd w Tamat, Weekend Fuala Breakfast Spread, Mehyawa Regag Bites, Date & Camel Milk Ice Cream, Gahwa Emirati (Dallah for the Table), Friday Family Machboos Platter and Ramadan Iftar Set Menu (per person). Breakfast is never delivered.
