# Dine-in pre-orders at Bayt Al Ghaf

How to pre-order food at Bayt Al Ghaf so it is ready when you arrive for your reservation, including the Ramadan Iftar Set Menu.

## What a Bayt Al Ghaf dine-in pre-order is

A dine-in pre-order is food ordered before arriving, so it is ready to serve soon after the guests sit down. It needs a confirmed table reservation made with the same phone number as the order. The food is served between the reservation time and one hour after it, and is paid at the table after the meal.

## Bayt Al Ghaf dishes that need a pre-order or reservation

The Ramadan Iftar Set Menu and the Friday Family Machboos Platter can be ordered for pickup or as a dine-in pre-order, but not for delivery.

## Cancelling a reservation with a Bayt Al Ghaf pre-order

Cancelling a reservation also cancels any linked dine-in pre-order that the kitchen has not started.
